## Summary
<!-- chronolog:begin region="summary" -->
Workspace admins and owners on Business and Enterprise plans can now see adoption by department in Insights when your workspace uses SSO without SCIM. To set it up, map the `department` attribute in your SAML or OIDC app. Lovable then reads it at each sign-in, so the breakdown fills as your members return.
