# Introducing Gamma 5

**Gamma** · 2026-10-26 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Launch · [Official source](https://ideas.gamma.app/changelog/whats-new-in-gamma-septemberoctober-2026) · from [[Releases/Gamma/gamma-2026-10-26-6adfa60dfb9f73d63d1f52fd-593d6949|What’s new in Gamma  /  September–October 2026]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We rebuilt how Gamma thinks, designs, and edits. Because your best ideas deserve better. Give Gamma 5 whatever you have: a sentence, a doc, a PDF, an image, an old deck. The agent will then brainstorm, research, and design a polished presentation within minutes. Bring in PDF and PPT designs for ongoing use. Edit however you'd like, then export - your changes stay intact. Skip the copy-and-paste. Connect Slack, Notion, Granola, and 20+ other tools to bring in context directly. Gamma builds the story around your data. Describe the look you want, or share visual inspiration. Gamma tailors a style to your goal, so the result is specific to your content, not generic.
