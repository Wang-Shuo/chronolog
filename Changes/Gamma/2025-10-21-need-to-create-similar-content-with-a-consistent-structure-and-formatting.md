# Need to create similar content with a consistent structure and formatting?

**Gamma** · 2025-10-21 · [[Themes/reliability-performance|Reliability and Performance]] · Launch · [Official source](https://ideas.gamma.app/changelog/remix-and-workspace-templates) · from [[Releases/Gamma/gamma-2025-10-21-68f7e7f13743257599cc5416-e745f2cd|Remix and workspace templates]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Whether it's sales decks, weekly reports, or client proposals, starting from scratch means unpredictable layouts. We've just launched two features that work together to help you create consistent outputs, faster:
>
> **🔀 Remix:** use any gamma as a starting point for new content
>
> Adapt existing content for new audiences or use cases
>
> Fill templates with fresh 📊 data, 📄 notes, or 💬 instructions
>
> Maintain consistent formatting while updating content
>
> Paste in CSV data 🔢, or drag in images 🌅
>
> **🗃️ Workspace templates:** organized library of go-to gammas your team can reuse
>
> Turn any gamma into a reusable template for your team
>
> Keep styling consistent across recurring content
>
> Templates appear on the create screen for quick access
>
> Perfect for sales decks, meeting notes, reports, and recurring content
>
> Remix and workspace templates are currently in beta. Remix is available across all plans and uses AI credits. To add a gamma to your workspace templates, you'll need the **Pro plan**.
>
> Source: https://ideas.gamma.app/changelog/remix-and-workspace-templates
