# 🎩 Now you see it, now you don't – hide cards in Gamma!

**Gamma** · 2025-03-28 · [[Themes/presentation-creation|Presentation Creation]] · [Official source](https://ideas.gamma.app/changelog/now-you-see-it-now-you-dont-hide-cards-in-gamma) · from [[Releases/Gamma/gamma-2025-03-28-67e71ed42f0403bf14bc2484-92766059|🎩 Now you see it, now you don't – hide cards in Gamma!]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now hide cards in your Gamma presentations and sites! Need to tuck something away for later? Want to fine-tune your flow without deleting content? Just right-click on the card in the filmstrip and—poof!—it’s out of sight (but not gone).
>
> 💡 Hidden cards stay in your presentation or site but won’t appear while presenting, or in the published website. Bring them back anytime with a simple click!
>
> Give it a try and add a little magic to your storytelling. ✨🚀
>
