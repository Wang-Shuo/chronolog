# 3 Apps and Integrations changes in Qoder 0.4.7

**Qoder** · 2026-10-14 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Digest · [Official source](https://changelog.qoder.com/app#0.4.7) · from [[Releases/Qoder/qoder-2026-10-14-0-4-7-c98dae8c|Qoder 0.4.7]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> **Phone use (Mac with Apple silicon)**: Added iPhone control. Enable the required plugin and complete device setup to let an Agent operate your connected iPhone and view its screen in the task sidebar.

> **WorkBuddy data import**: Import task records, scheduled tasks, memory, and supported Skills, MCP connectors, and plugins from WorkBuddy on your computer into Qoder to continue your existing work.

> **Remote Control**: Sync SSH workspaces and sessions to continue existing remote tasks from your phone or create new tasks in supported remote workspaces.

Source: https://changelog.qoder.com/app#0.4.7
