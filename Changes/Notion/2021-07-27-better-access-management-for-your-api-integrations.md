# Better access management for your API integrations

**Notion** · 2021-07-27 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2021-07-27) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2021-07-27-https-www-notion-com-releases-2021-07-27-7f038e82|Notion for non-profits ❤️]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Now you have even more control over what content is shared with an integration. And the process of adding an integration to your Notion workspace is much smoother now!
>
> You can pick specific pages to grant access to with the new search field — you're no longer limited to the top-level pages in your workspace
>
> The page picker will remember which pages you already granted access to, so you don't have to re-select them each time you want to adjust the integration's permissions
>
> Source: https://www.notion.com/releases/2021-07-27
