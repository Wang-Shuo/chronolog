# Organize your shred pages 🧹

**Notion** · 2021-03-10 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2021-03-10) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2021-03-10-https-www-notion-com-releases-2021-03-10-8743b25a|Organize your shared pages 🧹]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Notion's left-hand sidebar is the front door to your digital office, storing all your team's wiki pages, notes & tasks. Now it's much easier to keep your sidebar organized! You'll have easy access to the pages you need, without the added clutter of old one-off docs.
>
> As a quick reminder:
>
> Pages in the **WORKSPACE** section are visible to your whole team
>
> Pages in the **SHARED** section are only visible to specific people on your team
>
> Pages in the PRIVATE section are only visible to you
>
> In the new-and-improved SHARED section, you decide which pages stay in your sidebar. Everything else can be searched and sorted, pinned and unpinned in the new View all popover menu 👇
>
> Source: https://www.notion.com/releases/2021-03-10
