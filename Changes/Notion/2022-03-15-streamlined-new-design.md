> Discover and switch views with tabs . Views let you and your team slice and dice data however you need. And now they’re just one click away — access different views with tabs at the top of each database.
>
> Customize database views from a single menu . Before, view options were spread across multiple menus and dropdowns. Now they’re all accessible from one place, where you can change the view layout, visible properties, filters & sorts, grouping and more.
>
> Hide the database title . Whether you’re creating a personal dashboard with a minimalist look, or trying to clean up your team’s homepage, now you have the option to hide the title of the source database in a linked view. A top feature request!
