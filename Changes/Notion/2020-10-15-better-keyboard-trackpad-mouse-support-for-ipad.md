**Notion** · 2020-10-15 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2020-10-15) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2020-10-15-https-www-notion-com-releases-2020-10-15-65e9eb9c|October 15, 2020]]

> Using Notion on iPad just got a lot easier! If you use an external keyboard, trackpad or mouse with your iPad, you can now:

> Click & drag to rearrange content blocks, or selections of multiple content blocks
