# Task routing agents automatically manage incoming requests

**Notion** · 2026-02-24 · [[Themes/agent-mode|Agent Mode]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-02-24) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2026-02-24-https-www-notion-com-releases-2026-02-24-44f3629d|Notion 3.3: Custom Agents]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Instead of triaging across multiple systems, Custom Agents can capture incoming requests, turn them into tasks, and route them to the right owner, so nothing slips and the team stays focused.
>
> Remote’s IT Ops Manager, James Lawley, saved the team 20 hours per week. “We replaced an overgrown system with Custom Agents that triage with >95% accuracy and resolve 25%+ of tickets autonomously, all while keeping Slack and Notion in sync.”
>
> Watch Simon’s demo of our “all product feedback” agent (it handles bugs + tickets end-to-end). Or meet Scruff , the agent that saved our security team 6+ hours a week.
>
> Source: https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-02-24
