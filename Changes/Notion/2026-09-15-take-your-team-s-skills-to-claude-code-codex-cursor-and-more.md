<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Take your skills with you, no matter what agent you use. Create and update the skill in Notion, then download it to Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini, or Grok .
>
> Notion writes the skill as a SKILL.md file, along with approved supporting files. When the skill changes in Notion, it gets marked with a badge so you know to grab the latest version. Your team maintains the instructions in one place and your local agents can follow the same process. Learn more →
