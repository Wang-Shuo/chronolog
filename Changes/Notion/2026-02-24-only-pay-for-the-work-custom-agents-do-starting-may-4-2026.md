# Only pay for the work Custom Agents do (starting May 4, 2026)

**Notion** · 2026-02-24 · [[Themes/agent-mode|Agent Mode]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-02-24) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2026-02-24-https-www-notion-com-releases-2026-02-24-44f3629d|Notion 3.3: Custom Agents]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Custom Agents work at a scale that wasn’t possible before—an unlimited bench of teammates, running 24/7. Notion credits give teams the flexibility to match that to their needs.
>
> Custom Agents are free to try through May 3, 2026, so your team has time to explore and see what works for you. Starting May 4, 2026, they’ll use Notion credits, available as an add-on for Business and Enterprise plans. Admins can buy as many or as few as your team needs. Learn more about AI credits →
>
> Source: https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-02-24
