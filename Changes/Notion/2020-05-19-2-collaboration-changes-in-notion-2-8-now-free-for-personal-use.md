# 2 Collaboration changes in Notion 2.8, now free for personal use 🤘

**Notion** · 2020-05-19 · [[Themes/collaboration|Collaboration]] · Digest · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2020-05-19) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2020-05-19-https-www-notion-com-releases-2020-05-19-52004e7d|Notion 2.8, now free for personal use 🤘]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Enable edits on public pages 🔗 Before, only guests or members in your workspace could edit a page with you. Now you can give editing access to anyone by sending them the link. People will need a Notion account to make edits, but we simplified the sign-up process for anyone coming in via your link. Use this for crowdsourced projects, classroom collaboration, or simply sharing a grocery list with a loved one. Here's a step-by-step guide →

> Redesigned Share menu 🌏 The Share menu has a new look! We refreshed the design and the wording to make it a bit easier to share your pages however you like: with specific people, groups of people, your whole team, or with everyone on the web. Learn how sharing works here →
