# Embed Sketch designs, Excalidraw drawings & Framer prototypes

**Notion** · 2021-06-29 · [[Themes/meeting-notes|Meeting Notes]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2021-06-29) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2021-06-29-https-www-notion-com-releases-2021-06-29-e080ed59|Notion 2.11, now with Synced Blocks 🔄]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Now there are even more tools that you can embed directly in your Notion docs, meeting notes & tasks — especially useful to share with folks on other teams who may not have access to those services. Just use these slash commands:
>
> **/sketch** : Embed Sketch documents, artboards and prototypes in Notion to share your work with your team, as seen below
>
> **/excalidraw** : Easily sketch diagrams that have a hand-drawn feel to them, then embed in any Notion page
>
> **/framer** : New and improved 💫 Embed beautiful prototypes that are resizeable and fully interactive, right inside Notion
>
> Source: https://www.notion.com/releases/2021-06-29
