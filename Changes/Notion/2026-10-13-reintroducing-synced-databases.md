# Reintroducing synced databases

**Notion** · 2026-10-13 · [[Themes/meeting-notes|Meeting Notes]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-10-13) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2026-10-13-https-www-notion-com-releases-2026-10-13-a75e0ec7|Notion 3.8: Context for your agents]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We rebuilt our synced databases, starting with GitHub, Jira, and Salesforce, so your teams and agents always work from the latest data. They’re faster, more reliable, and show a live health status for each sync. They work like any other Notion database, so you can add your own properties, create views, and trigger automations. At Notion, we build customer hubs by syncing Salesforce accounts and linking to meeting notes and feature requests, so every team works from the same current view of each customer. Learn more →
