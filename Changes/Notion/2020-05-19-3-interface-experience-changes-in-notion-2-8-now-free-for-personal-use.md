# 3 Interface Experience changes in Notion 2.8, now free for personal use 🤘

**Notion** · 2020-05-19 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Digest · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2020-05-19) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2020-05-19-https-www-notion-com-releases-2020-05-19-52004e7d|Notion 2.8, now free for personal use 🤘]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Easy invite links for teams 🎟 For Team and Enterprise users, you can invite more teammates to join your workspace with a secret link (before, you had to enter each email address manually). Just go to the Members section of Settings & Members in your sidebar: Learn more about adding teammates with this guide →

> New templates for writing, planning, and organizing 🧭 Templates are a great way to see what's possible in Notion, or to quickly add some structure to your page. Just click Templates in your left-hand sidebar, add to your workspace, and customize to your needs. In honor of our new free Personal Plan, we created a bunch of new templates for life management. Find them in the Templates window under Personal . Learn how to get the most out of templates with this 2-minute video →

> The mobile apps for iPad and Android tablets now include ← and → buttons at the top left when in landscape orientation, for easier navigation

Source: https://www.notion.com/releases/2020-05-19
