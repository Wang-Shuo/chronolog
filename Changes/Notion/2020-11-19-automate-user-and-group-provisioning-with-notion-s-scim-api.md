**Notion** · 2020-11-19 · [[Themes/automation-scheduling|Automation and Scheduling]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2020-11-19) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2020-11-19-https-www-notion-com-releases-2020-11-19-b4b1ddbb|SCIM + Bug Bash 🐛]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> SCIM (System for Cross-domain Identity Management) enables organizations to create, manage and remove members and groups in Notion through an identity provider (like Okta or Rippling) or your own app.
>
> SCIM & SAML SSO are included in our Enterprise Plan. Contact our Enterprise Team to learn more →
>
> We currently support Okta , Rippling , and custom SCIM applications. If you need help configuring SCIM, please let us know !
>
> Notion's SCIM implementation supports adding and removing members and groups, updating members' profile information, adding and removing members from groups, and more. Learn how to provision users and groups with SCIM →
>
> IBM, Nike, Pixar and others use Notion's Enterprise Plan. See what's included →
