# Add unlimited content for free 💸 Previously, personal users had to pay $5/month…

**Notion** · 2020-05-19 · [[Themes/pricing-model|Pricing Model]] · Pricing · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2020-05-19) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2020-05-19-https-www-notion-com-releases-2020-05-19-52004e7d|Notion 2.8, now free for personal use 🤘]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Add unlimited content for free 💸 Previously, personal users had to pay $5/month to add as much content to Notion as they wanted. But hitting a storage limit is the last thing you should have to worry about right now. So we got rid of it. Now you can use Notion on your own for free, indefinitely. See FAQs for more on this → See how Notion can help you think, write, and get organized at notion.com/personal →
