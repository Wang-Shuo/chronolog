# Q&A agents answer repeat questions

**Notion** · 2026-02-24 · [[Themes/agent-mode|Agent Mode]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-02-24) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2026-02-24-https-www-notion-com-releases-2026-02-24-44f3629d|Notion 3.3: Custom Agents]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Instead of answering the same questions in Slack manually, over and over again, you can now give your Custom Agent access to the right sources (Notion, Slack, Mail, Calendar, MCP integrations) so agents can answer those questions autonomously. We’ve seen tons of examples with great names like Onboarding Eddie, IT Hero, and Sales Detective.
>
> Ben Levick leads Ops and Internal AI at Ramp and has built lots of Custom Agents. “Our agents answer dozens of nuanced product and enablement questions every day with a high success rate.
