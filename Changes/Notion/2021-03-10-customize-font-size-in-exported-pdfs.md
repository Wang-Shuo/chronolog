# Customize font size in exported PDFs 🔍

**Notion** · 2021-03-10 · [[Themes/file-data|Files and Data]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2021-03-10) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2021-03-10-https-www-notion-com-releases-2021-03-10-8743b25a|Organize your shared pages 🧹]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> When exporting any Notion page as a PDF, there's a new option that helps you increase or decrease the text size as needed. To do this:
>
> Click the ••• button at the top right of a page, and select Export
>
> Enter a number between 11% - 199% depending on how small or large you want the text to be
>
> Source: https://www.notion.com/releases/2021-03-10
