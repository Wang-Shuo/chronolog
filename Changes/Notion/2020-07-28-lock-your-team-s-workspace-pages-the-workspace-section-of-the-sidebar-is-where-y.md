# Lock your team's Workspace pages 🔒 The Workspace section of the sidebar is where…

**Notion** · 2020-07-28 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2020-07-28) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2020-07-28-https-www-notion-com-releases-2020-07-28-3450c910|Sign in with Apple 🍎]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Lock your team's Workspace pages 🔒 The Workspace section of the sidebar is where your team's most important pages live. Admins in Enterprise Plan workspaces can now add an extra layer of security to prevent any accidental changes to these pages. When you turn on this option, workspace members won't be able to create, move, re-order, or delete top-level pages in the Workspace section. This is particularly helpful for larger teams! To enable it, go to the Security & SAML section of Settings & Members on desktop. Learn more about workspace settings & security →
>
> Source: https://www.notion.com/releases/2020-07-28
