# Column permissions in databases

**Notion** · 2026-10-13 · [[Themes/agent-mode|Agent Mode]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-10-13) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2026-10-13-https-www-notion-com-releases-2026-10-13-a75e0ec7|Notion 3.8: Context for your agents]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Notion databases give teams and agents a shared, up-to-date source of context. But both teams and agents need the right level of access. Column permissions were a top requested feature for years (we spent time getting this one right). They let database owners control which properties each person or agent can view or edit. Recruiting teams can collaborate on candidates without exposing compensation. Support agents can use account context without seeing restricted customer data. Watch the trailer from one of it’s creators!
>
