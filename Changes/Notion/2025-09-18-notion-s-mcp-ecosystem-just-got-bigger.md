# Notion’s MCP ecosystem just got bigger

**Notion** · 2025-09-18 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2025-09-18) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2025-09-18-https-www-notion-com-releases-2025-09-18-c029ddc5|Notion 3.0: Agents]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Another big upgrade today: We’ve expanded our list of first-party MCP integrations with lots of new partners like Lovable, Perplexity, Mistral, and HubSpot. With Notion MCP, your tools can read context from your Notion workspace (and your connected apps) and write back to Notion.
>
> Here’s what that looks like in practice: If you’re coding in Cursor, it can pull the latest tech specs from Notion, update the codebase, then mark the task as done in Notion. The Notion ecosystem is more open than it’s ever been, and we’re just getting started.
>
> Source: https://www.notion.com/releases/2025-09-18
