# 9 other changes in Working from home 🏡

**Notion** · 2020-03-18 · Other changes · Digest · [Official source](https://www.notion.com/releases/2020-03-18) · from [[Releases/Notion/notion-2020-03-18-https-www-notion-com-releases-2020-03-18-4ce78918|Working from home 🏡]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Your most relevant notifications will arrive in the Inbox section. Click the X button to archive an update, or Archive All to hit inbox zero.

> The Following section now includes an Unfollow this page button, so you can curate these notifications to be more relevant.

> The new Archived section is your home for notifications that've already been acted on. This lets you minimize your Inbox so that it only includes tasks that still need your attention. Use the Unarchive button to put something back on your radar. Learn more about updates & notifications here >>

> Ideation & brainstorming

> UX research Just type /miro on any Notion page to get started.

> Version control

> Designer <> Developer handoff Just type /abstract on any Notion page to get started.

> The red notification badge now counts to 99 instead of 9.

> Public Notion pages containing suspicious content are now automatically blacklisted and display a warning.
