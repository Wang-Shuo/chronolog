**ChatGPT** · 2026-09-21 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · [Official source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes#credit-scores-in-finances) · from [[Releases/ChatGPT/chatgpt-2026-09-21-credit-scores-in-finances-a66dbae8|Credit scores in Finances]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now track your credit score in ChatGPT. Securely connect your Experian® credit report and VantageScore® 3.0 credit score to get personalized insights into what’s affecting your score and how it relates to your finances and goals.
>
> Your score and report update monthly, and credit monitoring can alert you to important changes, such as a new inquiry, account, or address. Your Experian connection is separate from your bank accounts, so you can use the credit-score feature without connecting accounts through Plaid.
>
> This feature is rolling out in Finances to Plus and Pro users in the U.S. on web, iOS, and Android. Some eligible users may not see it right away.
>
