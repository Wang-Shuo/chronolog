# We’re updating the model picker to make it easier to choose the balance of speed…

**ChatGPT** · 2026-06-10 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · [Official source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes#simplified-controls-in-the-model-picker) · from [[Releases/ChatGPT/chatgpt-2026-06-10-simplified-controls-in-the-model-picker-32a1fdba|Simplified controls in the model picker]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’re updating the model picker to make it easier to choose the balance of speed and reasoning effort that works best for your task.The model picker now has the following simplified options:
>
> On iOS and Android, you can find the picker at the top of the conversation. On web, it appears directly in the message composer.Users have the ability to decide whether Instant auto-switches to Medium for higher reasoning when required. You can turn this on or off in General > Settings.
>
> What changed from the previous model picker: Thinking Standard is now Medium, Thinking Extended is now High, and Thinking Heavy is now Extra High. Pro Standard and Pro Extended remain available under Pro. Thinking Light is no longer available.
>
> These model picker updates are rolling out to Plus and Pro users on web, iOS, and Android globally today.
>
> Source: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes#simplified-controls-in-the-model-picker
