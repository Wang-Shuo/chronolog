**ChatGPT** · 2026-10-15 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · [Official source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes#gpt-live-11-and-new-voices-in-chatgpt) · from [[Releases/ChatGPT/chatgpt-2026-10-15-gpt-live-1-1-and-new-voices-in-chatgpt-3e3f7f0c|GPT-Live-1.1 and new voices in ChatGPT]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’re rolling out GPT-Live-1.1 and GPT-Live-1.1 Quip, a mini version of the model, in ChatGPT Voice, with more natural accents across languages. Live also uses a wider range of short phrases to let you know when it’s searching.
>
> New Live voices include options for French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, and Mexican Spanish, plus Bubbie in English. The voices you see may vary by region and account.
>
> To choose a voice, open Settings > Voice, then select Voice.
>
