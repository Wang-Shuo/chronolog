**ChatGPT** · 2026-10-15 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · [Official source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes#applications-for-the-rosalind-academic-plan) · from [[Releases/ChatGPT/chatgpt-2026-10-15-applications-for-the-rosalind-academic-plan-b85717ef|Applications for the Rosalind Academic Plan]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Applications are now open to labs at U.S. universities. If you’re a principal investigator or lab lead, apply on behalf of your lab. Applying doesn’t give your lab immediate access. After approval, follow the instructions to set up your workspace and enable access for approved members. Labs covered by an OpenAI Enterprise agreement should use their university’s access process. For eligibility and setup details, see: Rosalind Academic Plan.
