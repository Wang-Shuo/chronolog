**ElevenLabs** · 2026-10-26 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Digest · [Official source](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/10/26) · from [[Releases/ElevenLabs/elevenlabs-2026-10-26-2026-10-26-ca7c552d|ElevenLabs updates — 2026-10-26]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> **Channel integrations out of alpha**: The [Slack](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/customization/integrations/slack), Telegram, Custom Channel and Intercom integrations are no longer in alpha. Freshdesk remains in alpha.

> **Telephony**: [SIP trunk](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/phone-numbers/sip-trunking) calls forward the `User-To-User` (UUI) header to the conversation initiation webhook. API keys with an IP allowlist now authenticate on the Genesys, Avaya, AudioCodes and Amazon Connect connector WebSockets. Batch call recipients whose call cannot be placed are marked `failed` instead of staying `dispatched`.

Source: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/10/26
