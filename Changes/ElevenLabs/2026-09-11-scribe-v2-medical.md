**ElevenLabs** · 2026-09-11 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/9/11) · from [[Releases/ElevenLabs/elevenlabs-2026-09-11-2026-09-11-fe1ab3d8|ElevenLabs updates — 2026-09-11]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> [Scribe v2 Medical](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/models#scribe-v2-medical) is now generally available. It is a batch speech recognition model specialized for medical and clinical audio, billed at the same rate as Scribe v2.
>
> Pass `scribe_v2_medical` as `model_id` on [Create transcript](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/speech-to-text/convert). Keyterm prompting, entity detection, speaker diarization, and no-verbatim mode work the same way as Scribe v2.
>
