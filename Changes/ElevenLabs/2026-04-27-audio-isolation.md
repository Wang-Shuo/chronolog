<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **History endpoints**: Two new endpoints expose your audio isolation history.
>
> [List audio isolation history](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/audio-isolation/list) (`GET /v1/audio-isolation/history`) returns a paginated list of all your audio isolation generations. Supports `page_size`, `page`, and `search` query parameters.
>
> [Delete audio isolation history item](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/audio-isolation/delete) (`DELETE /v1/audio-isolation/history/{history_item_id}`) deletes a specific item and its associated media files.
>
