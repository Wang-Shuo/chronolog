# Voices

**ElevenLabs** · 2026-10-12 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/10/12) · from [[Releases/ElevenLabs/elevenlabs-2026-10-12-2026-10-12-16e5a6a0|ElevenLabs updates — 2026-10-12]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> [List voice collections](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/voices/collections/list) - `GET /v1/voices/collections` - List the voice collections the caller can access, sorted by title.
>
> [Create voice collection](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/voices/collections/create) - `POST /v1/voices/collections` - Create a collection with required `title` and `icon` (string, maximum 1,000 characters each).
>
> [Get voice collection](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/voices/collections/get) - `GET /v1/voices/collections/{collection_id}` - Retrieve a collection with its `title`, `icon` and the caller's `permission_on_resource` (`admin`, `editor`, `commenter` or `viewer`).
>
> [Update voice collection](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/voices/collections/update) - `PATCH /v1/voices/collections/{collection_id}` - Update the optional `title` or `icon`.
>
> [Delete voice collection](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/voices/collections/delete) - `DELETE /v1/voices/collections/{collection_id}`
>
> [Add voice to collection](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/voices/collections/add-voice) - `POST /v1/voices/collections/{collection_id}/voice/{voice_id}`
>
> [Remove voice from collection](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/voices/collections/remove-voice) - `DELETE /v1/voices/collections/{collection_id}/voice/{voice_id}`
>
> Source: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/10/12
