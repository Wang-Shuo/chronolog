# 2 Pricing Model changes in ElevenLabs updates — 2026-10-26

**ElevenLabs** · 2026-10-26 · [[Themes/pricing-model|Pricing Model]] · Digest · [Official source](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/10/26) · from [[Releases/ElevenLabs/elevenlabs-2026-10-26-2026-10-26-ca7c552d|ElevenLabs updates — 2026-10-26]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> **Call duration and billing**: Call charges and stored call duration stop when the call ends, so teardown time after the call is no longer billed or counted. Calls no longer continue for up to 50 seconds after ending when a knowledge base lookup is in progress.

> **Business plan image and video pricing**: Business plans on the current pricing pay the same image and video credit rate as other self-serve plans, about 17% fewer credits per image and per video second than before.

Source: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/10/26
