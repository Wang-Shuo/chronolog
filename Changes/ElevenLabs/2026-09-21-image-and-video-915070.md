# Image and video

**ElevenLabs** · 2026-09-21 · [[Themes/image-generation|Image Generation]] · Launch · [Official source](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/9/21) · from [[Releases/ElevenLabs/elevenlabs-2026-09-21-2026-09-21-6d2d4d80|ElevenLabs updates — 2026-09-21]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> [Create image generation](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/flows/image/create) - `POST /v1/flows/image`
>
> Added request variants for `gpt-image-2.5-flare` and `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst`.
>
> Both variants require `prompt` and `model_id`, accept up to 10 `images`, and add `resolution` (`1K`, `2K` or `4K`, default `1K`).
>
> `quality` accepts `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh` or `max` and defaults to `high`. `aspect_ratio` accepts `auto` and 14 fixed ratios and defaults to `16:9`.
>
> Source: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/9/21
