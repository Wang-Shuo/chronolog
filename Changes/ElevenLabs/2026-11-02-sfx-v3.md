# SFX v3

**ElevenLabs** · 2026-11-02 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/11/2) · from [[Releases/ElevenLabs/elevenlabs-2026-11-02-2026-11-02-7efe9660|ElevenLabs updates — 2026-11-02]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> [SFX v3](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/sound-effects#sfx-v3) (`eleven_text_to_sound_v3`), our newest sound effects model, is now available to every workspace. Compared with `eleven_text_to_sound_v2`, it follows prompts that contain several instructions more closely, handles prompts with music or vocals better, sounds more natural and is less likely to clip on high-frequency sounds such as chimes and whistles.
>
> In ElevenCreative, SFX v3 is the default model in Sound Effects, Studio and Flows, and the sound effects that Video to music adds are generated with it. In the API, set `model_id` to `eleven_text_to_sound_v3` on [Create sound effect](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-sound-effects/convert), and set `loop` to `true` for a sound effect that loops smoothly. `eleven_text_to_sound_v2` is deprecated and will be removed on March 1, 2027.
>
> Source: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/11/2
