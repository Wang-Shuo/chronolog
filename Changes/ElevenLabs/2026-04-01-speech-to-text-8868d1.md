# Speech to Text

**ElevenLabs** · 2026-04-01 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/4/1) · from [[Releases/ElevenLabs/elevenlabs-2026-04-01-2026-04-01-d4336c3f|ElevenLabs updates — 2026-04-01]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Transcribe from URL**: The [convert speech to text](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/speech-to-text/convert) endpoint now accepts a `source_url` parameter (string, optional) for transcribing audio or video from a hosted URL, including YouTube videos, TikTok videos, and other video hosting services. This can be used as an alternative to uploading a file directly.
