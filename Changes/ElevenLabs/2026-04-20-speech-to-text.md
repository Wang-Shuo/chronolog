# Speech to Text

**ElevenLabs** · 2026-04-20 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/4/20) · from [[Releases/ElevenLabs/elevenlabs-2026-04-20-2026-04-20-9278926e|ElevenLabs updates — 2026-04-20]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Speaker role detection**: The [convert speech to text](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/speech-to-text/convert) endpoint now accepts a `detect_speaker_roles` parameter (boolean, default `false`). When enabled alongside `diarize=true`, speaker labels are returned as `agent` and `customer` instead of `speaker_0`, `speaker_1`. This cannot be used with `use_multi_channel=true`. Usage incurs an additional 10% surcharge on the base transcription cost.
>
