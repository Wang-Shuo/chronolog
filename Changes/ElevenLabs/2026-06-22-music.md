# Music

**ElevenLabs** · 2026-06-22 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/6/22) · from [[Releases/ElevenLabs/elevenlabs-2026-06-22-2026-06-22-645b32b5|ElevenLabs updates — 2026-06-22]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Higher-quality output formats**: [Generate music](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/music/compose), [Generate music detailed](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/music/compose-detailed) and [Stream music](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/music/stream) add `mp3_48000_240` and `mp3_48000_320` to supported `output_format` values. When `output_format` is `auto`, v2 models now default to `mp3_48000_192` instead of `mp3_48000_128`.
>
> **Section duration flag clarification**: `respect_sections_durations` applies only with `composition_plan` on `music_v1`. On `music_v2`, section durations are always enforced and this flag is ignored.
>
> **Composition plan schema split**: Music request `composition_plan` uses a `oneOf` union of `MusicPrompt` and `CompositionPlan`. The former is used for `music_v1` composition plans and the latter for `music_v2`.
>
> Source: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/6/22
