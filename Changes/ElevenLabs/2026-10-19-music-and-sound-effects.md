# Music and Sound Effects

**ElevenLabs** · 2026-10-19 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/10/19) · from [[Releases/ElevenLabs/elevenlabs-2026-10-19-2026-10-19-dd219c14|ElevenLabs updates — 2026-10-19]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Zero Retention Mode**: Music generation, composition plan, stem separation and video to music endpoints, plus [Sound Effects](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-sound-effects/convert), accept `enable_logging=false` to use [Zero Retention Mode](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-api/resources/zero-retention-mode). Zero Retention Mode is available to enterprise customers. Music requests that set `store_for_inpainting=true` are rejected in this mode.
>
> **WAV output for sound effects**: [Sound Effects](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-sound-effects/convert) accepts `wav_8000`, `wav_16000`, `wav_22050`, `wav_24000`, `wav_32000`, `wav_44100` and `wav_48000` output formats.
>
> Source: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/10/19
