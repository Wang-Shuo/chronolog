<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> [v2.72.0](https://github.com/elevenlabs/elevenlabs-python/releases/tag/v2.72.0) - Added clients for voice collections, the audio effects catalog and triage ticket merging. Text to Speech and Text to Dialogue methods accept `audio_effects`, triage ticket list methods add search and filters, and the realtime Speech to Text client adds the `turn_prediction` commit strategy. `play()` and `save()` now accept any iterable of audio chunks, such as a list or tuple. `RealtimeEvents.CLOSE` handlers receive a dictionary with the WebSocket close `code` and `reason`; handlers without parameters continue to work.
>
