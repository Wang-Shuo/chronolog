# Video to Music

**ElevenLabs** · 2026-10-19 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · Launch · [Official source](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/10/19) · from [[Releases/ElevenLabs/elevenlabs-2026-10-19-2026-10-19-dd219c14|ElevenLabs updates — 2026-10-19]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Video to Music writes a soundtrack for your video. The [Video to music](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/music/video-to-music) API is now available: you upload a video and get back music that fits it, without writing a music prompt from scratch. Use it to score product videos, ads and social clips.
>
> You can send up to 10 video files (200 MB combined). They're joined in order and scored as one video. To steer the result, add a text `description` and up to 10 style `tags`.
>
> Three options are worth knowing. `use_audio_as_reference` takes the genre, BPM, instruments and mood from the video's own audio. `add_sfx` adds sound effects that match the video. `separate_tracks` returns the music and the sound effects as separate files in a ZIP, so you can mix them yourself.
>
> Source: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog/2026/10/19
