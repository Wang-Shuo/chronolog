**Claude** · 2026-07-14 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes#h_a90c8544e7) · from [[Releases/Claude/claude-2026-07-14-2026-07-14-0c63f428|Claude updates — 2026-07-14]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now manage HIPAA readiness for your Claude organizations yourself. This applies to both Claude Enterprise and the Claude Platform (API).
