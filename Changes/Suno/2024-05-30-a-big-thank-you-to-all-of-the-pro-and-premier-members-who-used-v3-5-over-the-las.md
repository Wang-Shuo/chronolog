**Suno** · 2024-05-30 · [[Themes/music-generation|Music Generation]] · [Official source](https://suno.com/release-notes/v3-5-is-available-to-everyone) · from [[Releases/Suno/suno-2024-05-30-e-v3-5-is-available-to-everyone-dd89fd7b|v3.5 is Available to Everyone]]
