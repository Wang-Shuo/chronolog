# You can search for any public song on Suno

**Suno** · 2024-05-22 · [[Themes/music-generation|Music Generation]] · Launch · [Official source](https://suno.com/release-notes/public-song-search) · from [[Releases/Suno/suno-2024-05-22-e-public-song-search-e5e00a03|🔎 Public Song Search]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We're excited to announce that you can search for any public song on Suno. You can search for:
>
> 1️⃣ Song Titles: Search for specific songs, like "Egyptian Dance Party".
>
> 2️⃣ Tags: Find music by genre, mood, or anything else artists use to describe their work. Try searching for "Rock" or "Chill".
>
> 3️⃣ Lyrics: Even if you only remember a catchy phrase, you can search for lyrics like "Cat, cat, cat" and find the song! (We know the struggle is real.)
>
> We’ll also be adding search to all songs in your Library in upcoming weeks as well.
>
> Source: https://suno.com/release-notes/public-song-search
