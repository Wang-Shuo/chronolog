# Pro and Premier Users can now Exclude Styles when creating new songs

**Suno** · 2024-09-19 · [[Themes/music-generation|Music Generation]] · [Official source](https://suno.com/release-notes/exclude-styles) · from [[Releases/Suno/suno-2024-09-19-e-exclude-styles-7304e8bb|Exclude Styles]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Pro and Premier Users can now Exclude Styles when creating new songs. This gives better control over specific attributes that you might want to exclude. Try excluding specific instruments, specific styles, or even specific vocal-styles.
>
> Excluded styles will be shown in the Song Preview sidebar and on the Song Page. They will be listed in the Styles section with a "-" sign in front, indicating their exclusion (e.g., -piano).
>
> 1 - Go to Create and select “Custom”
>
> 2 - Pro and Premier users will find an “Exclude Styles” switch. Switch on.
>
> 3 - Enter any styles you wish to exclude in the new section that appears.
>
> Early-Access Beta:
>
> This feature is currently in **Early-Access Beta** only for Pro/Premier users. It helps us a lot if you like and dislike songs as you use Exclude Styles. Doing so is the best way to help us improve.
>
> Source: https://suno.com/release-notes/exclude-styles
