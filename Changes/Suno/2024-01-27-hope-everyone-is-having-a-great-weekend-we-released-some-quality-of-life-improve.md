# Hope everyone is having a great weekend!

**Suno** · 2024-01-27 · [[Themes/music-generation|Music Generation]] · [Official source](https://suno.com/release-notes/quality-of-life-improvements) · from [[Releases/Suno/suno-2024-01-27-e-quality-of-life-improvements-1bc6fb75|Quality-of-Life Improvements]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Hope everyone is having a great weekend! We released some quality of life improvements based on your recent feedback:
>
> Deleting Songs
>
> You can now delete songs using the Move to Trash button. To delete a song:
>
> Click the three dots / ellipses to the right of the song you'd like to delete
>
> To view your Trash, click the `More Actions` button in your Library, and select `View Trash`. You can restore deleted songs to your Library, or you can choose to delete them permanently. To restore a song to your Library from your Trash, select the song in your Trash, and click `Remove from Trash`. To delete a song permanently, select the song in your Trash, and click `Delete Permanently`.
>
> Random Style
>
> We've heard people mention needing style inspo when making songs using Custom Mode. You can now generate songs with a random style by clicking on the Use Random Style button in Custom Mode.
>
> We are receiving a tremendous volume of emails, and we sincerely apologize for our delayed responses to emails you've sent to [email protected] , [email protected] , and [email protected] . We are scaling up our support function to help us respond to each and every one of your emails.
>
> Source: https://suno.com/release-notes/quality-of-life-improvements
