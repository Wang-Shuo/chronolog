# Introducing Personas 🎤

**Suno** · 2024-10-31 · [[Themes/music-generation|Music Generation]] · Launch · [Official source](https://suno.com/release-notes/introducing-personas) · from [[Releases/Suno/suno-2024-10-31-e-introducing-personas-27571212|Introducing Personas 🎤]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’re excited to introduce Personas! Personas let you save the essence of a song - vocals, style, vibe - and reimagine it across your creations. Want to bring your favorite vocals or style into a new song?\<br/>\<br/>
>
> 1 - Find a song you love. Click “...” then go to “Create” and “Make a Persona”.\<br/>
>
> 2 - Toggle between Public and Private for your Persona. Public Personas can be used by others, will have their own page, and can be found in your library and profile.
>
> 3 - Add lyrics and style like you would any other song!\<br/>
>
> This feature is available to all Pro & Premier members in early-access beta. You get 200 free songs to make with Personas - after that, each new song made with Personas will cost 10 credits.
>
> We can’t wait to see what you do with your Personas!
>
> Source: https://suno.com/release-notes/introducing-personas
