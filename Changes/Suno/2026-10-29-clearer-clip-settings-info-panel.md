# Clearer Clip Settings & Info Panel

**Suno** · 2026-10-29 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://suno.com/release-notes/more-studio-updates-oct26) · from [[Releases/Suno/suno-2026-10-29-e-more-studio-updates-oct26-37e23464|Suno Studio Updates, Shaped by your Feedback]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We heard from a lot of you that the Clip Settings in Studio 2.0 were hard to find and difficult to read, due to their small size. Now, Clip Settings are in a sidebar to the left of the waveform viewer. Double-click on a clip to access volume, pitch, loop, reverse and timestretching features, all from one clearer and more spacious panel.
>
> A clip’s lyrics, style tags, and stem separation can now be found in the new Clip Info panel, accessible from the "Clip Info" item in the audio clip right click menu, or the button at the bottom of the new Clip Settings sidebar.
>
> Source: https://suno.com/release-notes/more-studio-updates-oct26
