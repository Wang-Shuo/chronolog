**Suno** · 2026-10-29 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://suno.com/release-notes/more-studio-updates-oct26) · from [[Releases/Suno/suno-2026-10-29-e-more-studio-updates-oct26-37e23464|Suno Studio Updates, Shaped by your Feedback]]

> We’ve added a new setting that lets you specify whether added audio from your library or through file uploads gets automatically timestretched to the project tempo.
