# Introducing Hooks!

**Suno** · 2025-09-22 · [[Themes/music-generation|Music Generation]] · Launch · [Official source](https://suno.com/release-notes/introducing-hooks) · from [[Releases/Suno/suno-2025-09-22-e-introducing-hooks-eea05f09|Introducing Hooks!]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Introducing Hooks — a new way to bring your Suno songs to life through short-form music videos. Your creativity shouldn’t be limited to audio alone. With Hooks, every song becomes a visual story your audience can see as well as hear. Hooks are free to create — they don’t cost credits — and while Suno doesn’t generate the video for you, we make it easy to pair your own video with any of your Suno songs or remixes to create engaging, shareable content. When you discover a Hook that moves you, the journey doesn’t end there: you can remix that track to create your own unique version, then make a Hook of the remix. Ready to make your music move?
>
