# Edit Lyrics are available from within the Song Page

**Suno** · 2024-07-12 · [[Themes/music-generation|Music Generation]] · [Official source](https://suno.com/release-notes/edit-lyrics-are-available-from-within-the-song-page) · from [[Releases/Suno/suno-2024-07-12-e-edit-lyrics-are-available-from-within-the-song-page-a606b352|Edit Lyrics are available from within the Song Page]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> 1 - Open up any song you’ve created (by clicking on the Song through Library or Create)
>
> 2 - Scroll down to the bottom of the page, and click “Edit Displayed Lyrics” to make edits
>
> 3 - To save your changes, just click anywhere on the page, or click on the “Save” button
>
> Please note that editing Lyrics will not update the lyrics sung in the song.
>
> Have feedback on this feature? Please tell us what you think by filling out this form: https://forms.gle/xWmRmzHQrn8Bmrzu9
>
