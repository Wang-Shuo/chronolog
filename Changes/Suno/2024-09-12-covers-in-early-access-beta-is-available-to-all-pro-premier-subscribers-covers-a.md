# Covers (in early-access beta) is available to all Pro/Premier subscribers

**Suno** · 2024-09-12 · [[Themes/music-generation|Music Generation]] · [Official source](https://suno.com/release-notes/covers-reimagine-the-music-you-love) · from [[Releases/Suno/suno-2024-09-12-e-covers-reimagine-the-music-you-love-374b7b0b|Covers - Reimagine the music you love]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Covers (in early-access beta) is available to all Pro/Premier subscribers. Covers allow you to keep the melody of your songs but adopt them into a different style.
>
> Cover Any of Your Own Songs By
>
> 1 - Finding songs in your Library or Create page
>
> 2 - Right click or click the vertical "..."
>
> 3 - Hover over Create > Click "Cover Song"
>
> 4 - Lyrics will automatically be pulled forward into the song you’re covering. For best results, keep lyrics the same to start. But you can change lyrics / add to them as you experiment.
>
> **Your first 200 covers are free!** Once the initial 200 covers are used, each new cover generation will cost 10 credits, similar to generating new songs.
>
> **Additional Creative Possibilities**
>
> Source: https://suno.com/release-notes/covers-reimagine-the-music-you-love
