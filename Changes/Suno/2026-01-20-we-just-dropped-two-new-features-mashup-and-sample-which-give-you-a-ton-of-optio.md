# We just dropped two new features

**Suno** · 2026-01-20 · [[Themes/music-generation|Music Generation]] · [Official source](https://suno.com/release-notes/meet-our-new-create-features-sample-mashup) · from [[Releases/Suno/suno-2026-01-20-e-meet-our-new-create-features-sample-mashup-a1f8179d|Meet our new Create features: Sample + Mashup]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We just dropped two new features: Mashup and Sample, which give you a ton of options when you want to take your songs to new places.
>
> Mashup lets you combine any two songs, mash them together and come up with something totally unique. Try blending two similar tracks to make a new version that borrows elements from both. Or choose two totally different tracks to get happy accidents beyond your wildest imagination.
>
> Sample lets you select a section of sound as the jumping off point for new ideas. Whether it’s a snippet from a Suno song, a melody in your voice memos or something you heard and loved out in the world… chop it up and drop it into the Sample feature.
>
> Source: https://suno.com/release-notes/meet-our-new-create-features-sample-mashup
