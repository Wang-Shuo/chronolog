> Sounds is a new Create mode that lets you create individual one-shot samples and loops from scratch.
>
> Need an airhorn sample, glass smash or airplane for your track, or footsteps for your foley project? We’ve got you covered — choose ‘one-shot’, type your prompt and hit Create.
>
> Want a specific loop, like a guitar riff, a bassline or synth lick to start an idea, or add to an existing Studio session? Choose loop, select your key and tempo (bpm) and describe the sound you want.
