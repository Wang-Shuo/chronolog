# Stem Separation improvements

**Suno** · 2026-06-11 · [[Themes/music-generation|Music Generation]] · [Official source](https://suno.com/release-notes/advanced-stems) · from [[Releases/Suno/suno-2026-06-11-e-advanced-stems-c0cbc219|Stem Separation improvements]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Our updated Stem Separation tool gives you three ways to extract stems from your music.
>
> [New] Advanced Split - Choose exactly what to extract from a list of nearly 100 instruments, from drum kit to didgeridoo. For Premier subscribers only.
>
> [Updated] Split from Mix - Pull any instrument or voice from the mix and get two stems: the instrument or voice, and everything else without it.
>
> [Staying the same] Auto Split - Our classic model, splitting songs into 12 stem categories. Great for quick separation.
>
> With the new improvements, stems are cleaner, crisper, free from artifacts and ready to drop into your track. Select Get Stems from the Edit menu and start extracting.
>
> Source: https://suno.com/release-notes/advanced-stems
