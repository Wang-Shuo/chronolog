# MiniMax H3 Max Turbo and native 1080p

**Krea** · 2026-09-09 · [[Themes/reliability-performance|Reliability and Performance]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.krea.ai/docs/changelog) · from [[Releases/Krea/krea-2026-09-09-2026-09-09-elements-in-seedance-studio-gpt-image-2-5-models-mini-abc5ae0d|Elements in Seedance Studio, GPT Image 2.5 models, MiniMax H3 Max Turbo with native 1080p, postpaid invoicing, and Kling retirements]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **MiniMax H3 Max Turbo is a faster, cheaper H3 Max tier, and both models now generate native 1080p.** Turbo keeps H3 Max's prompt adherence while cutting cost and latency.
>
> Turbo generates 5 to 15 second clips at 480p, 768p, or 1080p, with start and end frames, seed control, and prompt expansion modes.
>
> Turbo API pricing is $0.0063 per second at 480p, $0.01 per second at 768p, and \$0.02 per second at 1080p.
>
> H3 Max adds 1080p for text-to-video and image-to-video at \$0.04 per second. Reference-to-video stays at 480p and 768p for now.
>
> Current H3 Max and Turbo rates are promotional and run through September 15.
>
> Source: https://www.krea.ai/docs/changelog
