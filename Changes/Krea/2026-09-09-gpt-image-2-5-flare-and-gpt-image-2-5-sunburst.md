# GPT Image 2.5 Flare and GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst

**Krea** · 2026-09-09 · [[Themes/image-generation|Image Generation]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.krea.ai/docs/changelog) · from [[Releases/Krea/krea-2026-09-09-2026-09-09-elements-in-seedance-studio-gpt-image-2-5-models-mini-abc5ae0d|Elements in Seedance Studio, GPT Image 2.5 models, MiniMax H3 Max Turbo with native 1080p, postpaid invoicing, and Kling retirements]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Two new OpenAI image models are available through the API.** GPT Image 2.5 Flare supports transparent backgrounds, and GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst is optimized for speed.
>
> Flare accepts a `background` setting (`transparent`, `opaque`, or `auto`). Set `transparent` to get a cutout.
>
> Both models offer five quality tiers (`low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, `max`) and generate at 1K in 1:1, 3:2, or 2:3.
>
> Both accept up to 10 reference images through `image_urls`.
>
> Source: https://www.krea.ai/docs/changelog
