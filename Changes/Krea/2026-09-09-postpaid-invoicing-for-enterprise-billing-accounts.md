# Postpaid invoicing for enterprise billing accounts

**Krea** · 2026-09-09 · [[Themes/pricing-model|Pricing Model]] · Pricing · [Official source](https://www.krea.ai/docs/changelog) · from [[Releases/Krea/krea-2026-09-09-2026-09-09-elements-in-seedance-studio-gpt-image-2-5-models-mini-abc5ae0d|Elements in Seedance Studio, GPT Image 2.5 models, MiniMax H3 Max Turbo with native 1080p, postpaid invoicing, and Kling retirements]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Enterprise billing accounts with postpaid billing enabled can now review invoiced usage in Krea.** Organization Owners and Admins see accruing, outstanding, and historical invoices under **Billing** in [Manage](https://www.krea.ai/manage).
>
> API usage and open source license usage accrue as separate line items on one monthly usage invoice.
>
> Invoices go out at the end of the month in UTC, and payment is due 30 days after an invoice is sent.
>
> Open a hosted Stripe invoice from the Billing view to see the amount due and pay.
>
> Source: https://www.krea.ai/docs/changelog
