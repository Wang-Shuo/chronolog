# MiniMax H3 and MiniMax H3 Max

**Krea** · 2026-08-27 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.krea.ai/docs/changelog) · from [[Releases/Krea/krea-2026-08-27-2026-08-27-minimax-h3-and-h3-max-gemini-omni-flash-1-1-and-a-wor-7350de0b|MiniMax H3 and H3 Max, Gemini Omni Flash 1.1, and a workspace usage API]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Two new MiniMax video models are available in the Video tool and the API.** MiniMax H3 is a frontier 2K model built for reference-driven generation. MiniMax H3 Max is a post-trained H3 variant with stronger prompt adherence and better aesthetics.
>
> **MiniMax H3** accepts up to 9 reference images, plus reference videos and audio, and animates between start and end frames. Clips run 5 to 15 seconds.
>
> **MiniMax H3 Max** generates at 480p or 768p, supports start and end frames, and adds seed control and prompt expansion modes (`disabled`, `fast`, `balanced`, `quality`).
>
> Both models accept prompts up to 7,000 characters, so elaborate, programmatic direction fits without trimming.
>
> **H3 Max launch pricing**: $0.025 per second at 480p and $0.04 per second at 768p through the API. These are promotional rates at 50% off; the discount ends September 1.
>
> Source: https://www.krea.ai/docs/changelog
