# Organizations in private beta for enterprise

**Krea** · 2026-06-19 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.krea.ai/docs/changelog) · from [[Releases/Krea/krea-2026-06-19-2026-06-19-organizations-for-enterprise-dc46f34c|Organizations for enterprise]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Organizations are now in private beta for enterprise customers.** An Organization sits above your workspaces and centralizes billing, members, verified domains, and Single Sign-On under one enterprise account.
>
> **Owner, Admin, and Member roles** at the organization level, separate from workspace roles.
>
> **Organization dashboard** for Owners and Admins to create workspaces, invite members, assign roles, and manage access in one place.
>
> **Domain verification** unlocks Domain Capture (auto-enroll users with matching email addresses) and is a prerequisite for SAML SSO.
>
> **SAML Single Sign-On** so your team signs in with your identity provider.
>
> **Model Access Controls** (Business and Enterprise) to restrict which AI models are available to members using allowlist or blocklist modes.
>
> Start with [Organizations](https://www.krea.ai/docs/user-guide/help-and-support/organizations.md), then see [Managing your organization](https://www.krea.ai/docs/user-guide/help-and-support/organization-management.md), [Domain verification](https://www.krea.ai/docs/user-guide/help-and-support/domain-verification.md), [SAML SSO setup](https://www.krea.ai/docs/user-guide/help-and-support/saml-sso-setup.md), and [Model access controls](https://www.krea.ai/docs/user-guide/help-and-support/model-access-controls.md). [Contact sales](mailto:sales@krea.ai) to join the beta.
>
> Source: https://www.krea.ai/docs/changelog
