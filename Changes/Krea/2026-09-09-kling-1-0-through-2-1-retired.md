# Kling 1.0 through 2.1 retired

**Krea** · 2026-09-09 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Policy · [Official source](https://www.krea.ai/docs/changelog) · from [[Releases/Krea/krea-2026-09-09-2026-09-09-elements-in-seedance-studio-gpt-image-2-5-models-mini-abc5ae0d|Elements in Seedance Studio, GPT Image 2.5 models, MiniMax H3 Max Turbo with native 1080p, postpaid invoicing, and Kling retirements]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Kling is retiring Kling 1.0, 1.5, 1.6, 2.0 Master, and 2.1, so these models no longer appear in Krea's Video tool.** Their API endpoints are deprecated and sunset on September 15, 2026.
>
> Until September 15, the endpoints keep working and return `Deprecation` and `Sunset` headers. After that, they return `410 Gone`.
>
> Kling 2.5 is the recommended replacement. Kling 2.6, Kling 3.0, and Kling o1 also remain available.
>
> Existing generations from the retired models stay in your library.
>
> Source: https://www.krea.ai/docs/changelog
<!-- chronolog:end -->
