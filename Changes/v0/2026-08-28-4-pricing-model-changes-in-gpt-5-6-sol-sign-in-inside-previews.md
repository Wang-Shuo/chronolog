# 4 Pricing Model changes in GPT-5.6 Sol, sign-in inside previews…

**v0** · 2026-08-28 · [[Themes/pricing-model|Pricing Model]] · Digest · [Official source](https://v0.app/changelog#2026-08-28) · from [[Releases/v0/v0-2026-08-28-2026-08-28-1fac4f32|GPT-5.6 Sol, sign-in inside previews, and zero-config AI Gateway for generated apps]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Team upgrade and buy-more-credits tax estimates use the team's billing address, and paid scopes no longer see free-tier upgrade prompts.

> Duplicate checkout requests are deduplicated so a subscription cannot be created twice, and you return to the chat you upgraded from after a plan change.

> Enterprise-managed accounts keep their enterprise billing and gates when they have only team scopes, and a username that collides with a team slug no longer shows a paid team's balance as zero.

> Paid Enterprise teams default to token billing, so teams with a shared credit pool but no seat credits can spend it and see a Credits usage tab.
