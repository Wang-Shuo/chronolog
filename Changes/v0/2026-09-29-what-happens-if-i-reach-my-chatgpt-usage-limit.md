# What happens if I reach my ChatGPT usage limit?

**v0** · 2026-09-29 · [[Themes/pricing-model|Pricing Model]] · Policy · [Official source](https://v0.app/changelog#2026-09-29-chatgpt-subscription) · from [[Releases/v0/v0-2026-09-29-2026-09-29-chatgpt-subscription-674fbcd7|Use your ChatGPT subscription in v0]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> If you reach your ChatGPT usage limit, v0 pauses generation and saves your progress. You can wait until your allowance becomes available again or choose Continue with v0 credits to keep building.
>
> v0 will not automatically switch AI responses to v0 credits. Using v0 credits for these responses requires you to explicitly change your billing option.
>
> Connect your ChatGPT subscription in v0 to get started.
>
