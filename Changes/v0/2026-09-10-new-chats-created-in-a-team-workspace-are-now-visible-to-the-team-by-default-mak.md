# Team chats are easier to discover and collaborate on

**v0** · 2026-09-10 · [[Themes/collaboration|Collaboration]] · [Official source](https://v0.app/changelog#2026-09-10) · from [[Releases/v0/v0-2026-09-10-2026-09-10-3c3f563a|Team chats are easier to discover and collaborate on]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> New chats created in a team workspace are now visible to the team by default, making it easier for teammates to discover shared work and collaborate without waiting for an invitation.
>
> Team owners can choose the default for new chats in Settings → Workspace → Default Chat Visibility :
>
> Private : Restricts the chat to people with access.
>
> Team can view : Team members can view the chat; editing remains limited to people with edit access.
>
> Team can edit : Team members can view and edit the chat.
>
> The workspace setting applies only to new chats. Existing chats keep their current visibility, and chat creators can still change an individual chat from the Invite dialog.
>
> Project cards also make recent collaboration easier to spot. They now show the teammates who have been active in visible project chats during the last 24 hours, ordered by their most recent activity. The avatars represent recent chat activity, not live presence.
>
> Source: https://v0.app/changelog#2026-09-10
