**v0** · 2026-09-22 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · [Official source](https://v0.app/changelog#2026-09-22-model-picker) · from [[Releases/v0/v0-2026-09-22-2026-09-22-model-picker-d8bf5ce7|More model choices, with Enterprise owner controls]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> The updated model picker lets you browse and search available AI Gateway models through Add models in the chat composer. Add models to your picker, review token pricing, and customize Thinking and Fast Mode where supported.
>
> The updated model picker is now available to Enterprise teams with owner-controlled access. Teams start with v0 models, and members can browse locked custom models. A team owner can enable Allow custom models in Workspace settings to accept custom models for the team, including future releases. Existing Enterprise teams also need to opt in, even if they previously turned off Restrict to v0 Models .
>
> Owners can turn off custom model access again in Workspace settings. Individual model restrictions and isolated tenant restrictions continue to apply.
>
> Learn how to choose and customize models and manage Enterprise model access .
