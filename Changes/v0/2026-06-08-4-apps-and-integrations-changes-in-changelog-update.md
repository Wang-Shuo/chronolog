**v0** · 2026-06-08 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Digest · [Official source](https://v0.app/changelog#2026-06-08) · from [[Releases/v0/v0-2026-06-08-2026-06-08-4f50e6b2|Changelog update]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Added a Shopify integration. Connect your store from a chat and v0 builds storefronts against it with the Shopify AI Toolkit.

> The Snowflake integration is now in public preview. Connect your account and v0 builds dashboards and apps on top of your data, and you can share Snowflake-connected chats with your team.

> Generated apps no longer surface "GENERATING is not defined" in the preview, and build errors no longer leak into the runtime error overlay.

> Updated docs for agentic features and the Snowflake integration.
