# 3 other changes in v2026.9.8

**OpenClaw** · 2026-10-03 · Other changes · Digest · [Official source](https://docs.openclaw.ai/releases/2026.9.8) · from [[Releases/OpenClaw/openclaw-2026-10-03-v2026-9-8-0844d8d3|v2026.9.8]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Windows updates. If Windows briefly reports that installation files are in use, the updater retries. If it still cannot replace them, it leaves the installed package in place. An older updater cannot gain this fix mid-update; follow the update guidance if the first upgrade fails. #163074 , backported by @RomneyDa , with work by @fuller-stack-dev and @obviyus .

> Replies after reloads and wake. Fixed a case where a channel looked connected after a reload or waking the computer, but could no longer send replies. The problem was reported with Matrix. #163504 , backporting #163268 . Thanks to upstream author @scotthuang and contributor @vincentkoc .

> Thanks to @ericcaiwx-star , @fuller-stack-dev , @obviyus , @Patrick-Erichsen , @RomneyDa , @scotthuang , @VACInc and @vincentkoc for their contributions across this release.
