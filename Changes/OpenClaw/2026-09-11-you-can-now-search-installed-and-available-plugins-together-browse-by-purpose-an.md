# Search installed and available plugins together, browse by purpose, and keep…

**OpenClaw** · 2026-09-11 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · [Official source](https://docs.openclaw.ai/releases/2026.9.4) · from [[Releases/OpenClaw/openclaw-2026-09-11-v2026-9-4-6cdeafb2|v2026.9.4]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now search installed and available plugins together, browse by purpose, and keep exploring without clicking through pages. In chat , your Claw can recommend up to three official plugins or skills and give you a card that opens their details. Recommendations need the message tool enabled, and choosing Install opens a review before anything is added. An installed plugin may still need settings or an account connection before you can use it.
>
> Source: https://docs.openclaw.ai/releases/2026.9.4
