# 3 Model Support changes in v2026.10.3

**OpenClaw** · 2026-10-13 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Digest · [Official source](https://docs.openclaw.ai/releases/2026.10.3) · from [[Releases/OpenClaw/openclaw-2026-10-13-v2026-10-3-54cedb60|v2026.10.3]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> MiniMax M3.1 Flash Preview. Eligible Token Plan or MiniMax Code users can select the preview model and choose low, medium, high, xhigh or max thinking effort. The preview defaults to max, including saved off or adaptive selections. M3 remains the default model. Missing published catalog rates do not mean free use; see the MiniMax guide . #160089 , thanks to @NianJiuZst , @RomneyDa , @steipete , @Takhoffman , @php737 , @ns-spec , @Hansen1018 and @olveww-dot .

> Interrupted ChatGPT replies. An unfinished ChatGPT reply can continue from the saved conversation when a normal connection close interrupts it, within the existing retry limits. See model failover guidance . #167512 , thanks to @ericcurtin , @obviyus , @mightyx and @aleps001 .

> Copilot prompt capacity. Models with missing native limits can use the account's reported prompt capacity instead of the 128K estimate, while real limits and configured caps remain effective. Refresh discovery to replace previously cached estimates. #156636 , thanks to @saju01 , @RomneyDa , @snotty , @vinaycodeaxe , @zhangguiping-xydt and @Yigtwxx .
