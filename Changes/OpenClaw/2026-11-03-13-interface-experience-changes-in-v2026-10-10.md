# 13 Interface Experience changes in v2026.10.10

**OpenClaw** · 2026-11-03 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Digest · [Official source](https://docs.openclaw.ai/releases/2026.10.10) · from [[Releases/OpenClaw/openclaw-2026-11-03-v2026-10-10-c26ff355|v2026.10.10]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Worktrees tabs support keyboard navigation and keep the selected tab and keyboard focus visible in high-contrast mode.

> Pin and reorder pages and conversations in your sidebar. After upgrading, add any old personal shortcuts again because they do not carry over.

> Reuse a custom snooze time from another conversation's menu without entering it again.

> Find Copy link near the top of a conversation's menu, and start a new conversation from any sidebar view, including on mobile.

> Switch the Users sidebar between Online and All when you want to see more than the people currently connected.

> Reconnecting or refreshing the conversation sidebar no longer repeatedly drops the selected chat or briefly replaces its list with empty rows. Archived conversation dashboards also stay out of Pages.

> Conversation search now finds names written in equivalent Unicode forms and phrases separated by punctuation. If searching transcripts takes too long, it suggests narrowing the search.

> Unsent drafts , attachments and follow-up messages stay with the chat you wrote them in when you navigate away or reconnect. If a send is still unconfirmed, check the chat history before sending it again.

> Long HTML conversation exports display their transcript and navigation again. Regenerate older exports to get the fix.

> You can move an eligible conversation from its session menu to a local or remote computer, or retry a failed move to the same destination. Moving an active remote conversation interrupts its current turn without replaying it. Leaving an offline device requires confirmation.

> A valid Incognito computer selection no longer fails just because cleanup of an unrelated database lookup failed.

> Mac app navigation and panel controls no longer crowd or cover the native window buttons.

> On Wear OS, Retry stays reachable on round screens and larger text remains scrollable; replies, speech and navigation also recover from more interrupted states.

Source: https://docs.openclaw.ai/releases/2026.10.10
