# 24 other changes in v2026.10.3

**OpenClaw** · 2026-10-13 · Other changes · Digest · [Official source](https://docs.openclaw.ai/releases/2026.10.3) · from [[Releases/OpenClaw/openclaw-2026-10-13-v2026-10-3-54cedb60|v2026.10.3]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Blocked updates. Updates can clear eligible leftover recovery work and archive completed update records that previously blocked the next upgrade. Already-blocked older updaters need the manual installation or recovery first step ; an independent recovery helper can preserve the completed record and unblock the original updater. #168388 , #169038 , thanks to @steipete , @k94118 , @kimjinsuhh , @GoldenrodScript , @neilofneils404 , @mkdelta221 , @waynegault , @clawdgawd , @yoyo837 , @lishewen , @nukk-pain , @jullianatolentino , @joshpetras and @creanet-64 .

> Interrupted 2026.9.8 updates. The updated CLI can settle supported aborted records that prevented another update. This legacy recovery path warns when package contents could not be rechecked, so keep the recovery evidence and follow the repair guidance . #169027 , thanks to @steipete and @chedong .

> FreeBSD package recovery. Recovery helpers produced by the fixed updater can repair or retire interrupted npm package updates on FreeBSD. Older helpers still support status only, and package recovery remains separate from restarting or verifying the Gateway. See the updating guide . #168775 , thanks to @steipete .

> Leftover updater copies. Doctor can remove an eligible abandoned npm updater copy that prevents the next update. Wait for updater workers to finish, run openclaw doctor --fix from the affected installation, then retry. Keep the recovery journal and its permissions intact; see update troubleshooting . #170170 , thanks to @steipete , @sunnyshu0925 and @warhangel .

> Candidate checks with older updaters. A newer candidate is no longer rejected solely because Doctor would update version, setup or migration bookkeeping. Real settings repairs and substantive migrations still follow their existing handling. See the update guide . #170195 , thanks to @steipete , @maestrokling and @frxiaobei .

> Older Node updaters. Updates avoid an unnecessary rollback when older and newer update processes measure time differently and mistake a valid update for a lost one. #169915 , thanks to @vincentkoc .

> Windows update and recovery paths. Updater workers recognize short names and different capitalization for the same installation folder. Recovery snapshots also stop rejecting their own Windows temporary directory. The path correction requires the fixed updater to be installed before it starts the upgrade. #169292 , #169397 , thanks to @RomneyDa and @steipete .

> pnpm service commands. New or repaired Gateway service commands use pnpm's durable installation link, so replacing a versioned package folder does not break them. Update planning also recognizes installations reached through nested pnpm paths. Existing commands pinned to an old folder need repair or replanning; see update methods . #170003 , thanks to @steipete , @xilopaint and @k-sekula .

> Service refresh refusals. An update can restart the preserved Gateway service after safely refusing to rewrite its definition. Restart still requires the existing definition and service ownership to pass revalidation, preserving customizations. See how updates run . #169658 , thanks to @steipete .

> Busy state databases. Optional progress logging no longer aborts an update solely because the database is briefly busy, and update-history writes spend less time blocking Gateway work. Required recovery writes remain mandatory. These driver-side improvements require the fixed updater to start the upgrade; see the update guide . #168210 , #169283 , thanks to @steipete , @jlapenna and @DonnieFi .

> Unrelated dependency copying. Updates avoid copying dependencies from unrelated enclosing installations and scanning unrelated npm-global tools. The updater starting the upgrade must already contain these fixes; see the updating guide . #168927 , #169551 , thanks to @steipete , @RomneyDa , @goutamadwant and @holgergruenhagen .

> Update handoff reports. An already reported update outcome no longer produces a second misleading failure, and an update accepted by the background helper stays pending. Use the printed status and health commands to check its eventual result; see restart handoff . #168587 , #168993 , thanks to @steipete and @sayitfast .

> Update warning details. Recovery-copy warnings identify the affected database, while a failed candidate configuration read explains the error and leaves the service definition unchanged. See recovery captures and the updating guide . #169282 , #169284 , thanks to @DonnieFi and @RomneyDa .

> Channel tools after restart. Messaging accounts started or restarted by a chat or operator task keep using channel tools after that task finishes, with each later turn's permissions still enforced. #159396 , thanks to @ceckert , @RomneyDa , @slinkoff , @fuller-stack-dev and @sjf-oa .

> Messages already being sent. Recovery scanning leaves a message with its active sender, preventing the reported race that left queued messages unsent. #169010 , thanks to @RomneyDa .

> Steered conversations after restart. Human conversations can resume after a correction and Gateway restart when their original authorized source survives. Already-failed sessions are not reopened, and missing sender permissions are not reconstructed. See restart recovery . #170252 , thanks to @steipete .

> On this Mac setup. The Mac app can connect in eligible “On this Mac” setups that previously failed with “Gateway rejected auth”, with less unnecessary preparation and waiting. If a required new token cannot be saved, setup stops with a retryable error. See the bundled Gateway guide . #169403 , thanks to @steipete .

> Mac reconnection after renewal. The Mac app reconnects automatically after a trusted Gateway certificate renewal. Learned fingerprints can renew after normal trust checks; explicitly configured fingerprints stay fixed. See remote connection guidance . #168151 , thanks to @steipete and @fuller-stack-dev .

> Claude CLI tool discovery. Claude CLI receives the registered OpenClaw tool names and guidance for replies that must be sent through a tool, reducing failed lookups. This guidance applies to tools actually available to the conversation. See the CLI backend guide . #153032 , thanks to @kiranvk-2011 , @RomneyDa , @lakemike , @philknows , @destinedenergy and @wbumgardner-max .

> DeepSeek replies and tool actions. Recognized internal tool markup stays out of replies, and managed turns can recover valid marked-up tool actions, including the calls wrapper. Direct provider calls and final replies filter the markup without recovering tools; malformed or unfinished recognized blocks stay hidden without execution. See the DeepSeek guide . #169728 , thanks to @steipete and @TOMUIV .

> Unchanged one-shot jobs. Doctor stops making redundant recovery copies for one-shot jobs whose saved timestamps are already correct. Genuine repairs keep their backup protections. #168938 , thanks to @harshitgupta31415 and @wg-mojo .

> Commands on paired nodes. An unchanged connected node keeps handling commands, requests and events while unrelated pairing information is updated or another device's pairing is being written. Actual revocation and token rotation still reject the old connection. #169486 , #169575 , thanks to @steipete and @gisk0 .

> Restart with an unreachable cloud worker. The Gateway can stop or restart while deferring the stop of an unreachable stored cloud worker and reporting that deferral. The remote machine is not immediately stopped or deleted; later cleanup remains required. #168907 , thanks to @steipete .

> Deleting an unfinished conversation. Deleting a session records cancellation before stopping its unfinished reply, allowing chat clients to receive the cancelled status. e115a6a .
