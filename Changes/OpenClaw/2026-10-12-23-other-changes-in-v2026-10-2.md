# 23 other changes in v2026.10.2

**OpenClaw** · 2026-10-12 · Other changes · Digest · [Official source](https://docs.openclaw.ai/releases/2026.10.2) · from [[Releases/OpenClaw/openclaw-2026-10-12-v2026-10-2-9abf70fc|v2026.10.2]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> OpenClaw 2026.10.2 fixes errors during upgrades from 2026.10.1 and Doctor repairs, addresses missing or repeated replies, and helps long conversations continue, with fixes for credential masking in large tool output.

> Node during installation. Recovery installs use the compatible Node runtime OpenClaw selected even when an older system Node comes first in PATH, avoiding a misleading package-script version error. See the Node setup guide . #164677 , thanks to @steipete and @jarvisnextgeneration20-lang .

> Dashboard replies. Chats can answer after a daily or idle reset when background title work previously blocked them. Background commands that delegate and wait also avoid producing the final answer twice. #162689 , #166362 , thanks to @ericcaiwx-star , @dimonnld , @OmniScaleMatt , @gloria0021 , @obviyus and @shakkernerd .

> Windows update checks. Windows updates no longer reject a valid update solely because of how its launcher starts it. Backup checks also recognize short names and different letter casing for the same folder. #166530 , #168968 , thanks to @steipete , @wqj11a , @H0Nm4M1suZu and @RomneyDa .

> Update cleanup. A fixed CLI no longer reports a missing-module error while cleaning up an update that already succeeded. An older CLI can still show that error during its first upgrade; use the newly installed openclaw update status to check the result. See the update guide . #165233 , thanks to @steipete .

> Update and repair reports. An update confirmed successful in saved history no longer appears to fail just because OpenClaw cannot display its result. Optional configuration fallback notices now appear as warnings. Repair reports also call out configured chat accounts that remain stopped. Use openclaw health --json to investigate those accounts; see the repair guide . #169037 , #165539 , thanks to @steipete , @kvnloo , @RomneyDa and @Patrick-Erichsen .

> Container file copying. Updates can copy file bytes when a container blocks file cloning. Affected 2026.9.7 installations need one manual npm installation of a fixed release before later updates can use the repair. Keep native filesystem safeguards enabled and follow the recovery steps . #165257 , thanks to @steipete , @MrJanvar , @rybing7 and @Mohl .

> Workspace avatars. Update checks no longer reject valid absolute avatar paths inside your workspace. An older updater may still need a simple workspace-relative path such as avatar.png until it has been updated. #164638 , thanks to @nitinjwadhawan , @vincentkoc and @BottaniCals .

> Doctor after container replacement. After replacing an exited container, Doctor can wait for the previous Gateway's lock to expire and continue repairs. Updates also avoid a false “undergoing offline maintenance” refusal caused by Doctor's own work. If Doctor reports a live Gateway, follow the stop instructions in the Doctor recovery guide . #165355 , #165417 , thanks to @steipete .

> Linux repair checks. Timestamp changes on an unchanged database no longer cause false update or recovery refusals. Slow local database checks also avoid a false service-manager timeout before Doctor can stop the Gateway for maintenance. #165775 , #168161 , thanks to @steipete , @donquiche61 , @naturecurly , @dreamkeeper , @wangmiao0668000666 , @clockthunder , @honghongxinxi-zhb , @jayzhou2309 , @obviyus and @Tosko4 .

> Restart after refused maintenance. If an update finds OpenClaw already current but stops the Gateway for maintenance that cannot proceed, it can restart the service once recovery is safe. Maintenance still reports failure. Services you stopped yourself, or those kept stopped because data is at risk, remain stopped. #166548 , thanks to @steipete and @Shirleyyyhk .

> Telegram previews. A partial reply stays visible while its replacement is delayed or rejected. The old preview is removed after the replacement is accepted, preserving useful text even when the final send fails. See the streaming guide . #164649 , thanks to @jayzhou2309 , @obviyus and @jmar5439 .

> Telegram send failures. A reply blocked locally by unresolved account ownership shows an accurate failure notice instead of an uncertain-delivery warning. The ownership setting may still need correction; see the channel ownership guide . #167470 , thanks to @obviyus and @mgr-nick .

> Feishu streaming. Outdated waiting previews can be skipped so the completed answer does not wait behind every earlier version. #166610 , thanks to @rudycelekli , @feie22 , @cas1per , @L-Trump , @xiaobao-k8s and @csxjihk .

> Long Claude CLI replies. Fixed missing final reports from completed Claude CLI tasks with many streaming updates. #153545 , thanks to @zhangguiping-xydt , @methylester , @obviyus , @VACInc , @aaajiao , @telbus , @aoclaw-glitch , @aikohoshinoai-prog and @RTapiador .

> Claude CLI history. Imported history avoids duplicating your messages and forwarded input, and preserves who sent them. Recognized background instructions no longer look like messages you wrote, while large tool results appear as tool activity. The updated CLI guide explains these display changes and confirms that stored transcripts remain intact. #163521 , #166080 , #168851 , thanks to @rogeriochaves , @llmsommelier , @RomneyDa , @Marvinthebored , @Peetiegonzalez , @IndySweeney , @agierror and @Heighte .

> Codex session startup. Closing idle threads with background terminals no longer blocks later turns. Stalled startup waits can be cancelled and respect their request budget. #166693 , thanks to @jalehman .

> Codex provider errors. A provider failure after tool work remains visible with recovery advice instead of looking merely stopped. Completed actions are not repeated, and an explicit Stop still means cancellation. #165488 , thanks to @steipete .

> Custom Bedrock routes. Requests using a custom Bedrock provider ID no longer fail before contacting AWS, and use that route's configured region. See the Bedrock guide and custom-provider setup . #164406 , thanks to @Ayushdevo , @obviyus and @kai-ocean-kurt .

> Credential masking. Built-in masking no longer misses recognized credentials split across long tool output. Long or repetitive text also avoids the reported masking freezes and stack overflows, with less processing work under the existing rules. #161480 , #166701 , thanks to @eleqtrizit and @steipete .

> Configuration errors. When a helper cannot launch, configuration checks report the actual launch error and affected file instead of an unrelated trim error. #165373 , thanks to @steipete and @nnljl2003 .

> Earlier images. With an image decoder available, corrupt images are omitted from requests so text follow-ups and reopened conversations can continue. Missing older images also receive a neutral note instead of instructions to deny the assistant's earlier inspection. #166564 , #166165 , thanks to @jayzhou2309 , @steipete , @mirasrael and @nogikeyaksh .

> Stopped node workers. Force-stopping a node worker can stop surviving child processes and free its slot for later work once cleanup is verified. See background-process guidance . #168746 , thanks to @steipete , @aniruddhaadak80 and @linhongyu510 .
