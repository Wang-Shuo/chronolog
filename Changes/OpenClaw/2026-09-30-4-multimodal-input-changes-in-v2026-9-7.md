# 4 Multimodal Input changes in v2026.9.7

**OpenClaw** · 2026-09-30 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · Digest · [Official source](https://docs.openclaw.ai/releases/2026.9.7) · from [[Releases/OpenClaw/openclaw-2026-09-30-v2026-9-7-3d25d2bd|v2026.9.7]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> On Android, Request all lets you review optional permissions together and finish setup even if you deny SMS access. Camera and location still have separate on/off switches, and notification-listener access needs a separate Settings step. SMS requires a supported third-party build, a phone-capable device, and Gateway approval. Google Play builds do not support SMS.

> Translation records for 20 existing languages include current settings, camera, and chat guidance, including messages for recovering unsaved drafts. No new languages are added.

> Image, video, and music requests can run in the background again in chats that share the agent's main conversation history. Each chat keeps its own media tasks and status, and results return to the person who asked.

> Sora and the bundled custom OpenAI-compatible video connection have been removed following Sora's retirement. Replace saved openai/sora-2 or openai/sora-2-pro selections with another configured provider. They are not changed automatically and can keep failing.

Source: https://docs.openclaw.ai/releases/2026.9.7
