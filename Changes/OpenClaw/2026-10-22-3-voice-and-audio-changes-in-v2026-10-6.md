# 3 Voice and Audio changes in v2026.10.6

**OpenClaw** · 2026-10-22 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Digest · [Official source](https://docs.openclaw.ai/releases/2026.10.6) · from [[Releases/OpenClaw/openclaw-2026-10-22-v2026-10-6-56379f53|v2026.10.6]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> When a voice note fails to send, the text fallback now arrives before the next reply instead of after it.

> When you leave a Discord voice call, a reply that was already being written can still finish in the voice room’s text chat.

> Talk now recognizes provider names regardless of capitalization or extra spaces, and keeps the voice you selected.
