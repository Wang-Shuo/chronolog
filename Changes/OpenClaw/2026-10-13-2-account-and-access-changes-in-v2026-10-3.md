# 2 Account and Access changes in v2026.10.3

**OpenClaw** · 2026-10-13 · [[Themes/account-access|Account and Access]] · Digest · [Official source](https://docs.openclaw.ai/releases/2026.10.3) · from [[Releases/OpenClaw/openclaw-2026-10-13-v2026-10-3-54cedb60|v2026.10.3]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> MiniMax sign-in renewal. Global and CN OAuth sign-ins renew expired tokens at their issuing regional account endpoint. Older saved sign-ins and region changes require one new login with openclaw models auth login --provider minimax-portal . See the MiniMax guide . #160459 , thanks to @masatohoshino , @RomneyDa , @rongtianhua and @mychataa .

> Saved sign-ins during Doctor repairs. An unreadable legacy auth file no longer causes the affected cleanup to discard saved OAuth profiles. Doctor identifies the file to inspect or restore before retrying, while unrelated repairs can continue. Previously deleted profiles and retired credential imports are not restored. See Doctor state recovery guidance . #170256 , thanks to @steipete and @nosmile99 .
