# 2 Apps and Integrations changes in HUB v2.28.2 — Partner plugins…

**Google Antigravity** · 2026-10-29 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Digest · [Official source](https://antigravity.google/releases?tab=hub&version=2.28.2) · from [[Releases/Google Antigravity/google-antigravity-2026-10-29-rel-hub-2-28-2-cea25f26|HUB v2.28.2 — Partner plugins in the Marketplace and plugin sign-in]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> The Marketplace has a new Featured spotlight that highlights plugins such as Firebase, Notion, Linear, and Stripe.

> When a plugin would help with your task, the agent can now find it in the Marketplace and suggest it in the conversation with a card for adding it. This isn't available yet for business and enterprise accounts.
