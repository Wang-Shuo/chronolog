# Plugins that connect to an account now have an Accounts tab on their page, where you…

**Google Antigravity** · 2026-10-29 · [[Themes/account-access|Account and Access]] · [Official source](https://antigravity.google/releases?tab=hub&version=2.28.2) · from [[Releases/Google Antigravity/google-antigravity-2026-10-29-rel-hub-2-28-2-cea25f26|HUB v2.28.2 — Partner plugins in the Marketplace and plugin sign-in]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Plugins that connect to an account now have an Accounts tab on their page, where you can add, reconnect, or disconnect accounts. Adding one of these plugins from a shelf, the Featured spotlight, or its page takes you straight to its sign-in. This isn't available yet for business and enterprise accounts.
