# Midjourney V7 image generation is now ~40% faster!

**Midjourney** · 2025-05-29 · [[Themes/image-generation|Image Generation]] · Launch · [Official source](https://updates.midjourney.com/faster-v7-ideas-ranking-party-and-better-moderation/) · from [[Releases/Midjourney/midjourney-2025-05-29-https-updates-midjourney-com-faster-v7-ideas-ranking-party-and-b-f00e9838|Faster V7, ideas ranking party, and better moderation]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Fast mode jobs render-time are reduced from 36 to 22 seconds
>
> Turbo job render-time are reduced from 13 seconds to 9
>
> This is part of our ‘final optimization’ pass before making V7 default for the whole community and are necessary to prevent us from running out of GPUs.
>
> As always, if you want to make your model ‘take more time’ try –q 2 and –q 4 (more updates will be coming in the future to these settings.
>
> Note: Omni-reference jobs remain the same. Non-default settings may take longer (as before).
>
> Source: https://updates.midjourney.com/faster-v7-ideas-ranking-party-and-better-moderation/
