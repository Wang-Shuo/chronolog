<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> A big visual refresh. The feed is now full-width masonry, and prompts and buttons appear on hover instead of taking up space under every image. Videos play on hover again. There's a view menu on project feeds (select Grid or List), an in-place Organize mode, and Archive now opens your full library across all projects. If you generate images while on another page, a floating "Images ready" pill appears next to Create.
