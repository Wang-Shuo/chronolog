<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> ORGANIZE Dragging images to a folder works with the sidebar collapsed. Folder links survive a rename. Adding to a moodboard by link works for external sites. Organize is top-level, and hovering a collapsed icon opens a flyout. Change Style now matches the Styles panel.
>
> POLISH & BUGS Animate now works (sorry for breaking it). Things should feel snappier. Grids and profiles fill the width. Escape closes just the menu, not the whole image. PageUp/Down and Home/End scroll the feed while the search drawer is open.
>
> UP NEXT Setting default parameters. Previewing an sref with your prompt. Archive keeps your search when you open an image and go back. Scratchpad reachable from Archive. Drag-and-drop targets that make more sense.

> Thank you for being part of our community :heart:
