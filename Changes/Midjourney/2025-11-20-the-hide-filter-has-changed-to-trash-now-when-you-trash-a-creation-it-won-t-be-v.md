# The “Hide” filter has changed to “Trash”

**Midjourney** · 2025-11-20 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · [Official source](https://updates.midjourney.com/web-updates-3/) · from [[Releases/Midjourney/midjourney-2025-11-20-https-updates-midjourney-com-web-updates-3-d5daff6c|Web Updates]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> The “Hide” filter has changed to “Trash”. Now, when you trash a creation, it won’t be visible on your “Create” or “Organize” pages, and it also won’t show up in the “Archive” tab of your profile. You can find your trashed creations in the “In Trash” filter on your Organize page. Others might still find them on the Explore page, in ranking, or see them if they have the job ID or creation URL. For more information, see our documentation about trash here !
>
> Source: https://updates.midjourney.com/web-updates-3/
