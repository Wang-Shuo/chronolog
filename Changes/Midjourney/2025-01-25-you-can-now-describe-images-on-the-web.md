# "describe" images on the web

**Midjourney** · 2025-01-25 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Launch · [Official source](https://updates.midjourney.com/describe-on-web-moodboard-blending/) · from [[Releases/Midjourney/midjourney-2025-01-25-https-updates-midjourney-com-describe-on-web-moodboard-blending-5cf298a4|Describe on Web + Moodboard Blending]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> If you can't find the right words to turn an image into a prompt, you can now use the describe feature on the web! To use it, right click on any image and select "Describe," or drag an image from your desktop to the describe zone. You'll see four text prompts generated from the image at the top of your Create page. To keep your creation feed clean, these prompts will disappear once you refresh the page.
>
> Source: https://updates.midjourney.com/describe-on-web-moodboard-blending/
