# Other Tips and Tricks

**Midjourney** · 2026-03-17 · [[Themes/memory|Memory and Personalization]] · Launch · [Official source](https://updates.midjourney.com/v8-alpha/) · from [[Releases/Midjourney/midjourney-2026-03-17-https-updates-midjourney-com-v8-alpha-8527fed9|V8 Alpha]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> If you’re going for photos or a more ‘plain’ or ‘in control’ look we’d recommend switching to --raw right away or using moodboards or srefs to control your style. We’re still working on the ‘default’ aesthetics of V8, but we strongly encourage you to use your personalization and even crank it to –stylize 1000 asap. V8 is going to shine most right now when you rely heavily on our stylization systems and when you trend towards longer more specific prompting.
>
