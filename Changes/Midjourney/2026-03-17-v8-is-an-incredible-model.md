# V8 is an incredible model

**Midjourney** · 2026-03-17 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://updates.midjourney.com/v8-alpha/) · from [[Releases/Midjourney/midjourney-2026-03-17-https-updates-midjourney-com-v8-alpha-8527fed9|V8 Alpha]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> It’s much better at following detailed directions and will still surprise you when you ask. Its ability to understand your aesthetics through personalization, style references (srefs) and moodboards is amazing. Images are more coherent and detailed. Text rendering works better than ever (when specified in “quotes”). And image generation is ~5x faster than before.
>
> We’ve upgraded our web interfaces to support this speed. And more is coming. We have an improved conversation mode so you can just ‘talk’ in flow, a “Grid Mode” so you can focus on a single big set of images, and we’ve moved our settings into ‘sidebars’ so you can tweak settings without blocking your view.
>
> V8 launches with support for multiple aspect ratios, --chaos , --weird , --exp , --raw . We support backwards compatibility with your V7 personalization profiles, moodboards, and srefs.
>
> V8 brings a new --hd mode which natively renders images at 2K resolution and a --q 4 mode if you need a little bit of extra coherence.
>
> Source: https://updates.midjourney.com/v8-alpha/
