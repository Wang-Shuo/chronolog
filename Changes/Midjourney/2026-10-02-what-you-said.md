<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Now, sending a previewed style keeps your prompt-bar settings. Prompt parameters are saved per folder, within a tab's session. If you have 100K+ images, opening one deep in your history used to crash the site. Not anymore! We made scrolling/using arrow keys to scroll through images on the lightbox a lot clearer and easier. Filters clear when you switch between Organize and Create. Mac right click on create feed images works the old way. You can do sidebar style previews with profiles too – images coming next.
