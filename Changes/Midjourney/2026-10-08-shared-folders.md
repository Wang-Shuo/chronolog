# SHARED FOLDERS

**Midjourney** · 2026-10-08 · [[Themes/collaboration|Collaboration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://updates.midjourney.com/alpha-changelog-10-7-26/) · from [[Releases/Midjourney/midjourney-2026-10-08-https-updates-midjourney-com-alpha-changelog-10-7-26-cf436888|Alpha Changelog - 10/7/26]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’ve launched better ways for you to create together - folders can now be shared! Invite people by username as Collaborators or Viewers. Collaborators can generate right into the folder and build on each other's work. Viewers can browse the images within the folder. You can also share a folder by link. Keep it private to the people you add, let anyone with the link view it, or open it up so any Midjourney member can contribute. The owner can delete a shared folder for everyone. The images stay in each creator's own history.
>
> One heads-up: sharing doesn't change image privacy. Non-stealth images can still appear on Explore and on profiles, even if you made them inside a private folder. This is also an early version, and we'd love to hear your feedback.
>
> Source: https://updates.midjourney.com/alpha-changelog-10-7-26/
