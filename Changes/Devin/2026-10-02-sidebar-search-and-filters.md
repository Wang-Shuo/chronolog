**Devin** · 2026-10-02 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.devin.ai/release-notes/2026) · from [[Releases/Devin/devin-2026-10-02-2026-10-02-3eeb4015|Devin updates — 2026-10-02]]
