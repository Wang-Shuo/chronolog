# You'll receive warnings if Devin is about to sleep

**Devin** · 2024-11-01 · [[Themes/collaboration|Collaboration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.devin.ai/release-notes/2024) · from [[Releases/Devin/devin-2024-11-01-2024-11-01-948ae570|Devin updates — 2024-11-01]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Previously, users on our Personal and Team plans might've noticed that Devin may sleep unexpectedly.
>
> This is now fixed, and if Devin is about to sleep because it's low on ACUs or close to per-session ACU limits (which reset with each new instruction and can be configured in [Settings > Usage](https://app.devin.ai/settings/usage)), you'll receive a toast notification in the web app!
>
> Source: https://docs.devin.ai/release-notes/2024
