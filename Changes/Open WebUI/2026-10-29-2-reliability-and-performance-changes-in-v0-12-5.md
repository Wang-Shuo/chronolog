## Changes in this digest

> 🔄 **General improvements.** Various improvements were implemented across the application to enhance performance, stability, and security.

> 🎨 **ComfyUI failures reported.** A ComfyUI workflow that fails, for example when the GPU runs out of memory, now shows an error and tells the model the image could not be made, where it was reported as a successful generation with no images. [#33237](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/pull/33237), [#33236](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/issues/33236), [Commit](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/commit/d1ae9e75e00ab4401454a76c8e782dca193ff3e2)
