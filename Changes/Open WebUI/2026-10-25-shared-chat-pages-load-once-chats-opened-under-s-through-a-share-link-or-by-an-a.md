<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> 🔗 **Shared chat pages load once.** Chats opened under /s/, through a share link or by an administrator viewing another user's chat, no longer reload over and over and jump back to the bottom, which since 0.12.3 made scrolling up impossible.
