# 2 Apps and Integrations changes in v0.12.5

**Open WebUI** · 2026-10-29 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Digest · [Official source](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/releases/tag/v0.12.5) · from [[Releases/Open WebUI/open-webui-2026-10-29-v0-12-5-github-427647877-d6957e2e|v0.12.5]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> 🎚️ **Slider for number Valves.** Plugin authors can now show a number Valve or UserValve as a slider with a number box beside it, whose ends come from the field's minimum and maximum, so users see the allowed range while picking a value. [#33179](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/pull/33179), [#32045](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/discussions/32045), [Commit](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/commit/9ed317dc90c7a7fda96a6de68491648341d27ca2)

> 🔗 **MCP failures keep the chat.** A tool call to an MCP server that cannot be reached, for example after a restart or a dropped connection, now fails on its own and the reply still finishes, where the whole chat stopped as if you had pressed stop. [#33272](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/pull/33272), [#33268](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/issues/33268), [Commit](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/commit/2c64ab5487bd76e87c0306f6295acd7c710445e7)
