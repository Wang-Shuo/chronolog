# The average review time for Bugbot is now ~90 seconds, down from ~5 minutes

**Cursor** · 2026-06-10 · [[Themes/reliability-performance|Reliability and Performance]] · [Official source](https://cursor.com/changelog/bugbot-updates-june-2026) · from [[Releases/Cursor/cursor-2026-06-10-bugbot-is-now-over-3x-faster-22-cheaper-and-finds-10-more-bugs-h-c1721c78|Bugbot is now over 3x faster, 22% cheaper, and finds 10% more bugs]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> The average review time for Bugbot is now ~90 seconds, down from ~5 minutes. Bugbot also finds 10% more bugs per review on average — 0.62, up from 0.56 — and costs ~22% less per run.
>
> These performance gains are made possible by progress we've made training Composer 2.5, which now powers Bugbot. Bugbot respects model block lists, and speed and performance can vary depending on your configuration.
>
> Source: https://cursor.com/changelog/bugbot-updates-june-2026
