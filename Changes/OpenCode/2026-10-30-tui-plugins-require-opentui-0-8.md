# TUI plugins require OpenTUI 0.8

**OpenCode** · 2026-10-30 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Launch · [Official source](https://opencode.ai/changelog/v2.2.3) · from [[Releases/OpenCode/opencode-2026-10-30-v2-2-3-0c0c59eb|v2.2.3]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> @opencode/plugin now requires @opentui/core and @opentui/solid 0.8.0 or later. In OpenTUI 0.8, a ScrollBox's wrapper , viewport , content , and scrollbars are facade objects instead of renderables, and a custom RenderContext must implement clock and onAnimationTick . See the OpenTUI 0.8.0 release notes .
>
> Source: https://opencode.ai/changelog/v2.2.3
