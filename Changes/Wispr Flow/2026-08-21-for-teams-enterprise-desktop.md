# For Teams & Enterprise (Desktop)

**Wispr Flow** · 2026-08-21 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://wisprflow.ai/whats-new#august-21-2026) · from [[Releases/Wispr Flow/wispr-flow-2026-08-21-2026-08-21-07917b7e|Wispr Flow updates — 2026-08-21]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Daily teammates added digest: Admins now get a daily email summarizing every teammate who joined the workspace in the last 24 hours, whether they came in through domain auto-add, SCIM, or an invite link, so seat growth (and the bill) never comes as a surprise. Admins who don't want it can turn it off from the admin portal.
>
> Team trials for every joiner: Anyone joining a team for the first time now receives a 14-day trial, even if the team is already on a paid plan. New team members will not be billed until the first 2 weeks are complete.
>
> Help Center: FAQs for Flow Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans
>
> Source: https://wisprflow.ai/whats-new#august-21-2026
