# Wispr Flow Notetaker is now live on Windows (Notetaker)

**Wispr Flow** · 2026-09-15 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Expansion · [Official source](https://wisprflow.ai/whats-new#september-15-2026-wispr-flow-notetaker-is-now-live-on-windows) · from [[Releases/Wispr Flow/wispr-flow-2026-09-15-2026-09-15-6e6a8d9f|Wispr Flow updates — 2026-09-15]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Notetaker is now available on Windows, alongside Mac. Here’s what it does and what we’ve added since the Mac launch.
>
> Capture the details, then work from them
>
> Notetaker detects meetings across the platforms you already use and offers to take notes. It uses your personal dictionary to help capture names, acronyms, and technical terms correctly so you can:
>
> Catch up during a call: Ask “What did I miss?” for a recap of the discussion so far.
>
> Find answers across meetings: Ask “What follow-ups do I owe people from my calls this week?” or “Based on the calls I’ve led this month, how could I ask better questions?” Answers include citations back to the meetings.
>
> Draft from your notes: Turn conversations into follow-up emails, project updates, or briefs directly in Notetaker.
>
> Find it: Open the Notetaker tab in Wispr Flow.
>
> Connect your meetings to your other work
>
> Notetaker just added one-click setup for Gemini, alongside connections to Claude and ChatGPT. You can also connect to any other MCP-compatible AI tool.
>
> Once connected, your AI tool can grab your meeting notes and transcripts along with any other files and tools you’ve connected there. You’ll be able to dictate complex prompts:
>
> For sales: “Compare the objections from my discovery calls this quarter with our objection-handling doc. Where are we not answering well?”
>
> For founders: “Draft this month’s investor update from the board call, my team syncs, and the metrics in this sheet.”
>
> And your AI tool can pull the meeting context directly from Notetaker, no copy + paste required.
>
> The MCP connection only grants access to meeting transcripts, not your dictations.
>
> More updates since the Mac launch
>
> Meeting transcription in 21 languages: Including Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Hindi, and Arabic. Summaries and other features can also work in other languages, but the experience may be unreliable. See all supported languages .
>
> Outlook calendar: Connect Outlook to see upcoming meetings in Notetaker and bring in calendar context. Google Calendar is also supported.
>
> Import from Otter : Bring your previous transcripts into Notetaker with speaker names preserved, so you can search old and new meetings together. Otter transcript imports require a paid Otter plan. Granola imports are also supported.
>
> Controls for your organization
>
> Admins can manage Notetaker access, sharing, and privacy restrictions across their organization.
>
> Notetaker captures audio from your computer, so no bot joins the meeting. Always inform participants before recording. Organizations can also require users to confirm they’ve obtained consent before recording starts.
>
> Find it: Organization settings in the admin portal. See Notetaker admin controls .
>
> Source: https://wisprflow.ai/whats-new#september-15-2026-wispr-flow-notetaker-is-now-live-on-windows
