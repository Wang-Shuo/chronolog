# Mention people in your meeting notes (Notetaker)

**Wispr Flow** · 2026-10-09 · [[Themes/meeting-notes|Meeting Notes]] · Launch · [Official source](https://wisprflow.ai/whats-new#wispr-changelog-2026-10-05) · from [[Releases/Wispr Flow/wispr-flow-2026-10-09-2026-10-09-58884c93|Wispr Flow updates — 2026-10-09]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Type @ in My Thoughts or Summary to open a people picker. It lists the people in the meeting first, then your contacts, and gives you the option to add a new contact, so you can also mention someone who wasn't in the meeting. A mention stays in that meeting's notes: it doesn't notify the person, share anything with them or add them as an attendee. Hover a mention to see that person's contact card, and click it if you need to point the mention at a different contact.
>
