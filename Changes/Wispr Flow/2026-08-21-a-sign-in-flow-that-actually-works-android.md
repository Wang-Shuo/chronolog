# A Sign-In Flow That Actually Works (Android)

**Wispr Flow** · 2026-08-21 · [[Themes/account-access|Account and Access]] · Launch · [Official source](https://wisprflow.ai/whats-new#august-21-2026) · from [[Releases/Wispr Flow/wispr-flow-2026-08-21-2026-08-21-07917b7e|Wispr Flow updates — 2026-08-21]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Signing in with Google on Android now happens in a secure in-app browser tab that returns you straight to Wispr Flow. No more landing on a random browser tab, an old page, or the Wispr Flow website after picking your account. If your sign-in gets interrupted, a resume prompt lets you pick up right where you left off.
>
> Find it: automatic, the sign-in screen the first time you open Flow or sign back in.
