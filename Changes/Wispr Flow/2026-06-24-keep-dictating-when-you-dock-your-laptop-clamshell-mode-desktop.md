# Keep dictating when you dock your laptop (clamshell mode) (Desktop)

**Wispr Flow** · 2026-06-24 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Launch · [Official source](https://wisprflow.ai/whats-new#june-24-2026) · from [[Releases/Wispr Flow/wispr-flow-2026-06-24-2026-06-24-3a83c77f|Wispr Flow updates — 2026-06-24]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Close your laptop lid and Flow switches to your highest-ranked external mic on its own instead of interrupting you with a notification, then returns to your built-in mic when you open it again. Reordering mics in settings now applies instantly, with no need to restart recording. Find it: Settings > General > Microphone
