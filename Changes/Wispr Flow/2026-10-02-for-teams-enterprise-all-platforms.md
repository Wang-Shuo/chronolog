# For Teams & Enterprise: (All platforms)

**Wispr Flow** · 2026-10-02 · [[Themes/safety-privacy|Safety and Privacy]] · Launch · [Official source](https://wisprflow.ai/whats-new#oct-2-teams-and-ent-changes) · from [[Releases/Wispr Flow/wispr-flow-2026-10-02-2026-10-02-3eeb4015|Wispr Flow updates — 2026-10-02]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Model training stays off for Growth and Enterprise plans: If your organization is on a Growth or Enterprise plan, model training is now off for every member. It’s tied to your plan rather than an individual setting, so members can’t turn it back on, and sensitive fields are removed from members’ history uploads on our servers. You don’t need to do anything. Find it: Settings → Privacy in the desktop app shows this choice as controlled by your organization.
>
> Audit Logs capture more membership and domain changes: Enterprise admins can now see role changes, invitations, revoked invitations, and domain additions and removals in Audit Logs. Member added and removed events are also recorded across more ways people join or leave, including SCIM provisioning, auto-add, invite links, join requests and disbanded organizations.
>
