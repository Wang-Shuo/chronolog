# Wispr Flow Notetaker is here (Notetaker)

**Wispr Flow** · 2026-08-05 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://wisprflow.ai/whats-new#august-5-2026-wispr-flow-notetaker-is-live) · from [[Releases/Wispr Flow/wispr-flow-2026-08-05-2026-08-05-c50437f6|Wispr Flow updates — 2026-08-05]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Today we're launching Wispr Flow Notetaker, our first product beyond dictation. It records your meetings, gets your words, names, and speakers right, and turns the conversation into transcripts and summaries you can act on. No bot joins your call, and it works on every meeting platform.
>
> Where the accuracy comes from. Most notetakers hear a meeting cold. Notetaker walks in with context you've already built:
>
> The personal dictionary you've trained in Flow carries over, so your acronyms, product names, and industry terms come out spelled right.
>
> Speaker names come from the calendar invite, so "Biswaroop" doesn't become "Bishop" and the action item lands on the right person.
>
> You get a live transcript during the meeting. When it ends, Notetaker re-reads the entire recording with that context, which is where speaker names get attached and everything comes out sharper.
>
> Accuracy matters more now that meeting notes feed your other tools. Everything Notetaker produces is available over MCP to Claude, ChatGPT, or your own agents, and a wrong name in the transcript doesn't get caught downstream. It just travels.
>
> What it does:
>
> It works wherever your meetings happen: Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, even Slack huddles. It runs on your device and captures conversations that were never on your calendar with one tap.
>
> Summaries are organized by topic, with the decisions and who owes what.
>
> You can ask questions across your entire meeting history, with answers linked back to the exact moment in the transcript.
>
> If your attention drifts mid-meeting, it catches you up on what you missed.
>
> It's included in Wispr Flow as part of your existing plan: speaker labels, ask-anything, and MCP access. No credit card, no commitments. Pro just keeps more meetings for longer.
>
> Available now on Mac, with Windows on the way. If you see "coming soon" in the app, connect your calendar and Slack to unlock access instantly.
>
> Audio Playback in Dictation History
>
> Transcript look off? You can now play back the original audio from your History to check whether the problem was the audio itself (like a muffled mic) and fix your setup instead of guessing. Hover over any row from the last 14 days and hit play. It picks up where you left off if you pause, and switching to another row stops the first one automatically.
>
> Audio playback is available for dictations from the last 14 days, on both Mac and Windows.
>
