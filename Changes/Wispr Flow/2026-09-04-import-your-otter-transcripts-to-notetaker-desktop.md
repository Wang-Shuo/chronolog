# Import Your Otter Transcripts to Notetaker (Desktop)

**Wispr Flow** · 2026-09-04 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Launch · [Official source](https://wisprflow.ai/whats-new#september-4-2026) · from [[Releases/Wispr Flow/wispr-flow-2026-09-04-2026-09-04-ccf2eec3|Wispr Flow updates — 2026-09-04]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now bring your Otter.ai meeting transcripts straight into Wispr Flow. Just drop in your Otter export and your meetings appear alongside everything else, with speaker names preserved instead of collapsed into a single anonymous voice. Imports run in the background, so you can keep working while your history fills in.
>
> Find it: Settings > Notetaker > Import, or the import picker where you'll see the Otter tile alongside Granola.
>
> [Help Center: Import your Otter transcripts into Wispr Flow Notetaker ]
>
> Source: https://wisprflow.ai/whats-new#september-4-2026
