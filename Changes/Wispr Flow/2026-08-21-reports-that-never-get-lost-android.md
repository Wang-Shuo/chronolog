# Reports That Never Get Lost (Android)

**Wispr Flow** · 2026-08-21 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Launch · [Official source](https://wisprflow.ai/whats-new#august-21-2026) · from [[Releases/Wispr Flow/wispr-flow-2026-08-21-2026-08-21-07917b7e|Wispr Flow updates — 2026-08-21]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Issue reports, feedback, and transcription reports now keep sending even if you leave the screen. You'll get a confirmation wherever you are in the app, and the form clearly shows when it's busy: a spinner, a dimmed Send button, and a short note explaining why it's temporarily locked.
>
> Find it: Dashboard menu > Report an issue or Share feedback, and Home > any transcript > Report.
