# Bring Your Own Cloud to Replit

**Replit** · 2026-10-16 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.replit.com/updates/2026/10/16/changelog) · from [[Releases/Replit/replit-2026-10-16-2026-10-16-3443a38d|Replit updates — 2026-10-16]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Bring Replit to your enterprise cloud environment for greater control over where your platform and apps run. Choose how you build and run with Replit in your own cloud:
>
> **Full BYOC (private preview):** Run the full Replit platform in your cloud tenant.
>
> **BYOC for Published Apps (early access):** Host published Replit apps in your cloud tenant.
>
> **Image Exports (early access):** Export and self-host Replit-built apps.
>
> Explore the [BYOC documentation](https://docs.replit.com/teams/bring-your-own-cloud.md) or read the [announcement](https://replit.com/blog/bring-your-own-cloud).
>
