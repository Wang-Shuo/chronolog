**Replit** · 2025-11-21 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.replit.com/updates/2025/11/21/changelog) · from [[Releases/Replit/replit-2025-11-21-2025-11-21-d3d6b6ca|Replit updates — 2025-11-21]]

> Design Mode is now live. Powered by Google's Gemini 3, Design Mode creates beautiful interactive designs and static websites in under 2 minutes—faster and better-looking than ever before on Replit.
