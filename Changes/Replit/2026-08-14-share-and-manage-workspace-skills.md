# Share and manage workspace skills

**Replit** · 2026-08-14 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.replit.com/updates/2026/08/14/changelog) · from [[Releases/Replit/replit-2026-08-14-2026-08-14-e5550355|Replit updates — 2026-08-14]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Any workspace member can now create skills and keep them private or share them with the workspace. Shared skills start turned off for other members, and each person chooses which ones to enable.
