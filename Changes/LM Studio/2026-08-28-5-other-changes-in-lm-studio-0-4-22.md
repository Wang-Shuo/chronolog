## Changes in this digest

> Support for DFlash, DSpark, and MTP assistant drafters

> Requires llama.cpp engine version 2.29.1 or newer

> Added support for images returned by tools in OpenAI-compatible `/v1/responses` and `/v1/chat/completions`, and Anthropic-compatible `/v1/messages`

> [Linux] Enabled in-app updates for AppImage and `.deb` installations

> [Linux] Improved recovery after canceling `.deb` update authorization
