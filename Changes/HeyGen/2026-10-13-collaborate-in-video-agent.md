# Collaborate in Video Agent

**HeyGen** · 2026-10-13 · [[Themes/agent-mode|Agent Mode]] · Launch · [Official source](https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/october-2026-enterprise-web-releases) · from [[Releases/HeyGen/heygen-2026-10-13-https-heygen-noticeable-news-publications-october-2026-enterpris-4784ae14|October 2026 Enterprise Web Releases]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Teams can now work on one Video Agent session together.
>
> Collaborate . The session header has a Collaborate button that opens the same sharing window you know from AI Studio. Add teammates by name or email, or share with the whole workspace. Everyone you add can view and edit the session.
>
> Editors can drive the session . Anyone you add can send prompts and keep building the video with you.
>
> One editor at a time . If someone else is editing, or you have the session open in another tab, you'll see a notice and the editing controls pause, so no one overwrites anyone else. Select Edit here to take over.
>
> Copy link . Anyone with access to the session can copy its link.
>
> Share finished videos . To show a finished video to someone without giving them the session, share that video with its own link.
>
> Source: https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/october-2026-enterprise-web-releases
