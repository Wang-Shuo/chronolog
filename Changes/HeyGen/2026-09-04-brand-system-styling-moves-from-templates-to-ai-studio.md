# Brand System styling moves from Templates to AI Studio

**HeyGen** · 2026-09-04 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · Launch · [Official source](https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/september-2026-enterprise-releases) · from [[Releases/HeyGen/heygen-2026-09-04-https-heygen-noticeable-news-publications-september-2026-enterpr-39d060a6|September 2026 Enterprise Releases]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Brand styling now lives in AI Studio instead of the template list. Rather than selecting a Brand System on the templates page to pre-style previews, open a template in AI Studio, choose a Brand System from the Brand panel, and select Apply to full video.
>
> Colors, fonts, and logos will be applied across the entire video in one step, and a single undo will revert the changes. You can still fine-tune the video afterward by selecting an individual element. Its Brand section will provide access to the colors, typography roles, and logo assets from the same Brand System.
>
> Source: https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/september-2026-enterprise-releases
