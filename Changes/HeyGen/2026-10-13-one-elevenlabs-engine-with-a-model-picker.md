<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> ElevenLabs is now a single engine. Pick the model underneath it: Eleven Multilingual v2 , Eleven Turbo v2 , ElevenLabs v3 , or the new ElevenLabs v4 . The controls change with the model. v2 models have similarity, stability, style and speaker boost, and v3 and v4 have a three-step stability setting. Voices and scenes you saved on ElevenLabs V3 open with the v3 model selected and sound the same as before.
