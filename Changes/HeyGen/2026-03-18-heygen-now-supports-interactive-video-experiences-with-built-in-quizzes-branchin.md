# HeyGen now supports interactive video experiences with built-in quizzes, branching…

**HeyGen** · 2026-03-18 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · [Official source](https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/introducing-interactive-video-experiences) · from [[Releases/HeyGen/heygen-2026-03-18-https-heygen-noticeable-news-publications-introducing-interactiv-9592428d|Introducing interactive video experiences]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> HeyGen now supports interactive video experiences with built-in quizzes, branching, and external resources. Add in-video quizzes to check knowledge in real time, create branching paths so viewers can navigate content based on their choices, and include CTA buttons to guide them to external links or specific moments in the video.
>
> When you’re done, export your interactive video as a SCORM file and upload it directly into your LMS.
>
> Source: https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/introducing-interactive-video-experiences
