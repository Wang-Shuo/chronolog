# 5 Enterprise and Administration changes in July 2026 Enterprise Releases

**HeyGen** · 2026-07-08 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Digest · [Official source](https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/july-2026-enterprise-releases) · from [[Releases/HeyGen/heygen-2026-07-08-https-heygen-noticeable-news-publications-july-2026-enterprise-r-27a9b7ed|July 2026 Enterprise Releases]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> The enterprise audit log now shows clean, human-readable activity names — like "Changed role for [email protected] ," "Invited team member(s)," or "Updated workspace MFA settings" — instead of raw API paths.

> Admins can narrow the log using several filters: filter by event type to isolate specific actions like role changes, SSO updates, or approval decisions via a multi-select dropdown; filter by team member to see all activity from one or more users; and filter by date range to scope the log to a specific window. Parent workspace admins automatically see activity rolled up from all subworkspaces in a single view, and the full log can be exported to CSV for reporting.

> The Usage & History experience has been upgraded for both parent workspace and sub-workspace admins, with richer reporting and analytics that give clearer visibility into how resources are being consumed across the organization.

> Parent workspace admins get a new resource consumption graph that lets them compare usage across all sub-workspaces at a glance. Sub-workspace admins get detailed usage analytics within their own workspace, including breakdowns by member and by feature.

> The API, Agentic Skills, and Connections tabs have moved out of the enterprise settings modal and into the dedicated Developers section in the home sidebar — keeping settings focused and giving developers a single, consistent place to find everything they need.

Source: https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/july-2026-enterprise-releases
