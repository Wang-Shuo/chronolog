# Brand Glossary, rebuilt

**HeyGen** · 2026-10-13 · [[Themes/collaboration|Collaboration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/october-2026-enterprise-web-releases) · from [[Releases/HeyGen/heygen-2026-10-13-https-heygen-noticeable-news-publications-october-2026-enterpris-4784ae14|October 2026 Enterprise Web Releases]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> The Brand Glossary has been rebuilt for teams that manage hundreds of terms across languages.
>
> Language-aware terms . Each term can hold its own pronunciation and translation rule for every language. Videos made with your brand glossary use the right one for the video's language automatically.
>
> Organized by term . An A to Z list with filters for rule type and language, so you can find any term quickly.
>
> Pronunciations you can hear . Type a pronunciation, or record it with your microphone, then select Hear it in a phrase to listen with a HeyGen voice and engine before you save.
>
> Translations in one place . Set "don't translate" or "always translate as" for each language, right next to the term.
>
> Spreadsheet import and export . Bring a glossary in or out as CSV or Excel. The whole file is checked first, and any row with a problem is clearly marked.
>
> Source: https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/october-2026-enterprise-web-releases
