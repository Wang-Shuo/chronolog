# Orca similarity and delivery presets

**HeyGen** · 2026-10-13 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/october-2026-enterprise-web-releases) · from [[Releases/HeyGen/heygen-2026-10-13-https-heygen-noticeable-news-publications-october-2026-enterpris-4784ae14|October 2026 Enterprise Web Releases]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Similarity: Medium, High or Max . Decide how closely an Orca voice matches the original speaker and how much it adapts to the scene. High is the default. Max is available for voices cloned from your own recordings.
>
> Direct Voice presets . Give a scene one of seven deliveries: Friendly, Confident, Excited, Calm, Warm, Brisk or Emphatic. Revert takes it back to the voice as designed.
