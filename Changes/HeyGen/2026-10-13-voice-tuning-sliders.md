# Voice-tuning sliders

**HeyGen** · 2026-10-13 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/october-2026-enterprise-web-releases) · from [[Releases/HeyGen/heygen-2026-10-13-https-heygen-noticeable-news-publications-october-2026-enterpris-4784ae14|October 2026 Enterprise Web Releases]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> The voice-tuning sliders are gone from Video Agent, and slider settings saved earlier are no longer applied. Narration now uses each voice's standard tuning, plus the delivery Video Agent directs for each line. Your saved settings aren't deleted.
>
> What to do: Nothing. If a video sounds different from before, you can change it in either place. In Video Agent, ask for the delivery you want, for example "calmer" or "more energetic". In AI Studio, adjust the voice settings for each scene in the Edit Voice panel.
>
> Source: https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/october-2026-enterprise-web-releases
