# Hyperframes in Video Agent

**HeyGen** · 2026-08-07 · [[Themes/agent-mode|Agent Mode]] · Launch · [Official source](https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/august-2026-enterprise-releases) · from [[Releases/HeyGen/heygen-2026-08-07-https-heygen-noticeable-news-publications-august-2026-enterprise-ee1eb69b|August 2026 Enterprise Releases]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> HyperFrames is now inside HeyGen's Video Agent. The Video Agent already made all the decisions: script, voice, presenter, pacing, captions, music, what to show, and when. One prompt, and it's done. That was already the impressive part. Now it builds the graphics too.
>
> HyperFrames, our motion graphics framework, runs in code, so it's fully customizable and legible to the agent. That means it builds any graphic you describe instead of picking one from a template. A chart growing over a photo. Words popping on beat. If you can describe it, it can build it. It also works with real media. Stock or its own generated video and photos, graphics built right on top.
>
