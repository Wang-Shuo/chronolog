<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> When uploading a PowerPoint file through the PowerPoint to Video flow, you'll now see a prompt directly in AI Studio at the top of the screen — "We found brand colors and fonts in your uploaded presentation" — with a quick Save brand option. Once you click this, a "We found your brand assets" modal will appear, giving you a head start on building your brand system directly from your presentation.
