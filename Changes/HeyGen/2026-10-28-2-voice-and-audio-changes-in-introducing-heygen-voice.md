## Changes in this digest

> Studio-quality output from casual recordings. A rough phone take comes back as a clean, polished read in your voice clone.

> More emotional range than text prompting. Not every voice responds to emotional direction written as text. With STS, the warmth, urgency, or energy in your recording carries over to your clone, including whispers, laughs, and accents.
