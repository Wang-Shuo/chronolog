# Build your own custom Look Pack

**HeyGen** · 2026-09-04 · [[Themes/search-browsing|Search and Browsing]] · Launch · [Official source](https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/september-2026-enterprise-releases) · from [[Releases/HeyGen/heygen-2026-09-04-https-heygen-noticeable-news-publications-september-2026-enterpr-39d060a6|September 2026 Enterprise Releases]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Previously, liking a single look inside a pack meant generating the entire pack. Custom Look Packs remove that constraint: browse all available looks and references across every pack, select the 3–12 you actually want, and generate a new look for each one — identity preserved across the set, delivered straight into your avatar group. You're no longer bound by preset packs; you build the pack you want, look by look.
>
