## Changes in this digest

> The new Organization tab provides admins with a centralized view of their entire workspace structure, making it easier to manage teams, members, and sub-workspaces from one place.

> View and manage all sub-workspaces from one place

> Create new sub-workspaces

> View members across the entire organization

> Filter members by workspace

> Improvements include:

> Ability to restrict sub-workspaces to their own allocated usage limits - note this is disabled by default! If you wish to prevent sub-workspaces from using parent space usage limits, you need to turn on this toggle.

> Why it matters: Organizations now have greater flexibility to meet branding, transparency, and governance requirements for AI-assisted content.
