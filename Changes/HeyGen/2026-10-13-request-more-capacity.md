**HeyGen** · 2026-10-13 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/october-2026-enterprise-web-releases) · from [[Releases/HeyGen/heygen-2026-10-13-https-heygen-noticeable-news-publications-october-2026-enterpris-4784ae14|October 2026 Enterprise Web Releases]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Instead of Contact Sales , admins now see Request more . Pick what you need (seats, video minutes or credits, or avatar slots), say how much, and add a note. Your HeyGen account team gets the request and follows up.
>
> You'll also see Request more wherever a limit shows up: when a video needs more minutes than you have left, and on Usage & History, Organization and Manage Seats.
