## Changes in this digest

> We've completely redesigned Settings to make it easier for users and admins to find what they're looking for. Settings are now organized into two distinct views: Personal Settings and Workspace Settings .

> Workspace Settings contains settings related to the workspace itself, giving users a clearer separation between managing their own experience and managing shared workspace resources. Admins will also see a new Workspace Controls section here for managing workspace-wide policies and permissions.

> Admins can now:

> We've expanded the Usage & History experience to help parent workspace admins better manage resources across their organization.

> Workspace Controls includes three sections:

> Access & Invitations – Centralizes controls related to workspace membership and access management. Admins can configure: invitations, public invite links, and sub-workspace access. This gives admins greater control over how users enter and move throughout their organization.

> Admins can upload a custom image, preview how it will appear, and automatically apply it to downloaded videos when watermarking is enabled.
