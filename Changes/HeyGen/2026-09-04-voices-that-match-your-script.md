# Voices that match your script

**HeyGen** · 2026-09-04 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/september-2026-enterprise-releases) · from [[Releases/HeyGen/heygen-2026-09-04-https-heygen-noticeable-news-publications-september-2026-enterpr-39d060a6|September 2026 Enterprise Releases]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We've simplified voice selection across AI Studio and the Voices page. The refreshed interface shows only the engines that support your script's language, removes chat, and collapses advanced settings, so choosing a voice takes fewer steps and fewer decisions. “Auto” has been retired in favor of explicit, language-matched selection.
>
> AI Studio now validates each scene's script language against the selected voice engine before you preview or generate. If the two don't match, Improve Voice routes the scene to a compatible engine, so every render sounds the way you intend.
>
> Source: https://heygen.noticeable.news/publications/september-2026-enterprise-releases
