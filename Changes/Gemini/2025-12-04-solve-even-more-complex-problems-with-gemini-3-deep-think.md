# Solve even more complex problems with Gemini 3 Deep Think

**Gemini** · 2025-12-04 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-12-04-2025-12-04-845b44b3|Gemini updates — 2025-12-04]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Starting today, Google AI Ultra subscribers get access to Gemini 3 Deep Think, Gemini’s most advanced reasoning mode. Deep Think uses iterative rounds of reasoning to explore multiple hypotheses simultaneously to solve problems, enabling it to excel at complex tasks including math, science and logic problems. Gemini 3 Deep Think mode pushes the boundaries of intelligence even further, delivering a meaningful improvement in reasoning capabilities. To access, select “Deep Think” in the prompt bar and ‘Thinking’ in the model dropdown. Once you submit your task, Gemini will let you know when your response is ready – generally in a few minutes.
>
> Why: We're committed to bringing Google's latest AI innovations faster to Google AI Ultra users, including our most powerful reasoning capabilities to help you tackle even more complex problems.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
