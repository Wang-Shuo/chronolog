# Introducing Gemini 1.5 Flash to Gemini

**Gemini** · 2024-07-25 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-07-25-2024-07-25-931f3bc2|Gemini updates — 2024-07-25]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Gemini now gives you access to 1.5 Flash, our model optimized for speed and efficiency. 1.5 Flash provides a 4x longer context window (from 8k tokens to 32k tokens) compared to 1.0 Pro and improved overall response quality and accuracy.
>
> Why: 1.5 Flash provides a longer context window and improved performance to unlock new ways to create, interact and collaborate with Gemini. A longer context window allows Gemini to process a larger amount of content for each prompt, helping you solve more complex problems and boosting your productivity when you explore and analyze information.
>
