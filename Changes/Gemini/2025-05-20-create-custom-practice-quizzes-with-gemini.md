# Create custom practice quizzes with Gemini

**Gemini** · 2025-05-20 · [[Themes/file-data|Files and Data]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-05-20-2025-05-20-3970ada9|Gemini updates — 2025-05-20]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Starting today, you can generate custom practice quizzes to help you prepare for an upcoming exam or simply increase your knowledge of any topic - big or small. Create quizzes based on documents you want to study, such as PDFs or class notes, or ask Gemini to create a quiz on a specific topic, and you’ll get a dynamic quiz experience, complete with hints, explanations for right and wrong answers, and a helpful summary at the end highlighting where you did well and where you may need to study a little harder. This experience is available to users over the age of 18 and to qualifying Google Workspace education plans.
>
> Why: With Gemini’s intuitive and streamlined practice quizzes, learners can lean on the advantages of generative AI to help them prepare: unlimited quiz generation, personalized responses, and conversational learning experiences.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
