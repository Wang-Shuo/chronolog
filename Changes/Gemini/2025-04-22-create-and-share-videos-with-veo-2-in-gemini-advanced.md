# Create and share videos with Veo 2 in Gemini Advanced

**Gemini** · 2025-04-22 · [[Themes/collaboration|Collaboration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-04-22-2025-04-22-c9dc8138|Gemini updates — 2025-04-22]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Starting today, Gemini Advanced subscribers can generate high-quality, 8-second videos using Veo 2. Simply describe what you have in mind and watch your ideas come to life in motion - whether you're creating for fun, sharing with friends, or adding a dynamic element to your projects. This feature is available globally on web and mobile to Gemini Advanced subscribers as part of the Google One AI Premium Plan.
>
> How to access: Select ‘Veo 2’ from the model dropdown.
>
> How to share: Download the MP4 file, generate a public link, or share the video directly (mobile only).
>
> Why: Veo 2 in Gemini puts powerful video creation tools directly into your hands. It offers an intuitive way to visualize concepts, tell stories, and bring creative ideas to life dynamically, without requiring complex software or prior video-editing experience. Just describe it and Gemini will create it.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
