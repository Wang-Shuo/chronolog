# The Gemini mobile app is available in Europe

**Gemini** · 2024-06-05 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Expansion · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-06-05-2024-06-05-34d6bc2b|Gemini updates — 2024-06-05]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: The Gemini app is now available in countries like the UK, Germany, France, Italy, Sweden, Switzerland, and more. You can download the Gemini app from the Android Play Store, or try it in the Google App on iOS. Chat to get help with writing, planning, learning and more in a variety of supported languages. More languages and countries will be coming soon. Learn more about language, country, and device availability in the Help Center .
>
> Why: We’re dedicated to bringing Gemini responsibly to more countries and languages, on the devices you use the most.
