# Gemini introduces Personal Intelligence

**Gemini** · 2026-01-20 · [[Themes/safety-privacy|Safety and Privacy]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2026-01-20-2026-01-20-2a086562|Gemini updates — 2026-01-20]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Starting today, you can now connect Gemini to your Google apps —including Gmail, Photos, YouTube, and Search—to unlock a more proactive and personalized experience. We built Personal Intelligence with privacy at the center. Connecting your apps is off by default: you choose to turn it on, decide exactly which apps to connect, and can turn it off anytime. This beta version of Personal Intelligence is rolling out to Google AI Pro and AI Ultra subscribers in the U.S. It works across Web, Android, and iOS. The experience is not yet available to Google Workspace business and education plans.
>
> Why: The best assistants don’t just know the world; they know you. By securely connecting to the apps you use every day, Gemini understands your personal context to provide uniquely tailored answers. You won’t have to start from scratch every time you ask for help; instead, it feels like you’re collaborating with a partner who is already up to speed.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
