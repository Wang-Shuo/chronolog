# Gemini can connect to more apps, starting with OpenTable, Canva, and Instacart

**Gemini** · 2026-05-19 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2026-05-19-2026-05-19-16a11edf|Gemini updates — 2026-05-19]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Gemini can now connect to even more apps you use every day, starting with OpenTable, Canva, and Instacart. Whether you are booking a table at a new restaurant tailored to your taste, ordering groceries for a family recipe, or creating a flyer—Gemini doesn’t just find information; it helps you take action seamlessly with connected apps. We are currently in the process of rolling out OpenTable (en-US), Canva (en*), and Instacart (en-US), which will become fully available to all eligible users over the next few weeks.
>
> Why: Gemini is evolving to help you take action, going beyond simply finding information. It acts as an intelligent orchestrator across a growing network of connected apps, starting with OpenTable, Canva, and Instacart, with many more on the way, including partners like Zillow, Adobe, and Box.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
