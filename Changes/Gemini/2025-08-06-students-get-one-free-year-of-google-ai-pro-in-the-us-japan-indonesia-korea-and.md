# Students get one free year of Google AI Pro in the US, Japan, Indonesia, Korea, and Brazil

**Gemini** · 2025-08-06 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-08-06-2025-08-06-0a3b54f6|Gemini updates — 2025-08-06]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Google is offering students aged 18 and older in the US, Japan, Indonesia, Korea, and Brazil a free one-year upgrade to Google AI Pro, available until October 6, 2025. This plan includes unlimited chat, image uploads and quiz generations for homework help, and exam prep, plus 2 TB of storage. Students will have access to our Gemini 2.5 Pro model, Veo 3 Fast, Deep Research, and an enhanced NotebookLM with 5x more notebooks and audio overviews.
>
> Why: Our mission is to empower every student with the AI tools and skills they need to advance their learning, unlock their creativity, and build the future of work.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
