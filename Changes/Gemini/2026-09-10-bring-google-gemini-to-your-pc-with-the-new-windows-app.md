# Bring Google Gemini to your PC with the new Windows app

**Gemini** · 2026-09-10 · [[Themes/file-data|Files and Data]] · Expansion · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2026-09-10-2026-09-10-3c3f563a|Gemini updates — 2026-09-10]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: The Gemini app is now available on Windows. Press Alt + Space to summon Gemini instantly from anywhere on your PC whenever you need it. You can get quick answers, draft messages, and brainstorm ideas right alongside your active applications. Inside your dedicated desktop workspace, you have access to everything you love about Gemini. You can connect to Google apps like Gmail and Google Drive to summarize documents and find information quickly, generate custom images with Nano Banana, and dive into in-depth research on any topic. The app is available on Windows 10 or later. Download it today at gemini.google/desktop .
>
> Why: Your desktop is where you get work done. Bringing Gemini straight to your PC with instant shortcut access keeps you in your flow, making it simple to get answers and create visuals right alongside your everyday tools.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
