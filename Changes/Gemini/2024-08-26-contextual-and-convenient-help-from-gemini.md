# Contextual and convenient help from Gemini

**Gemini** · 2024-08-26 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-08-26-2024-08-26-b4a7fc10|Gemini updates — 2024-08-26]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: On Android , Gemini’s overlay offers convenient assistance when and where you need it. Activate by holding the power button on most phones, or saying "Hey Google" to get quick answers, contextual help, and everyday assistance without leaving the screen you were on.
>
> Why: Having Gemini’s responses appear directly in the overlay helps you stay focused on the task at hand; for example you can simply drag and drop custom images directly into Gmail where generated images are available , or find key information from a YouTube video you are watching.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
