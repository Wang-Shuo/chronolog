# Launching Gemini to Google Workspace for Education teen students

**Gemini** · 2024-11-12 · [[Themes/safety-privacy|Safety and Privacy]] · Policy · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-11-12-2024-11-12-5a3a95e6|Gemini updates — 2024-11-12]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Google Workspace for Education users 13+ (or the applicable age in their country ) can now access the Gemini app with added data protection. Education administrators manage access settings for these users via the Google Workspace Admin console.
>
> Why: Google is committed to making AI helpful for everyone, in the classroom and beyond . We want to both prepare teens with the skills and tools they need to thrive in a future where GenAI exists and help them learn how this technology can be used to unlock creativity and facilitate learning. Gemini can provide guided support to help students learn more confidently with in-the-moment assistance, practice materials and real-time feedback and ideas.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
