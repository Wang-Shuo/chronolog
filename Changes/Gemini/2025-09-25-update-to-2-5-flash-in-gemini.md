# Update to 2.5 Flash in Gemini

**Gemini** · 2025-09-25 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-09-25-2025-09-25-acfaa37d|Gemini updates — 2025-09-25]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: An enhanced version of Gemini 2.5 Flash is now available to Gemini app users. Responses are now better organized with improved formatting. Gemini 2.5 Flash uses elements like headers, lists, and tables to make even complex outputs clearer and easier to understand. We’ve also improved image understanding, so Gemini can better interpret and respond to your uploads. To feel the difference, try working through a problem at school or work involving an image, like a diagram, and having Gemini walk you through it; or upload your class notes and ask Gemini to organize, summarize or create flashcards for you.
>
> Why: We’re committed to continually improve performance and making Gemini a more insightful, collaborative partner. When it’s better at understanding context and images, it’s easier for you to create, interact, and accomplish your goals.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
