# Go Beyond the Answer with Step-by-Step Guided Learning

**Gemini** · 2025-08-06 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-08-06-2025-08-06-0a3b54f6|Gemini updates — 2025-08-06]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Gemini now offers Guided Learning, a new mode designed to help you build a deeper understanding of whatever you’re trying to learn or do. Instead of providing a single answer, this feature guides you through subjects step-by-step, breaking down concepts and providing interactive help along the way. It's ideal for understanding complex information or developing a new skill. You can activate this tool using the ‘Guided Learning’ chip on desktop or the ‘Learn’ chip on mobile. This experience is open to all users.
>
> Why: Learning isn't about finding the answer quickly; it's about grasping the underlying concepts. Our mission is to provide tools that foster genuine comprehension and critical thinking. Guided Learning moves beyond simple Q&A to create a more effective learning environment, empowering you to build lasting knowledge and the confidence to tackle any subject.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
