# Priority access with Gemini Advanced

**Gemini** · 2024-12-11 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-12-11-2024-12-11-83a5f0fa|Gemini updates — 2024-12-11]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Gemini Deep Research is your personal research assistant that helps you get up to speed on just about any topic. It’ll break down your prompt into a number of relevant research questions, automatically analyze sites on your behalf for relevant information, and create comprehensive multi-page reports on just about any topic—all in minutes. Your reports are presented in an immersive viewer that you can refine with Gemini and even open in Google Docs! It’s starting to roll out in English with Gemini Advanced as part of the Google One AI Premium Plan. Try it by selecting “Gemini 1.5 Pro with Deep Research” from the Gemini Advanced model drop-down.
>
> Why: Save hours of work with Deep Research, the most advanced way to research with Gemini. Instead of spending hours researching, let Gemini do the work for you – so you can get on to doing everything else.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
