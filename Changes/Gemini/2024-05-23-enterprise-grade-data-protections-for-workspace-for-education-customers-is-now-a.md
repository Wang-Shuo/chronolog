# Enterprise-grade data protections for Workspace for Education customers is now…

**Gemini** · 2024-05-23 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-05-23-2024-05-23-e7c92409|Gemini updates — 2024-05-23]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Available today, the new Gemini Education and Gemini Education Premium add-ons give Google Workspace for Education customers access to 1.5 Pro - Google’s most capable AI model widely available today, with enterprise-grade data protections. Administrators can manage Gemini settings within the Google Workspace Admin Console. The Gemini Education add-ons are available and optimized in English in over 150 countries and territories
>
> Why: With Gemini Education, education leaders, staff and students 18 years and older can save time, make learning more personal, inspire creativity and learn confidently in a private and secure environment. Gemini Education add-ons are covered under the Google Workspace for Education Terms of Service; your data is not reviewed by anyone, used to train AI models, or shared with other users or organizations.
>
