# Meet your new AI browsing assistant: Gemini in Chrome

**Gemini** · 2026-01-28 · [[Themes/search-browsing|Search and Browsing]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2026-01-28-2026-01-28-27ea870c|Gemini updates — 2026-01-28]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: We’re introducing major updates to Gemini in Chrome to help you get the most out of the web. Built on Gemini 3, our most intelligent model, this update brings a new side panel experience that lets you multitask, access deeper Google apps integration, and quickly edit images with Nano Banana—all without leaving your tab. We're also introducing a preview of auto browse to help you automate tasks while keeping you in control. This experience is available on Windows, MacOS, and Chromebook Plus in the U.S. Auto browse is currently rolling out in preview in the U.S. to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers . These updates are not yet available to Google Workspace business and education plans.
>
> Why: Think of the new Gemini in Chrome as an assistant that helps you find information and get things done on the web easier than ever before. It cuts down on context switching, actively helping you do more right on your page.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
