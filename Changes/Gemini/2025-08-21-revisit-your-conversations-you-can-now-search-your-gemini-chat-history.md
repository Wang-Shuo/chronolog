# Revisit your conversations: You can now search your Gemini chat history

**Gemini** · 2025-08-21 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-08-21-2025-08-21-30436cd0|Gemini updates — 2025-08-21]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Find and revisit past conversations in Gemini. To get started, simply expand your navigation menu and tap the magnifying glass or the “Search for chats” bar. Enter a keyword or phrase, and Gemini will display all relevant conversations. This feature is live globally on web and mobile.
>
> Why: This is one of our most highly requested features, and lets you collaborate more seamlessly with Gemini. No matter if you're working on a project from last week or searching for a prompt you crafted a month ago, the search bar helps you find what you need in seconds to keep your momentum going.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
