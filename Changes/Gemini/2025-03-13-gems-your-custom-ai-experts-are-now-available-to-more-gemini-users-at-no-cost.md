# Gems, your custom AI experts, are now available to more Gemini users at no cost

**Gemini** · 2025-03-13 · [[Themes/collaboration|Collaboration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-03-13-2025-03-13-af613e5f|Gemini updates — 2025-03-13]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Gems are a simple way to customize Gemini towards your specific goals, so you can do more with less effort. It's like having a team of AI experts for help with everything from everyday tasks—like writing, translating, and meal planning—to more complex problem-solving. To get started, choose from our premade Gems or quickly create custom Gems by listing specific instructions for your Gem to reference.
>
> Why: We want more users to experience the magic of Gems. Whether you need a Gem for writing help in a specific tone and style, or expert knowledge on a niche topic, Gems can supercharge your productivity.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
