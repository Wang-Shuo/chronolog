**Gemini** · 2024-02-21 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-02-21-2024-02-21-1639548e|Gemini updates — 2024-02-21]]

> What: Available today, the new Gemini Business and Gemini Enterprise plans give Google Workspace customers access to one of Google’s most capable AI models, 1.0 Ultra in Gemini and enterprise-grade data protections. Administrators can manage Gemini settings via the Google Workspace admin console. The Gemini Business and Gemini Enterprise plans are available and optimized in English in over 150 countries and territories .

This is just the beginning with even more innovation to come, upgrade today to Gemini Business or Gemini Enterprise.
