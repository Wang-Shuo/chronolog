# Your Custom Gems are Now Shareable

**Gemini** · 2025-09-18 · [[Themes/context-window|Context Window]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-09-18-2025-09-18-0b265ee7|Gemini updates — 2025-09-18]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Starting today, you can share the Gems you create with friends, family, and colleagues. Gems are a simple way to customize Gemini to achieve your specific goals. This means you can tackle recurring tasks without retyping lengthy prompts and providing context each time. Share your weekly meal planner Gem to help a friend get inspiration, or send your vacation planner Gem to a family member before their big trip. To share, simply open your Gem manager on the web and click “Share” next to any Gem you’ve created. Sharing Gems works just like sharing files in Google Drive, giving you control over who can view or edit your Gem. Visit the Help Center to learn more about sharing Gems.
>
> Why: Turn your favorite Gems into a shared resource so you can help others also save time on repetitive prompting and get started more easily. Rather than creating from scratch, you can use a fine-tuned Gem with effective instructions with friends or even an entire organization.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
