# Introducing Gemini 2.0 Flash to Gemini

**Gemini** · 2025-01-30 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-01-30-2025-01-30-a3d58baf|Gemini updates — 2025-01-30]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Gemini is now powered by 2.0 Flash, our latest model that is designed for the agentic era, delivering fast responses and stronger performance across a number of key benchmarks for everyday help with tasks like brainstorming, learning, or writing. In addition to 2.0 Flash, Gemini Advanced users will continue to have access to a 1M token context window for up to 1,500 pages of file uploads, priority access to features such as Deep Research and Gems, 2TB of storage, and more. Gemini 1.5 Flash and 1.5 Pro will remain available for the next few weeks.
>
> Why: With faster responses and improved performance, we’re making it easier for you to unlock new ways to create, interact, and collaborate with Gemini.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
