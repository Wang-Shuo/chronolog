# Gemini with 2.0 Flash Thinking (experimental) is now even better at completing…

**Gemini** · 2025-03-13 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-03-13-2025-03-13-af613e5f|Gemini updates — 2025-03-13]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: For complex tasks that require multiple apps, Gemini's 2.0 Flash Thinking delivers improved performance by breaking down tasks and showing its thoughts as it reasons, plans and works across multiple apps. Flash Thinking can now connect to more of your apps like Calendar, Notes, Tasks, Google Photos, Youtube, and Google Maps. You can ask Gemini to do things like search for YouTube tutorials on how to cook a specific cuisine for the first time, save the ingredients to a shopping list, and search for grocery stores to stop by all in a single prompt. Some Apps are not yet available to Enterprise and Education users but will be in the future.
>
> Why: Switching between apps to complete tasks can be tedious and time consuming. We want to help users be more productive and save time completing multi-step tasks on the phone or desktop.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
