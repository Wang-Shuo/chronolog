# Daily Brief is your personalized overview of what matters today

**Gemini** · 2026-05-19 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2026-05-19-2026-05-19-16a11edf|Gemini updates — 2026-05-19]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Daily Brief is your personalized daily overview built with Gemini. Powered by Personal Intelligence, it distills priorities from your connected apps (like Gmail and Google Calendar from Workspace) and Gemini chats into one intelligent, easy-to-read view. The brief organizes your day to easily see what’s “Top of Mind,” surfacing your immediate priorities with suggested next steps, and helps you “Look Ahead” to make progress on your longer-term goals . It transforms your morning from scramble to smooth with an actionable plan letting you jump right into the things that actually matter. Daily Brief is starting to roll out to all Google AI subscribers 18 and over in the United States.
>
> Why: The hardest part of the day shouldn't be figuring out where to begin. Daily Brief replaces the morning mental gymnastics of checking various apps with a single, prioritized digest so you can save your cognitive energy for the work that really matters.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
