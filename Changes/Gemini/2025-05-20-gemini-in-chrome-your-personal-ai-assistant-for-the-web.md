# Gemini in Chrome, your personal AI assistant for the web

**Gemini** · 2025-05-20 · [[Themes/context-window|Context Window]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-05-20-2025-05-20-3970ada9|Gemini updates — 2025-05-20]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Meet Gemini in Chrome , your personal AI assistant for the web. Simply talk or type to understand complex topics, brainstorm ideas, or compare details using the context of your current page, all without needing to switch tabs. Gemini in Chrome is starting to roll out tomorrow on Windows and Mac OS devices to Google AI Pro or Ultra subscribers in the U.S. who have their Chrome language set to English. The experience is not available to Google Workspace business and education plans. We look forward to bringing this feature to more people and additional languages soon.
>
> Why: Gemini in Chrome helps you make the most of the web. Instantly get key takeaways and simple explanations for complex subjects, helping you do more and get assistance right on the page you’re browsing.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
