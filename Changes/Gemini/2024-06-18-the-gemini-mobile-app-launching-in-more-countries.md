# The Gemini mobile app launching in more countries

**Gemini** · 2024-06-18 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Expansion · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-06-18-2024-06-18-33742b91|Gemini updates — 2024-06-18]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: The Gemini app is now available in India, Türkiye, Bangladesh, Pakistan, and Sri Lanka. You can now interact with Gemini on Android, when you download the Gemini app in the Google Play Store. On iOS, try Gemini in the Google app. Chat to get help with writing, planning, learning and more. Learn more about language, country, and device availability in the Help Center.
>
> Why: We believe AI should be easier to access in your language, on the device you use the most.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
