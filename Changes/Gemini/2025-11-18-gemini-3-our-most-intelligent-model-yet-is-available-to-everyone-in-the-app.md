# Gemini 3, our most intelligent model yet, is available to everyone in the app

**Gemini** · 2025-11-18 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-11-18-2025-11-18-3d75836f|Gemini updates — 2025-11-18]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Gemini 3 Pro is rolling out to everyone in the Gemini app starting today, with higher limits for users in the Google AI Plus, Pro & Ultra plans. It brings state-of-the-art reasoning to your most complex problems, and is our best vibe coding model yet. It can also reason across text, images, audio and video better than ever before. So whether you're uploading a video of you playing a sport to get advice on how to improve your form, or transcribing notes from a lecture you missed, the Gemini app can help without missing a beat. Starting from your first use you’ll also notice responses are more helpful, better formatted and more concise. Try Gemini 3 Pro by selecting “Thinking” in the model drop down.
>
> Why: We want to make Gemini the most helpful AI assistant for everyone. With the advanced reasoning of Gemini 3 Pro, you can now tackle your most ambitious projects with greater confidence and efficiency.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
