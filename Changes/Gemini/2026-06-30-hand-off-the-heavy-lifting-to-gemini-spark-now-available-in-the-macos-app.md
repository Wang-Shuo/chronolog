# Hand off the heavy lifting to Gemini Spark, now available in the macOS app

**Gemini** · 2026-06-30 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Expansion · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2026-06-30-2026-06-30-11e709aa|Gemini updates — 2026-06-30]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Gemini Spark, your personal AI agent, is coming to the Gemini app for macOS. It helps you navigate your digital life by working on tasks on your behalf. Operating autonomously and under your direction, it organizes folders, uses your local files to build documents, and handles complex workflows across Google Workspace. Coming soon, you will be able to command Gemini Spark to run tasks or access local files on your Mac directly from the web or mobile app, ensuring your desktop workflows remain at your fingertips wherever you go. Starting today, Gemini Spark is available in English in the Gemini macOS app to Google AI Ultra subscribers aged 18 and over in supported countries . Download the app at gemini.google/desktop .
>
> Why: We built this to help you automate your daily busywork, from managing local files to streamlining multi-step projects. With Gemini Spark, we are transforming Gemini from an AI assistant that answers your questions into one that actually works proactively on your behalf.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
