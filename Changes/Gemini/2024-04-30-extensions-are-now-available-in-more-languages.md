# Extensions are now available in more languages

**Gemini** · 2024-04-30 · [[Themes/safety-privacy|Safety and Privacy]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-04-30-2024-04-30-782fdae3|Gemini updates — 2024-04-30]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: You can now access real-time info from Google apps and services like YouTube, Maps, Flights, and Hotels in over 40 new languages where Gemini is available, including German, Portuguese, Spanish, and more. With the Gmail, Docs, and Drive Extensions, you can even easily find, summarize and get answers even from your personal content. You’re in control of your privacy settings when deciding how you want to use extensions.
>
> Why: We’re dedicated to developing Gemini for everyone. This means steadily introducing important features to new languages while ensuring the best possible user experience.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
