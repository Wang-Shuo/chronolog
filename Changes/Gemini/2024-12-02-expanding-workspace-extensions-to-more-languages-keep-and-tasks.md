# Expanding Workspace extensions to more languages: Keep and Tasks

**Gemini** · 2024-12-02 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Expansion · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-12-02-2024-12-02-1473255e|Gemini updates — 2024-12-02]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Over the coming days, more users around the world will be able to connect with more apps and services in 9 languages (Korean, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Bahasa Indonesia, Hindi, Portuguese, and Chinese), letting you set reminders with Google Tasks, make and refine lists with Google Keep, and engage with playlists in YouTube Music. Learn more about extensions .
>
> Why: We're continuing to build new ways for Gemini to connect to the apps you rely on every day. Through these extensions, Gemini can coordinate across your apps and take actions for you, better than ever. Read more about how Gemini works with your favorite Assistant actions, including details on upcoming improvements.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
