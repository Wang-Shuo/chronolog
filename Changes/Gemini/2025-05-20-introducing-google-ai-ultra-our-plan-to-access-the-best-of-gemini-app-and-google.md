# Introducing Google AI Ultra, our plan to access the best of Gemini app and Google AI

**Gemini** · 2025-05-20 · [[Themes/context-window|Context Window]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-05-20-2025-05-20-3970ada9|Gemini updates — 2025-05-20]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Get the best of Gemini with Google AI Ultra. Subscribers get the highest access to our best Gemini models, including 2.5 Pro, and powerful features like Deep Research, video generation with Veo 3, and a 1M token context window. Google AI Ultra subscribers also get early access to our newest AI innovations as they become available, such as Project Mariner. Gemini in Google AI Ultra is available in the US and includes additional benefits as part of a Google AI Ultra subscription. Learn more . Google AI Ultra is not currently available to Google Workspace business and education customers. However, we will make some of its capabilities accessible through our Workspace Alpha program soon and we're excited to see how customers will use these capabilities.
>
> Why: We're committed to bringing you Google's latest AI innovations faster. Google AI Ultra is designed for our most dedicated users, giving them the best of Gemini and Google AI wherever they are. In addition, Ultra subscribers get priority access to our newest AI advancements, helping them shape the future of AI. Upgrade to Google AI Ultra .
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
