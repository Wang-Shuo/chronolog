# Introducing Gemini 1.5 Pro to Gemini Advanced

**Gemini** · 2024-05-14 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-05-14-2024-05-14-1af0ccd7|Gemini updates — 2024-05-14]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Gemini Advanced gives you direct access to Google’s next-generation model, 1.5 Pro. With 1.5 Pro, Gemini Advanced can process significantly more information, running up to 1 million tokens - that's 1,500 pages. Gemini Advanced will also be available in 150+ countries and territories, as well as 35+ supported languages.
>
> Why: A longer context window lets you solve more complex problems than ever before, which can save you time and boost productivity when you explore and analyze information. We're also gradually expanding access to more languages, countries, and territories to enable more people to experience Gemini Advanced. Upgrade to Gemini Advanced
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
