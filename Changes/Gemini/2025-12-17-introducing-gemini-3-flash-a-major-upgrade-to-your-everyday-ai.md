# Introducing Gemini 3 Flash, a major upgrade to your everyday AI

**Gemini** · 2025-12-17 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-12-17-2025-12-17-9b7b562c|Gemini updates — 2025-12-17]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Gemini 3 Flash is now the new default model in the Gemini app. It offers next generation intelligence at lightning speed and represents a major capability upgrade over Gemini 2.5 Flash. This model provides PhD-level reasoning comparable to larger models and delivers a significant leap in multimodal understanding, allowing you to ask questions using images, audio, text, and more. Gemini 3 Flash can be accessed in the Gemini app by selecting “Fast” for quick answers and “Thinking” to help solve more complex problems faster. Gemini 3 Pro is also available as “Pro” in the model picker and remains the best choice for advanced math and coding.
>
> Why: We want to make Gemini the most helpful AI assistant for everyone. By upgrading our default model to Gemini 3 Flash, we’re bringing next generation intelligence to all your tasks, helping you tackle your most ambitious projects, faster.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
