# Meet your new desktop assistant: Gemini for Mac

**Gemini** · 2026-04-15 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2026-04-15-2026-04-15-d55fb74d|Gemini updates — 2026-04-15]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: We’re introducing the Gemini app for Mac, a native desktop experience built to help you get more done without disrupting your flow. With a simple keyboard shortcut (Option + Space), you can bring up Gemini alongside any application you're using. You can also seamlessly share your window, allowing Gemini to understand your context and assist with exactly what’s on your screen. The new desktop app is available to users on macOS versions 15 and up, globally, for free. Download the app directly at gemini.google/desktop .
>
> Why: By bringing a native app to Mac, we're cutting down on context switching and giving you a faster, more integrated way to use Gemini alongside the tools you use every day.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
