# Code, prototype, and build with Google Antigravity in the Gemini macOS app

**Gemini** · 2026-10-13 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2026-10-13-2026-10-13-315d8911|Gemini updates — 2026-10-13]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Google Antigravity is now built into the Gemini macOS app, so go straight from brainstorming in chat to coding apps, building web experiences, and prototyping ideas. Spin up a coding agent in Antigravity, approve its plan, and head right back to your Gemini chat. The agent builds and tests the code in the background, notifying you the second it’s ready to review. To get started, click Antigravity at the top of the Gemini app on your Mac. Available in English for personal Google Accounts and eligible work accounts (18+) across all regions where Antigravity is supported. Download the Gemini macOS app at gemini.google/desktop .
>
> Why: We want to help you turn ambitious ideas into working code and prototypes faster, right where you already work on your Mac. With Antigravity built into the Gemini app, you get an agentic coding partner that plans, builds, and verifies complex code, making it easier to bring your projects to life.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
