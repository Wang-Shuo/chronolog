# An easier way to customize how Gemini works for you and more connected apps

**Gemini** · 2026-10-13 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2026-10-13-2026-10-13-315d8911|Gemini updates — 2026-10-13]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Open the left menu and click Customize to tailor how Gemini responds and connects to your everyday tools in one place. You can discover apps and add skills so Gemini can tackle your most repetitive tasks, like writing in your style or following your formatting rules. You can connect more than 40 apps or services to get things done in one conversation. Whether you are ordering groceries on Instacart, reserving a table on OpenTable, or editing photos with Adobe, you decide which apps to connect to and can manage your settings at any time.
>
> Why: We want to make Gemini work the way you do. By bringing your favorite apps and custom skills together in a single conversation, Gemini helps you skip the context switching and get more done, faster and easier. Customize is rolling out globally across web, mobile, and the Gemini desktop app on Windows and macOS, for all user tiers. Most connected apps are available in English in the U.S., with select apps available in additional English-speaking regions, Brazil, and India.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
