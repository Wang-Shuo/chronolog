# Priority access with Gemini Advanced: Try our latest 2.0 Experimental Advanced model

**Gemini** · 2024-12-17 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-12-17-2024-12-17-824521e8|Gemini updates — 2024-12-17]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Gemini Advanced users can now try Gemini-exp-1206, our model designed to be exceptional at complex tasks such as coding, math, reasoning & instruction following. Whether you’re tackling advanced coding challenges, solving mathematical problems for school or personal projects, or providing detailed, multi-step instructions to craft a tailored business plan, 2.0 Experimental Advanced will help you navigate complex tasks with greater ease. This experimental model is meant to be an early preview and can have unexpected behaviors and may make mistakes. Additionally this model will not have access to real time information and won't be compatible with some Gemini features in its experimental state. You can access this experimental model in the Gemini model drop-down.
>
> Why: We believe in rapid iteration and bringing the best of Gemini to the world, and we want to give Gemini Advanced subscribers priority access to our latest AI innovations. Which is why we’re introducing Gemini-exp-1206 to enable users to experiment with cutting-edge models with our latest enhancements. Your feedback helps us improve these models over time and learning from experimental launches informs how we release models more widely.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
