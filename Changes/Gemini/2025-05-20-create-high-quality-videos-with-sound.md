# Create high-quality videos with sound

**Gemini** · 2025-05-20 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-05-20-2025-05-20-3970ada9|Gemini updates — 2025-05-20]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: You can now create 8-second videos with sound using our latest video generation model, Veo 3. Bring your ideas to life with whimsical characters that speak, scenes enriched with background music, or sound effects for more realistic details. Veo 3 opens up exciting new possibilities for visual storytelling. Veo 3 is available to Google AI Ultra subscribers. The experience is not available to Google Workspace business and education plans. We look forward to bringing this feature to more people and additional languages soon.
>
> Why: We believe in rapid iteration and bringing the best of Gemini to the world, and we want to give Google AI Ultra subscribers priority access to our latest AI models. Your feedback helps us improve these models over time and learning from experimental launches informs how we release models more widely.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
