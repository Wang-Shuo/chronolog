# Add your own files to Deep Research and bring reports to life in Canvas, all on Gemini 2.5

**Gemini** · 2025-05-20 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-05-20-2025-05-20-3970ada9|Gemini updates — 2025-05-20]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: You can now upload your own files and images to use as a source in Deep Research reports, and transform those reports into interactive visuals, quizzes, and more in Canvas. We’re also making Deep Research available on our latest generation 2.5 Flash model for everyone to try at no cost.
>
> Why: These improvements help to make your research more relevant, compelling, and ultimately help you get up to speed on any topic better. With this integration into Canvas, we’re making learning with Deep Research more immersive, by turning highly detailed text into interactive content, quizzes, and apps to further increase your understanding.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
