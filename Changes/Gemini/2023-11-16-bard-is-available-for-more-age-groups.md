# Bard is available for more age groups

**Gemini** · 2023-11-16 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Policy · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2023-11-16-2023-11-16-942bdb18|Gemini updates — 2023-11-16]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Bard is expanding access to teens in most countries around the world, starting with English. We’ve added age-appropriate protections, updated onboarding for teens, and developed experiences geared to empower exploration and learning with Bard. Want to learn more about generative AI and its abilities and limitations? Learn about generative AI .
>
> Why: We believe Bard can be a helpful tool for teens when they need a little extra inspiration and motivation on their ideas, hobbies, and plans, or when they want to better understand topics quickly in a style that works for them. Whether it's learning homework concepts or getting support through big milestones like applying to their first job or preparing for college, Bard can help.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
