# Plan ahead with scheduled actions

**Gemini** · 2025-06-19 · [[Themes/automation-scheduling|Automation and Scheduling]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-06-19-2025-06-19-d0100f17|Gemini updates — 2025-06-19]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: You can now schedule actions in the Gemini app so you can plan ahead for future and recurring tasks. Ask Gemini to help you get organized with a daily summary of your calendar, to-do's and important unread emails. Or get weekly creative ideas for a project and updates on topics you’re passionate about. You can schedule recurring actions, like finding local weekend music events every Friday, or one-time tasks, like asking for a recap of a keynote the next morning. Just tell Gemini what you need and when—it'll handle it for you. Scheduled actions are available to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers and qualifying Google Workspace business and education plans.
>
> Why: We want Gemini to be your personal, proactive and powerful AI assistant, and scheduled actions can help boost your productivity in new and exciting ways with proactive help that free up your time.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
