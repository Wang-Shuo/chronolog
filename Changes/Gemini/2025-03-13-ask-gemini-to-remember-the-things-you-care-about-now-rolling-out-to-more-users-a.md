# Ask Gemini to remember the things you care about

**Gemini** · 2025-03-13 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-03-13-2025-03-13-af613e5f|Gemini updates — 2025-03-13]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: The ability to let you ask Gemini to remember your interests and preferences for more helpful responses, is now rolling out to Gemini users and in over 40 languages! Previously, this was only offered in English to Gemini Advanced subscribers with the Google One AI Premium plan. You're in control: You can easily view, edit, or delete shared information. The experience is not yet available to Google Workspace or Education users.
>
> Why: Now, even more users can customize Gemini to better fit their needs and get things done more effectively.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
