# Get more from the Gemini app with the Google AI Pro subscription

**Gemini** · 2025-05-20 · [[Themes/pricing-model|Pricing Model]] · Policy · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2025-05-20-2025-05-20-3970ada9|Gemini updates — 2025-05-20]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: The Gemini Advanced name is retiring. With this update, all the Gemini app features and benefits you rely on will now be part of your Google AI Pro subscription. As a Google AI Pro subscriber, you'll also retain access to key Gemini app capabilities, such as video generation with Veo 2, our most intelligent Deep Research, 1 million token context window, and more access to the 2.5 Pro model. Google AI Pro (previously called Google One AI Premium) is available in 150 countries and territories , and includes additional benefits as part of a Google AI Pro subscription. Learn more . Gemini app, as part of Google AI Pro, will continue to be available to qualifying Google Workspace business and education plans.
>
> Why: Google AI Pro more accurately reflects the value this tier of Gemini service offers, including access to additional Google AI products and professional-grade tools, such as NotebookLM, Whisk, Flow, and more. Upgrade to Google AI Pro .
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
