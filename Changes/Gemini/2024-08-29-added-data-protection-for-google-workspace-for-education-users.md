# Added data protection for Google Workspace for Education users

**Gemini** · 2024-08-29 · [[Themes/safety-privacy|Safety and Privacy]] · Launch · [Official source](https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en) · from [[Releases/Gemini/gemini-2024-08-29-2024-08-29-cbac8fe7|Gemini updates — 2024-08-29]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> What: Google Workspace for Education users now get added data protection when using Gemini as an Additional Service. With added data protection, your data is not human reviewed or used to train AI models. Gemini as an Additional Service is covered by the Google Terms of Service .
>
> Why: Education leaders, staff and students 18 years and older can now chat with Gemini - free of charge - knowing their data is not being used to train AI models.
>
> Source: https://gemini.google/us/release-notes/?hl=en
