> You can now [sign in to Devin with ChatGPT](https://devin.ai/blog/sign-in-with-chatgpt) and use your eligible ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan for GPT model usage in Devin Desktop. Choose a GPT model and those requests are billed to your ChatGPT plan; for the best value, use Fusion with GPT-6 Astra as the lead and SWE-2 as the sidekick. See [Use your ChatGPT plan](https://docs.devin.ai/admin/billing/chatgpt.md) for setup and eligible models.
