<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Cloud Sync : Introduced Cloud Sync v2, bringing support for syncing more of your Raycast data across devices

> AI Chat : Archive chats to keep your chat list tidy without deleting conversations

> AI Chat : Added Recently Deleted to view and restore deleted chats for up to 60 days

> Quicklinks and Snippets : Organize your collection with tags that sync across devices

> Shortcuts : Added the New Chat action to start a fresh conversation

> Widgets : Open AI Chat projects directly from the Ask AI widget

> Notes : Find recent notes more easily with Previous 7 Days and Previous 30 Days groups

> AI : See your credit renewal date and time in your local time zone
