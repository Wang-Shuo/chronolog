## Changes in this digest

> Keyboard: Translate text without leaving the app you’re typing in, with your recent languages close at hand

> Keyboard: Change text case or give your words a new look with text styles

> Keyboard: Add a dedicated number row to keep digits within reach

> Keyboard: Add individual AI Commands to your action row for one-tap access

> Emoji: Get emoji suggestions based on the message you’re writing

> Keyboard: Added an option to turn off automatic Snippet expansion

> Keyboard: Added haptic feedback when selecting emoji and tapping action row buttons

> Keyboard: Improved autocorrection

> GIFs: Automatically return to the keyboard panel after copying a GIF

> Keyboard: Keep typing available when signing in or syncing needs attention

> Keyboard: Remember your Split/Join choice in landscape on Duo, even after the keyboard restarts
