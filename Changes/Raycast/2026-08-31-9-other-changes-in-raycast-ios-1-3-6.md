# 9 other changes in Raycast ios 1.3.6

**Raycast** · 2026-08-31 · Other changes · Digest · [Official source](https://www.raycast.com/changelog/ios/1-3-6) · from [[Releases/Raycast/raycast-2026-08-31-ios-1-3-6-3c7112fa|Raycast ios 1.3.6]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Conversation History : Added an option to show conversations from AI Chat and AI Commands together or keep them separate

> Cloud Sync : Improved reliability for accounts with large sync histories by downloading changes in resumable batches

> Cloud Sync : Improved reliability when signing out or switching accounts

> AI : New icons for AI commands

> AI : You can now swipe down on the AI composer to dismiss the keyboard

> AI : Responses stream more smoothly, and chats running in the background resume more reliably

> AI : Chat titles are now generated in the conversation's language

> Misc : Favorites and built-in commands are ready right at launch

> Subscriptions : Your trial end date is now displayed
