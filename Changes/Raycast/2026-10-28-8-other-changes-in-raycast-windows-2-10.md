## Changes in this digest

> Games : Added an Auto HDR setting that turns HDR on while a game is in front and restores it afterwards

> Notes: You can now add images to notes

> Notes: Free plan now gets unlimited notes

> AI : Usage meters now warn in orange and red as you approach the limit

> AI Chat : Table headers now stay visible while scrolling through long tables

> Notes: You can now drag and drop list items

> Notes : Clicking a checkbox while holding ctrl will also complete its children

> Snippets & Quicklinks : Fixed stale dynamic placeholder values when copying Quicklinks or copying and pasting snippets
