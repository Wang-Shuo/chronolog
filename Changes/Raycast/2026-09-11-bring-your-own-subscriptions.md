# Bring Your Own Subscriptions

**Raycast** · 2026-09-11 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.raycast.com/changelog/macos/2-3) · from [[Releases/Raycast/raycast-2026-09-11-macos-2-3-56ab676e|Raycast macos 2.3]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now connect your Claude or ChatGPT account to any paid Raycast plan and use those models in AI Chat, Quick AI, and AI Commands. This lets you run the heaviest tasks without worrying about additional costs. We plan to add other providers in the future, turning AI Chat into your single interface to get the most out of your subscriptions. You can enable models from your subscriptions in Settings → AI → Models & Providers.
>
> Source: https://www.raycast.com/changelog/macos/2-3
