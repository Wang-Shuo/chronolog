## Changes in this digest

> Keyboard : Added a full typing experience with Slide to Type, autocorrection, word suggestions, Quick Fix, and inline Snippet expansion

> AI Chat : Queue follow-up messages while AI responds, or choose Steer in Settings → AI to redirect the conversation

> Search : Find chats by their message content

> Search: Discover AI projects directly from search

> Cloud Sync : View your synced devices, check their last sync, rename them, and remove devices you no longer use

> AI Chat : Send attachments without adding a text prompt

> AI Chat : Show photo suggestion in composer
