**Raycast** · 2026-10-16 · [[Themes/reliability-performance|Reliability and Performance]] · Digest · [Official source](https://www.raycast.com/changelog/macos/2-8) · from [[Releases/Raycast/raycast-2026-10-16-macos-2-8-5f7981fc|Raycast macos 2.8]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Extensions : Installs and updates are about 4× faster on average, and up to 30× faster

> File Search : Results show up to 6× faster, slow searches are gone, typos are handled much better, and indexing your files is faster and uses less CPU

> Smoother scrolling : Root Search and other lists scroll up to 2× faster
