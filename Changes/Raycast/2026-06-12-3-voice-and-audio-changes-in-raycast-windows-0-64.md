# 3 Voice and Audio changes in Raycast windows 0.64

**Raycast** · 2026-06-12 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Digest · [Official source](https://www.raycast.com/changelog/windows/0-64) · from [[Releases/Raycast/raycast-2026-06-12-windows-0-64-b12c3d44|Raycast windows 0.64]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Dictation : Transcription Styles let your dictated text automatically adapt its tone and formatting to the app you're writing in (email, messaging, and more).

> Dictation : Dictation now uses the context of the app you're dictating into to transcribe names, terminology, and on-screen words more accurately.

> Dictation : Finish and Cancel Shortcuts, use Ctrl+Esc/Ctrl+Enter or Esc/Enter to quickly submit or cancel dictation session.

Source: https://www.raycast.com/changelog/windows/0-64
