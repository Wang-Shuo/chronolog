# Quick AI gets a major refresh in this release, bringing it closer to the AI Chat…

**Raycast** · 2026-05-08 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · [Official source](https://www.raycast.com/changelog/macos/0-56) · from [[Releases/Raycast/raycast-2026-05-08-macos-0-56-0dfd8c64|Raycast macos 0.56]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Quick AI gets a major refresh in this release, bringing it closer to the AI Chat experience you already know. The redesigned interface feels like a natural sibling of AI Chat, with a multi-line composer at the bottom so you can see and edit longer prompts at a glance. You can also attach files and images, dial in reasoning effort, and use most of what AI Chat offers. Quick AI now handles longer, more involved questions just as well as short ones.
>
> On top of that, AI Extensions, AI Skills, and MCP servers are now available in Quick AI and AI Chat, and they can be loaded automatically based on your request. No more tagging extensions to bring in the right tools.
>
> Source: https://www.raycast.com/changelog/macos/0-56
