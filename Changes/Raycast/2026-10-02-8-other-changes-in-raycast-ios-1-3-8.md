# 8 other changes in Raycast ios 1.3.8

**Raycast** · 2026-10-02 · Other changes · Digest · [Official source](https://www.raycast.com/changelog/ios/1-3-8) · from [[Releases/Raycast/raycast-2026-10-02-ios-1-3-8-6fb225d6|Raycast ios 1.3.8]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> AI: Ask Raycast to find and read previous conversations with the AI Chat tools

> AI: View Mermaid diagrams directly in chat responses

> Notes: Added the Append to Note action in Shortcuts

> AI: Unsent messages are now saved as drafts in Home and individual chats

> AI: Added favorite and pin actions directly inside chats, and a favorite action in the chat list

> Apple Health: Improved distance precision in AI responses for health metrics and workouts

> AI: Restored clipboard attachments and fixed missing or stale attachment previews

> Widgets: Keep the current note or chat open when returning to the app from a widget

Source: https://www.raycast.com/changelog/ios/1-3-8
