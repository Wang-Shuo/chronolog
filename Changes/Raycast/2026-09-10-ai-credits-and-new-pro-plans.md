# AI Credits and New Pro Plans

**Raycast** · 2026-09-10 · [[Themes/pricing-model|Pricing Model]] · Pricing · [Official source](https://www.raycast.com/changelog/ios/1-3-7) · from [[Releases/Raycast/raycast-2026-09-10-ios-1-3-7-b3823ae4|Raycast ios 1.3.7]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> This update introduces AI credits and a new Max plan. Raycast AI is moving to usage-based pricing, with monthly credits included in every paid plan. Different models and requests use different amounts of credits, and the new AI Usage view makes it easy to check your balance and see where your credits go.
>
> Pro includes 500 credits a month, and Advanced AI is now called Pro+, with 3,000 credits. Need more room for AI? Max includes 7,500 credits a month for more demanding work. Prices for existing Pro and Advanced AI subscriptions stay the same.
>
> Source: https://www.raycast.com/changelog/ios/1-3-7
