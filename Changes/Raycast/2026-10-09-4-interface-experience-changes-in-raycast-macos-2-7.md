# 4 Interface Experience changes in Raycast macos 2.7

**Raycast** · 2026-10-09 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Digest · [Official source](https://www.raycast.com/changelog/macos/2-7) · from [[Releases/Raycast/raycast-2026-10-09-macos-2-7-72853197|Raycast macos 2.7]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> AI Chat : Included archived chats in sidebar search results

> AI Commands : When Replace Selection can't paste the response, the response is kept on your clipboard and in Clipboard History, and a warning tells you it was copied. This also applies to Translator

> Navigation : Added support for Page Up and Page Down in lists and grids

> Dictionary : Allowed text selection in the detail view
