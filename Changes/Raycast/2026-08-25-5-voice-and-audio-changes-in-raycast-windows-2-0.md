# 5 Voice and Audio changes in Raycast windows 2.0

**Raycast** · 2026-08-25 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Digest · [Official source](https://www.raycast.com/changelog/windows/2-0) · from [[Releases/Raycast/raycast-2026-08-25-windows-2-0-327831d3|Raycast windows 2.0]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Dictation : New Context-Aware Paste setting (experimental) adapts each transcription to the text before your cursor adding a leading space mid-sentence and matching capitalization

> Dictation : New Auto Submit setting (experimental) presses Enter or Shift+Enter after a transcription is pasted, either every time or only when you finish with a chosen keyword

> Dictation : Settings from Wispr Flow, Aqua Voice and Handy can now be imported on Windows, including their hotkeys

> Dictation : Website-specific transcription styles now recognize Arc, Floorp, Helium and Edge, so per-site styles apply in more browsers

> Dictation : Added a word count to the details of each entry in dictation history

Source: https://www.raycast.com/changelog/windows/2-0
