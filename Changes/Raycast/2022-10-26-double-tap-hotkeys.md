**Raycast** · 2022-10-26 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · Launch · [Official source](https://www.raycast.com/changelog/macos-v1/1-42-0) · from [[Releases/Raycast/raycast-2022-10-26-macos-v1-1-42-0-ef85727b|Raycast macos-v1 1.42.0]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
