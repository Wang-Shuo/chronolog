# It's your space for real, back-and-forth conversations with AI

**Raycast** · 2026-06-18 · [[Themes/apps-integrations|Apps and Integrations]] · [Official source](https://www.raycast.com/changelog/windows/0-65) · from [[Releases/Raycast/raycast-2026-06-18-windows-0-65-d3330da5|Raycast windows 0.65]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> It's your space for real, back-and-forth conversations with AI. Quick AI is great for a fast answer. AI Chat is where you go when one answer isn't enough and you want to keep digging. Ask a question, follow up, and keep the thread going, whether you're debugging, drafting some writing, or researching a topic. And it's not just talk. With AI Extensions and MCP servers, AI Chat can pull in live context and take actions across your apps and tools, like creating a Linear issue or searching your codebase, without leaving the conversation. Just open Raycast and start a chat.
>
> Source: https://www.raycast.com/changelog/windows/0-65
