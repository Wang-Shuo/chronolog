# Project monitoring catches errors in your visitors' browsers

**Lovable** · 2026-11-02 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog) · from [[Releases/Lovable/lovable-2026-11-02-2026-11-02-7efe9660|Lovable updates — 2026-11-02]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> On paid plans, [project monitoring](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/project-monitoring.md) now also checks the errors that happen in your visitors' browsers. Lovable looks for the cause of those errors in your code and shows what it finds in the editor. The project owner receives an email about important findings. Lovable collects browser errors only while **Visitor analytics** is enabled.
>
> Source: https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog
