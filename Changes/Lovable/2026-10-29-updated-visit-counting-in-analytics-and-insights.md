# Updated visit counting in Analytics and Insights

**Lovable** · 2026-10-29 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · [Official source](https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog) · from [[Releases/Lovable/lovable-2026-10-29-2026-10-29-850979f2|Lovable updates — 2026-10-29]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> [Analytics](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/analytics.md#chart-and-metrics) for your published apps now counts visits without storing anything on your visitors' devices, and filters out more bots. A reload or a new tab starts a new visit, so many apps show more visits from October 26, 2026. When the period you select starts before October 26 and includes it, the Analytics chart marks that day. Workspace Insights counts **App visits** the same way.
>
> For periods that start on or after October 26, 2026, **Engagement rate** replaces **Bounce rate**. It shows the percentage of visits with at least two page views or at least 10 seconds of engagement.
>
> Source: https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog
