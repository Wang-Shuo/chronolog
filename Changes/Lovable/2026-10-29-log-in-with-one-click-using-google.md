# Log in with one click using Google

**Lovable** · 2026-10-29 · [[Themes/account-access|Account and Access]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog) · from [[Releases/Lovable/lovable-2026-10-29-2026-10-29-850979f2|Lovable updates — 2026-10-29]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now [log in to Lovable](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/create-an-account.md#log-in-to-an-existing-account) from Google's sign-in card. The card appears on the login and sign-up pages when your browser is signed in to Google. Select your account on the card, and Lovable logs you in, or creates an account if you don't have one. Two-factor authentication still applies.
>
> Source: https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog
