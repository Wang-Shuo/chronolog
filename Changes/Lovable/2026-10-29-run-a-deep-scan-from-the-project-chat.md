# Run a Deep scan from the project chat

**Lovable** · 2026-10-29 · [[Themes/safety-privacy|Safety and Privacy]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog) · from [[Releases/Lovable/lovable-2026-10-29-2026-10-29-850979f2|Lovable updates — 2026-10-29]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now ask Lovable in the project chat to review your app's security, and Lovable starts a [Deep scan](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/security#on-demand) in the background. Lovable also runs the **Quick scan**, and you can follow the Deep scan's progress in the Security view. When the Deep scan finishes, Lovable explains the findings in the chat. The scans are free. Your request and Lovable's explanation of the findings are treated as standard build usage and consume credits.
>
> Source: https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog
