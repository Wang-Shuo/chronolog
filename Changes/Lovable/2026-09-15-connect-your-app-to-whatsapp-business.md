**Lovable** · 2026-09-15 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog) · from [[Releases/Lovable/lovable-2026-09-15-2026-09-15-6e6a8d9f|Lovable updates — 2026-09-15]]
