# Lovable remembers what you tell it

**Lovable** · 2026-10-30 · [[Themes/memory|Memory and Personalization]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog) · from [[Releases/Lovable/lovable-2026-10-30-2026-10-30-8c94fa6c|Lovable updates — 2026-10-30]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Lovable now [remembers the preferences, decisions, and facts you state](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/memory.md) as you build, and applies them in later conversations without being reminded. Project memory belongs to one project and is shared by everyone who can edit it. Personal memory covers how you want Lovable to work with you, in every project you build.
>
> Your workspace and project **Memory** settings show a plain-language summary of what Lovable remembers. To correct something, select the text and choose **Change in chat**. In the same settings, you choose which memory Lovable uses or disable it.
>
> Source: https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog
