# Bring your app or website to Lovable

**Lovable** · 2026-10-29 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog) · from [[Releases/Lovable/lovable-2026-10-29-2026-10-29-850979f2|Lovable updates — 2026-10-29]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now [import a project](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/projects/import-project.md) that you built with another tool, or an [existing website](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/projects/import-website.md), and keep building it in Lovable. For a project from Replit, Bolt, Base44, v0, or Manus, export it as a ZIP and upload it. For a website, including one built with Wix, Squarespace, or Webflow, enter its address. Start from **Import project to Lovable** in the `+` menu on your dashboard. Your first import is free. After that, imports are treated as standard build usage and consume credits.
>
> Source: https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog
