# Move SSO to a new identity provider

**Lovable** · 2026-10-29 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog) · from [[Releases/Lovable/lovable-2026-10-29-2026-10-29-850979f2|Lovable updates — 2026-10-29]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Workspace admins and owners on Business and Enterprise plans can now [move an SSO provider to a new identity provider](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/business/sso.md#move-to-a-new-identity-provider) without deleting it. Change the entity ID, issuer, or client ID on the provider's **Connection** tab. The provider keeps its settings, so **Enforce SSO**, SCIM, and the domain lock stay enabled.
>
> Source: https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog
