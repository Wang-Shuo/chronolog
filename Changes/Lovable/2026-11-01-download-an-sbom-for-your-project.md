# Download an SBOM for your project

**Lovable** · 2026-11-01 · [[Themes/safety-privacy|Safety and Privacy]] · Launch · [Official source](https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog) · from [[Releases/Lovable/lovable-2026-11-01-2026-11-01-6ae925ca|Lovable updates — 2026-11-01]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now [download a software bill of materials (SBOM)](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/security-view.md#review-project-dependencies) for your project from the dependency list in the Security view, in SPDX 2.3 or CycloneDX 1.5 format. The file lists your production dependencies and their known vulnerabilities, so you can share it with a security or procurement team or load it into your own security tools. Downloading an SBOM is free.
>
> Source: https://docs.lovable.dev/changelog
