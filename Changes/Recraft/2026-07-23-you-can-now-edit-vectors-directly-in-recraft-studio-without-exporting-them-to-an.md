> You can now edit vectors directly in Recraft Studio, without exporting them to another tool.
>
> Tweak every point, curve, or color until it's just right. Then just export when you're ready.
>
> Double-click any shape in a vector image to start editing. You can:
>
> Move anchor points and adjust curves
>
> Recolor individual shapes
>
> Duplicate elements across frames
