# 4 other changes in Improved mockups controls

**Recraft** · 2025-11-11 · Other changes · Digest · [Official source](https://feedback.recraft.ai/changelog/improved-mockups-controls) · from [[Releases/Recraft/recraft-2025-11-11-69137f21c90acc97906944ff-8c24bf9d|Improved mockups controls]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> **Blend Mode**: define how your design interacts with lighting and surfaces. Use **Multiply** for darker blends, **Screen** for lighter ones, or **Overlay** for that printed-on look.

> **Show Texture** Toggle: keep or remove original material details for a realistic or clean finish.

> **Tiling**: create seamless repeating patterns.

> Mask Refinement Tools: fine-tune your mask with **Brush** and **Lasso** for precision, **Area** for quick clean-ups, or **Wand** for automatic selections.
