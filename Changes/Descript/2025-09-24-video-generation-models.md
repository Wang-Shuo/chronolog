# Video generation models

**Descript** · 2025-09-24 · [[Themes/video-generation|Video Generation]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupseptember-24th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-09-24-68d436f9a25ccc2773ae3774-f0a4507e|Release roundup—September 24th, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> The following video generation models are available:
>
> **Pixverse 4.5** - Default model for video generation
>
> **Hailuo 02** - Alternative video generation model
>
> **Veo3** - Higher quality video model
>
> **Veo3 [fast]** - Faster version of the Veo3 model
>
> **Wan v2.2 [turbo]** - Good for realism and dynamic movement
>
> You can select your generation models in the *Settings menu → Advanced* section. For users on the new plans, these different models have [different credit costs](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/27841674958221-Track-and-understand-your-Media-Minutes-and-AI-Credits). The model picker displays the price of each model with a tooltip containing additional information.
>
> *These settings are available exclusively for our new plans. Users on legacy plans will have to upgrade to get access to the new premium models. *
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupseptember-24th-2025
