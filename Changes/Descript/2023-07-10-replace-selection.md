# Replace selection**

**Descript** · 2023-07-10 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-68-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-07-10-64ac412e0a49ed588306f998-b4887a52|Version 68 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now [replace any selection in the Script with a new recording](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/17460399245453-Replace-selection). Whether you have text, an AI voice, or a past take, simply highlight it in your Script, then open the Recorder to replace the selection with a new take.
>
> The length of the scenes will be adjusted intelligently to match the new pacing. We’ll do our best to manage changes in the script so your scene placement and B-roll remain in the right spot for simple editing later.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-68-release
