# Embedded Video Authentication

**Descript** · 2024-07-02 · [[Themes/account-access|Account and Access]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-93) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2024-07-02-668418cb98cc7426793646c1-467c3990|Release Version 93]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> For users who embed [Descript share pages](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/15210580318349-View-share-and-manage-a-published-page) with Private view settings (aka “Drive access required”) - we now can detect whether the user is logged in to an authorized Descript account with Drive access. If the user playing the embedded video is logged in, the video will play as expected. If they are not logged in, they will be redirected to the main share page and prompted to login first.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-93
