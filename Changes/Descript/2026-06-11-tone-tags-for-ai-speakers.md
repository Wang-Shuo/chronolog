# Tone Tags for AI speakers

**Descript** · 2026-06-11 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-round-upjune-11th-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-06-11-6a2b3bab1b3cd1eee09fe520-f254003b|Release round-up—June 11th, 2026]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now add inline [tone directions](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10166020211085-Generate-text-to-speech-audio#tone-tags) to your AI speech scripts when using ElevenLabs V3 speakers. A Tone Tag is a small chip that appears directly in your transcript at the insertion point — it tells [ElevenLabs v3](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/17372069745293-AI-Speakers-overview) how to deliver the surrounding speech. Think whispers, laughs, pauses, excitement, or anything you want to describe in plain language.
>
> When you add or edit a tone tag, the surrounding paragraph regenerates automatically with the new tone applied. Paragraph breaks are the natural way to reset tone — the model determines how far the effect carries from each insertion point, so what you're really controlling is placement.
>
> Tone Tags are available for **ElevenLabs v3 speakers only**. On any other speaker, inline notes remain regular in-line notes.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-round-upjune-11th-2026
