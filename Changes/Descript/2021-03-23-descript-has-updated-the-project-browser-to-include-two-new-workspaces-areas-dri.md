> Descript has updated the Project Browser to include two new Workspaces areas: Drive Workspace and My Workspace. Workspaces provide a more clearly defined experience for storing Team and Personal projects.
>
> The Drive Workspace houses projects that are shared to all team members of a Drive, as well as collaborators with whom the project has been shared directly.
>
> My Workspace houses all of the projects that are exclusive to you.
