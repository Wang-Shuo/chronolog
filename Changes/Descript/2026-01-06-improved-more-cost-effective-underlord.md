# Improved, more cost-effective Underlord

**Descript** · 2026-01-06 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/announcingunderlord-v2) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-01-06-695d8c261791e8a1ac173e7a-d8a269af|Announcing—Underlord v2]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Since the beginning of the Beta, we’ve learned a lot from how [you’re using Underlord](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/36803785502221-Underlord-beta-Your-AI-co-editor-in-Descript). Your feedback has shown us both what's working and where we need to raise the bar.
>
> Today we’re to rolling out improvements to Underlord that make it faster, less expensive, and more capable across the board.
>
> You’ll notice more thoughtful edits and stronger responses when using:
>
> [Slide-to-video](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/37947106801165-Upload-a-slide-deck-PDF-into-Descript) workflows
>
> Time and duration-based editing
>
> [Image](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/17045119670029-Generate-AI-images) and [video](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/38621681243789-Generate-AI-video) generation
>
> Here are a few examples of the kinds of queries where Underlord has improved:
>
> *Can you create a visual for the highlighted section? Take the information he is saying and make a graph for the audience to understand.*
>
> *Add captions for the first 30 seconds of this video, and make the video fade out for the last 5 seconds.*
>
> *Turn these slides into a narrated video I can post on YouTube.*
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/announcingunderlord-v2
