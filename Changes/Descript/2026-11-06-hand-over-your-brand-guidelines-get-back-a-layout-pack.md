# Hand over your brand guidelines

**Descript** · 2026-11-06 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupnovember-6-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-11-06-6aee4d89b5b0a5f95c0b0cc2-f54c26f4|Release roundup—November 6, 2026]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> This is a big one. Building on-brand layouts by hand means hours of nudging text boxes. Now you give Descript your brand guidelines, your logo, and your colors, or just describe a look. Descript’s AI shows you four directions. Pick one, and it builds a full layout pack in your brand that everyone on your drive can use on every video.
>
> Every layout in the pack is editable, and you can ask Underlord to change any of them. Underlord also styles the scenes in your project for you. Tell it what you want, and it lays them out.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupnovember-6-2026
