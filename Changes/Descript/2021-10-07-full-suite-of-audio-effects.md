# Full suite of audio effects

**Descript** · 2021-10-07 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-24-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2021-10-07-6390c9cd4a4a0d64fa56472c-f35f9385|Version 24 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> In addition to Studio Sound, we're releasing over a dozen new [audio effects](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042638551) to give you the same level of mixing and creative control that you'd expect in a DAW.
>
> We have mixing effects, like a compressor, a limiter, a noise gate, and ducking. We have creative effects like reverb, delay, pitch shifting, distortion, and a flanger. Each effect has easy to use presets, or if you know what you're doing, you can drill in and tweak the knobs and dials to your liking.
>
> Descript had a few effects before (compressor and equalizer), but with these effects, podcasters have the full set of tools necessary to get whatever sound they want.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-24-release
