# Welsh transcription now available in Beta

**Descript** · 2025-10-23 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupoctober-23rd-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-10-23-68faa249aae7b47cd5e72ea1-b70e43a4|Release roundup—October 23rd, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now [transcribe audio and video](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10249408168845-Supported-transcription-languages) in Welsh. To use it, manually select Welsh from the language dropdown when transcribing your file.
>
> Since this is still in beta, Welsh transcription will not be detected automatically if you use the "Auto detect language" option. You'll need to select Welsh manually each time you transcribe.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupoctober-23rd-2025
