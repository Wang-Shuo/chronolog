# Studio Sound 1.0

**Descript** · 2021-10-07 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-24-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2021-10-07-6390c9cd4a4a0d64fa56472c-f35f9385|Version 24 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> If you've yet to hear about [Studio Sound](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405856365709), it's Descript's AI-based take on noise reduction and audio enhancement. We released it in beta last month, and many people (including us, actually) were surprised by how good it is.
>
> Today, Studio Sound is graduating from beta, and we're adding a few handy improvements. First, you can turn it on as a default setting for new recordings, either in the editor or in Descript's screen recorder. Second, we added an intensity dial, so you can fine tune the strength of the effect. Finally, you can control it from the normal effects menu in the inspector, so you can tweak it without leaving your Composition.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-24-release
