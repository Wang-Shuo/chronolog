# New in Rooms—Role specific invite links

**Descript** · 2025-10-23 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupoctober-23rd-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-10-23-68faa249aae7b47cd5e72ea1-b70e43a4|Release roundup—October 23rd, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’ve made it easier to setup your Rooms session, before you ever start recording. You can now create [invite links](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/30293678303885-Create-a-Rooms-session-and-invite-participants) that automatically assign participants a specific role when they join your Room. This means you can send different links to different people based on what they need to do in the session and pairs well with Control Room—available exclusively to users on Business and Enterprise plans.
>
> When you click the Invite button, select a role from the dropdown:
>
> **Guest** - Recorded - No controls (default)
>
> **Co-host** - Recorded - Shared control
>
> **Producer** - Not recorded - Full control (Business and Enterprise only)
>
> **Viewer** - Not recorded - No controls (Creator, Business, and Enterprise only)
>
> Each role has its own unique invite link that doesn't expire. When someone joins from a role-specific link, they'll automatically have that role assigned.
>
> This is useful if you have a fixed cast or want to share a link with viewers who shouldn't be recorded. You can still manually change someone's role when approving their join request if needed.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupoctober-23rd-2025
