> There’s a new option in settings that allows you to [define a default](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10164803814285-Speakers) AI speaker in your projects. When you enter Write mode in a blank project, it will use this AI Speaker by default. This is a great time saver if you're starting your projects in Write mode and typically use the same AI voice.
