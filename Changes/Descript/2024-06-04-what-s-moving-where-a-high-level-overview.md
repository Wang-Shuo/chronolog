# What’s Moving Where?

**Descript** · 2024-06-04 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/descript-season-6-release-version-91) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2024-06-04-665e365f94fa1d97d3fc73dc-63f9523b|Descript Season 6 - Release Version 91]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> The first thing to notice is that the Top Toolbar has been cleaned up, and many of the most useful features have migrated to the new and improved sidebar menu. The goal of all of these updates is to make it faster and easier to find the tool you need when you need it.
>
> We’ve added a Home button to the top bar that takes you back to Drive view.
>
> The D-logo menu has been replaced with a hamburger menu, but the contents are the same.
>
> The project management drop-down and Composition drop-down remain on the Top Bar but are centered.
>
> The Media Library, Record button, Text, Shapes, and Templates are all now on the righthand sidebar.
>
> Media is now the [Project panel](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10285678701453) and contains all of your project files, Compositions, AI Speaker files, and Sequences.
>
> The Action bar is now all contained with the [Underlord panel](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/20531819066509) and includes classic tools like Studio Sound, and Green screen, as well as a host of AI tools to enhance your workflow.
>
> [Scene properties](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10327905838349) has its own icon on the sidebar where you can apply templates, backgrounds, effects, and transitions directly to a scene.
>
> [Layer properties](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/27197208246797) has its own icon where you can apply effects, adjust layouts, add animations, and more.
>
> The [Record panel](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10165881574413) works exactly as it did in the past, now on the righthand sidebar.
>
> The new [Elements panel](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10256426025101) combines shapes, dynamic text, waveform/progress bar animations, annotations, overlays, and frames.
>
> [Captions](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/12878201987597) is now a separate panel, with new styles, and a current scene/all scene toggle
>
> Stock Media has been separated into the visual [Media panel](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/27204675401485) and audio SFX and Music in the [Music panel](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/27204810040973).
>
> The [Jump to Marker](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10164735239693) button has moved from the top of the Script down to the Marker icon next to the Play controls panel (just above the Timeline.)
>
> For more information on the new layout, [click here](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/27224628105485).
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/descript-season-6-release-version-91
