# Announcing—the Descript Customer Obsession Hackathon Telethon (May 14–15)

**Descript** · 2026-04-16 · [[Themes/collaboration|Collaboration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupapril-16th-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-04-16-69e11a9bcbb8c43b6e38c002-bf103ab2|Release roundup—April 16th, 2026]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’re hosting our first-ever [Customer Obsessed Telethon](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/VxqCPd0681I?feature=share)—a 2‑day, live, telethon‑style hackathon where we’ll be building alongside real customer feedback in real time.
>
> Have a “biggest little thing” you want added or improved?
>
> Drop it (or upvote an existing idea) on our [official Telethon board.](https://descript.canny.io/telethon) You have until April 30th to submit your ideas. **We’re especially looking for:**
>
> Small problems with big impacts: *“I should be able to edit Comments” / “Allow me to save custom effects presets”*
>
> Issues that slow you down day-to-day
>
> The hackathon-telethon begins May 14th.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupapril-16th-2026
