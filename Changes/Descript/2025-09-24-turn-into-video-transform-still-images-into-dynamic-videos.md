# Turn into video—Transform still Images into dynamic videos

**Descript** · 2025-09-24 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupseptember-24th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-09-24-68d436f9a25ccc2773ae3774-f0a4507e|Release roundup—September 24th, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now bring your images to life right from the Scene editor (formerly known as the Canvas.)
>
> With the brand-new [Turn into video](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/38621681243789-Generate-AI-video) button, you can instantly transform any image in your project into a vibrant, dynamic video clip—no extra tools needed. In just a few clicks, your still images become animated visuals, unlocking fresh creative possibilities and adding motion to your storytelling.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupseptember-24th-2025
