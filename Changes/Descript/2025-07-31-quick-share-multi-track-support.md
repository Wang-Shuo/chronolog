# Quick Share & multi-track support

**Descript** · 2025-07-31 · [[Themes/collaboration|Collaboration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupjuly-31st-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-07-31-688bbf021a0bafe843472e0e-c36eb6f1|Release Roundup—July 31st, 2025]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Recordings can now publish instantly to a Descript share page as soon as you stop recording.
>
> With Quick Share **on**: recordings are auto-published and combined into a single track
>
> With it **off**: your recording opens in the editor, and each input gets its own track
>
