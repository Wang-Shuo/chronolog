# **Working with ranges & clips**

**Descript** · 2023-04-17 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-62-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-04-17-643dae58600c4309156766d8-73e550bd|Version 62 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Working with ranges & clips**: You can now adjust duration and audio for any range or clip selection from the main property panel. We’ve removed the clip list in favor of a new **Playback** section, where you can now find controls to adjust duration, speed and looping for your current selection. To adjust the start time of a clip, we now recommend [using the **Slip** tool in the timeline](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10249344527501-Timeline-tools#slip-tool) (which you can also access temporarily by holding down the **Y** key while dragging a clip).
>
