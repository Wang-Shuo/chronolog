# New Transcription Flow

**Descript** · 2021-07-13 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-18-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2021-07-13-6390c9b44a4a0d64fa56442c-f4a52cf2|Version 18 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We've updated the transcription flow to make automatic transcription even easier! Now you can add your files directly to a blank composition and your files will begin transcribing immediately. You can also add Speaker Labels directly in the confirmation box and create a Sequence.
>
> Users wishing to initiate [White Glove transcription](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042640111-White-Glove-Human-powered-transcription-with-Descript), [sync a pre-existing transcript](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042210632-Importing-and-syncing-a-transcript-you-already-have-to-your-audio), or initiate [Speaker Detective](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042639331-Speaker-Detective) can do so in the file Inspector sidebar on the right.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-18-release
