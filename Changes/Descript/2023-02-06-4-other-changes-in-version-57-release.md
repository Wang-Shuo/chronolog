<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Scene thumbnails have moved from the Script to the new Scene Rail, which makes it easier to quickly navigate, rearrange, and interact with scenes in your compositions

> Added ability to right-click a scene in the Scene Rail and 'Save to template'

> Layers now snap to other layers when dragged, resized, and rotated

> [Basic seat limitations](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10163662152589-Drive-membership-types#basic-member) are now fully enforced in Descript
