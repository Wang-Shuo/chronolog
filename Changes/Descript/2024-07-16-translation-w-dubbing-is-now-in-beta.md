# Translation w/ dubbing is now in Beta

**Descript** · 2024-07-16 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-94) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2024-07-16-6696a8f5b4bfbbdd0e357e35-249bec50|Release Version 94]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> The [Translation Beta](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/27177566394509-Translate) is growing, and you can now Translate the audio in your projects to another language in just a few clicks. When you open Underlord and select translate, there is a new toggle labeled Dub speech that will replace the audio track with your selected AI Speaker in your selected language. While Captions are available in all currently supported languages, Translation with dubbing is limited to English, French, German, Italian and Spanish for now.
>
> Expand your audience and impress your friends by giving it a try.
>
> Note: this feature is rolling out to users over the course of a few days, so if you do not see this option yet, check back later this week.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-94
