# **Using** **Overdub & Regenerate with Videos**

**Descript** · 2023-08-22 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-71) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-08-22-64e51d957de238097a7bdd9c-4db80053|Release Version 71]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Using** **Overdub & Regenerate with Videos**: [Overdub](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/17372069745293-Overdub-overview) and [Regenerate](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/17676714027533-Regenerate-beta-) no longer create [Sequences](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10256430454925-Sequence-overview) when used on a video, and instead move the original video out into a new layer in the composition. This allows you to adjust the original video with the generated audio right from the composition timeline without needing to enter a Sequence.
>
