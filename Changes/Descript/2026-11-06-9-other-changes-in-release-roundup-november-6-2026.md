# 9 other changes in Release roundup—November 6, 2026

**Descript** · 2026-11-06 · Other changes · Digest · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupnovember-6-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-11-06-6aee4d89b5b0a5f95c0b0cc2-f54c26f4|Release roundup—November 6, 2026]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Volume keyframes at the start and end of a clip aren't hidden behind the trim handles anymore.

> The timeline no longer flickers when you scroll.

> Healthy WebM, MKV, and image files no longer get a compatibility warning.

> A copied layer now pastes into a shorter scene.

> Create Clips no longer comes back empty when your script has markers or AI speech tone tags.

> Publishing to Captivate no longer fails with "alignmentFailed."

> Japanese dubs no longer fail with a server error.

> Rooms: echo cancellation removes the echo, the host's recording captures the mic channel that's actually live, and tracks line up after recording.

> Rooms upload status stays accurate when a host switches someone to Producer or Viewer mid-upload.

Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupnovember-6-2026
