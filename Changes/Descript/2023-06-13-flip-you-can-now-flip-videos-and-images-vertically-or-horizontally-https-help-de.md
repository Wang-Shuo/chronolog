# **Flip**: You can now [flip videos and images vertically…

**Descript** · 2023-06-13 · [[Themes/file-data|Files and Data]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-66-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-06-13-6487c2cf2398630910585901-ad99a9ee|Version 66 Release]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Flip**: You can now [flip videos and images vertically or horizontally](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10165302607757-Canvas-tools). Click on a layer in the canvas, then you’ll see options to Flip Horizontal and Flip Vertical in the canvas toolbar, next to the Crop icon. Alternatively, you can achieve the same effect by setting negative values for the width and height in the properties panel.
>
