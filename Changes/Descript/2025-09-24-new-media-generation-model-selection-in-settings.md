# New media generation model selection in Settings

**Descript** · 2025-09-24 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupseptember-24th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-09-24-68d436f9a25ccc2773ae3774-f0a4507e|Release roundup—September 24th, 2025]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> For users on the new plan types, we’ve added new premium, supercharged [generative model options](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10606771145869-App-settings) to the settings menu.
>
> These models offer higher quality output, delivering significantly improved quality, particularly with video models like *Veo3*, and image models like *Nano Banana*. They can also provide better character consistency across image generations, maintaining appearance and identity across different angles and scenes.
