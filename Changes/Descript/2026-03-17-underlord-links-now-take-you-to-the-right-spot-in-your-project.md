# Underlord links now take you to the right spot in your project

**Descript** · 2026-03-17 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupmarch-17th-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-03-17-69b3257b60b225e10f6a3a28-246aeffc|Release roundup—March 17th, 2026]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> [Underlord](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/36803785502221-Underlord-beta-Your-AI-co-editor-in-Descript) now does a better job of linking from chat back into your project, so it’s easier to review what it changed or jump to what it found.
>
> When Underlord references something in your project, it can now link you directly to specific places like:
>
> A composition
>
> A timestamp or time range
>
> This helps when you ask Underlord to make edits and want to quickly confirm the result without searching through your timeline.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupmarch-17th-2026
