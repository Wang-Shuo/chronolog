# White Glove Transcription Sunset

**Descript** · 2024-01-10 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Policy · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-80) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2024-01-10-659d7ecd3c8af3aabfb226c7-4effc43f|Release Version 80]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> As of March 4th, 2024, we will be sunsetting the human-powered White Glove transcription feature. We’ve seen automated transcription become increasingly powerful over the years, and we’d like to keep investing our team's resources into making it even better.
>
> Starting in v80, new users will no longer see the feature, but existing users will still have access until the sunset date. If this is a feature you rely on, we highly recommend working directly with Rev, who has been our White Glove transcription provider. You can [import](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10249406128781-Importing-an-existing-transcript) human-powered transcripts into Descript with just a few clicks.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-80
