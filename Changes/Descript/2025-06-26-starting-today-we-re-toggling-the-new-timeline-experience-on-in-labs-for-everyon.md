# Starting today, we’re toggling the new timeline experience ON in Labs for everyone

**Descript** · 2025-06-26 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/the-new-timeline-is-on) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-06-26-685dcfebdae93acd4370d711-3b49d7b8|The new timeline is ON]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Starting today, we’re toggling the new timeline experience ON in Labs for everyone.
>
> If you haven’t had a chance to try it yet: We've completely redesigned the timeline to make video editing simpler and more intuitive. The new timeline gives you a clearer view of your video and makes it faster to trim, split, and add scenes directly from the timeline.
>
> To open the new timeline, hover over the solid grey bar above the play button and use your mouse to drag it *up*.
>
> This update comes with so many small changes and improvements we recommend that you view the video above as well our [help center article](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10249275208717-Timeline-overview) to get acquainted.
>
