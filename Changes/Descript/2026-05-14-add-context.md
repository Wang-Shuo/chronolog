<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> [Underlord](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/36803785502221) now lets you pin specific parts of your project directly to a request. Click the **@-button** in the Underlord chat panel to open the context picker and attach files, scenes, timestamps, or individual layers before you describe what you want done.
>
> This is most useful in complex projects where Underlord might otherwise have trouble knowing what you're referring to. Instead of describing the scene, just attach it.
>
