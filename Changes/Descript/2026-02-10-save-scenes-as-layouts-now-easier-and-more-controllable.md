# Save scenes as Layouts - now easier and more controllable

**Descript** · 2026-02-10 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupfebruary-10th-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-02-10-698b53cfff8de99c84c132f7-0801d6da|Release roundup—February 10th, 2026]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You now have more control when saving scenes as layouts. When you click "[Save as new layout](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10255964343053-Save-your-own-layouts#save-scene-layout)" from the scene sidebar or right-click menu, a new panel lets you choose what to do with each layer:
>
> **Keep as is** — The layer stays unchanged in your layout
>
> **Don't include** — The layer will be removed from the layout
>
> **Turn into placeholder** — Replaces the layer with an appropriate placeholder based on its type
>
> When you turn a layer into a placeholder:
>
> Text layers enable [Smart Fill](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10255964343053-Save-your-own-layouts#smart-fill)
>
> Image layers become Media [placeholders](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/32694053567629-Placeholder-layers)
>
> Video layers become the appropriate [placeholder](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/32694053567629-Placeholder-layers) based on their content (like Screen for screen recordings)
>
> *Note: Audio layers can only be kept as is or removed—they can't be converted to placeholders.*
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupfebruary-10th-2026
