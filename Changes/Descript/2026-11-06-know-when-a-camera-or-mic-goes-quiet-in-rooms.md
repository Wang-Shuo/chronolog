# Know when a camera or mic goes quiet in Rooms

**Descript** · 2026-11-06 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupnovember-6-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-11-06-6aee4d89b5b0a5f95c0b0cc2-f54c26f4|Release roundup—November 6, 2026]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Usually you find out about a dead mic after the call. Now Rooms warns you while you record. If a camera or screen share stays black for 10 seconds, or a mic is nearly silent for 30, you get a warning. Hosts and producers see warnings for everyone in the room. Recording keeps going either way.
