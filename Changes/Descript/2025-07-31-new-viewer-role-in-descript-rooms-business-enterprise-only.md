# New Viewer role in Descript Rooms (Business & Enterprise only)

**Descript** · 2025-07-31 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupjuly-31st-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-07-31-688bbf021a0bafe843472e0e-c36eb6f1|Release Roundup—July 31st, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’ve added a Viewer role to Descript Rooms, available on Business and Enterprise Drives.
>
> [Viewers](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/38049993092749-Roles-in-Descript-Rooms) join the session in a dedicated Viewer Room, where they can observe the Rooms session without being seen, heard, or recorded. While Viewers don’t interact with Producers or manage tech, they do count toward the Control Room participant limit.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupjuly-31st-2025
