# **Drive Invite links**

**Descript** · 2023-06-13 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-66-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-06-13-6487c2cf2398630910585901-ad99a9ee|Version 66 Release]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Drive Invite links**: We've added Drive invite links so that you can [add team members without having to manually enter each email address](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10163704593293-Inviting-drive-members#Drive-invite-links). You will have one link for Editors and another link for Basic seats. Admins or editors can reset or disable links at any time. And remember, if you want to manually approve all new editors joining your drive, make sure this is enabled in the Members section of your drive settings.
>
