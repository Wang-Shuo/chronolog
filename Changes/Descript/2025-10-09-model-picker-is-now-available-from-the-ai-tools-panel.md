# Model picker is now available from the AI tools panel

**Descript** · 2025-10-09 · [[Themes/video-generation|Video Generation]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupoctober-9th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-10-09-68e7d9ed49008132ccb9626b-3840242a|Release roundup—October 9th, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> For users on our new plan types, we’ve added the model picker button directly to the AI tools panel when you select either [Generate video](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/38621681243789-Generate-AI-video), or [Generate an image](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/17045119670029-Generate-AI-images).
>
> When using image or video generation in Descript, you'll now see a Cube icon that allows you to select different AI models for your generation tasks. This gives you the flexibility to choose the model that best suits your specific needs.
>
> Different models offer different strengths:
>
> Choose faster models when you need quick results
>
> Choose cheaper models when you need to experiment
>
> Pick the right balance between speed, quality, and resource usage based on your project needs
>
> The [model picker](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/39869916772621-Generative-image-and-video-models-in-Descript) puts more control in your hands. If you’re still on a Legacy plan, you’ll eventually be moved to a new plan type, or you can switch early to take advantage of the model picker, and our new top ups.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupoctober-9th-2025
