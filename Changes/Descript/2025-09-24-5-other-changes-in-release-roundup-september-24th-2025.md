# 5 other changes in Release roundup—September 24th, 2025

**Descript** · 2025-09-24 · Other changes · Digest · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupseptember-24th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-09-24-68d436f9a25ccc2773ae3774-f0a4507e|Release roundup—September 24th, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> Click the Underlord icon

> **Resolution Controls**: Select your preferred camera resolution directly from the pre-Room lobby page before joining a room and from the camera settings menu during your call.

> **Host Controls**: Room hosts can now adjust resolution settings for guests, giving you more control over recording quality.

> **Quality Notification**: When a host changes your resolution, you'll receive a notification to keep you informed.

> Added an error message for Veo3 failure state

Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupseptember-24th-2025
