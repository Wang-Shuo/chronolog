# **Removal of Pages tab:** With this update we've removed the "Pages" tab in the Drive View

**Descript** · 2022-06-02 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-40-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2022-06-02-6390c9ff4a4a0d64fa564d5f-b80871d8|Version 40 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Removal of Pages tab:** With this update we've removed the "Pages" tab in the Drive View. Previous updates have created a 1:1 relationship between published Pages and their associated Compositions, so you can now [access your Published page content](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042208912) (including the associated URL) by opening it's source project and clicking on the **Publish** button in the Composition.
>
