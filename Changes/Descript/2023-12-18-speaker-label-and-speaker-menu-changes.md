# Speaker Label and Speaker Menu changes

**Descript** · 2023-12-18 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-7910) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-12-18-658093f19d17b2db493d619c-9a7277e4|Release Version 79.1.0]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Speaker labels have darker text and an input outline for added clarity.
>
> The speaker label menu is now separated into speakers already *In this project* and *Add AI speaker to project* which populates with your trained AI Speakers or frequently used Stock AI Speakers.
>
> Any speaker with a trained AI voice will be labeled *AI.* This includes AI Speakers you’ve trained, as well as our Stock AI Speakers.
>
> Speakers trained with the Legacy Overdub model will be labeled *Legacy AI.*
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-7910
