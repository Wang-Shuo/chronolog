# Lip sync for translated videos

**Descript** · 2025-12-04 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupdecember-4th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-12-04-6930e3d143084a3405d0352d-695ede77|Release roundup—December 4th, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> When you translate and dub a video you can [now apply lip sync to match](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/38973059333133-Make-dubbed-speech-look-authentic-with-lip-sync) the speaker's mouth movements to the translated audio. This makes translated videos look more natural and seamless.
>
> Translate your video and select the dubbing option
>
> Lip sync is only available on our [updated plans](https://www.descript.com/pricing) using AI credits.
>
> **Note:** Lip sync only applies to faces detected in your video's main camera layers—it won't affect screen recordings or b-roll footage.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupdecember-4th-2025
