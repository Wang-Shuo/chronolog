# **Note:** Windows 7 reached "end of life" status (EOL / unsupported) by Microsoft…

**Descript** · 2022-11-12 · [[Themes/reliability-performance|Reliability and Performance]] · Policy · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/patch-release-5114-windows-only) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2022-11-12-6390ca224a4a0d64fa5651b3-44453883|Patch release 51.1.4 (Windows only)]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Note:** Windows 7 reached "end of life" status (EOL / unsupported) by Microsoft on January 14, 2020 and Windows 8.1 will reach EOL on January 10, 2023. As such, we no longer officially certify these two operating systems for use with Descript and they will not be supported moving forward.
>
> We anticipate that over time Descript performance and or functionality may degrade or become inoperable should users continue to use these operating systems. Please see our [System Requirements](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042640451-System-Requirements) article for more details.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/patch-release-5114-windows-only
