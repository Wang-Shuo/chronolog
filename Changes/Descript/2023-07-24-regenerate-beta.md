# Regenerate (beta)

**Descript** · 2023-07-24 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-69-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-07-24-64beb9832d8629095cea0062-3cd6b9ab|Version 69 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We are excited to release [Regenerate](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/17676714027533-Regenerate-beta-), our new cutting-edge AI audio editing feature that seamlessly corrects mismatched edits, revives lackluster dialogue, and erases annoying background noises in just one click. By perfectly replicating any voice and its recording environment, Regenerate ensures that your edits sound natural and smooth.
>
> **This will be rolling out to everyone over the coming days**, and once you get access follow these simple steps to use Regenerate:
>
> Hover your mouse between an edit point and click on the bandaid icon. Or, highlight the section of your script that needs fixing and click on Replace > Regenerate.
>
> Choose to play back the new audio, Regenerate again for a different performance, keep the AI-generated voice converting it to an audio file, or restore to the state before Regenerate.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-69-release
