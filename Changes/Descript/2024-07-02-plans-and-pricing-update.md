# Plans and Pricing Update

**Descript** · 2024-07-02 · [[Themes/pricing-model|Pricing Model]] · Pricing · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-93) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2024-07-02-668418cb98cc7426793646c1-467c3990|Release Version 93]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> As of July 2nd, 2024, new plan tiers and plan names are going into effect. You can view full details on our [Pricing](https://www.descript.com/pricing) page, but here are the important parts:
>
> If you are already on a [plan](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10255874128397-How-billing-works-in-Descript), your features will remain unchanged unless you cancel your subscription or change your plan. These old plans will be referred to as **Legacy** plans to keep them separated from the new pricing model.
>
> The new plan names are Hobbyist (taking the place of our old Creator Plan,) Creator (taking the place of our old Pro plan,) and a new Business plan which is designed for small to medium teams. It has the highest level of feature support outside of customized Enterprise plans.
>
> There are new limits on AI features, storage, file upload and more. These limits are broken down in detail by category on our pricing page. Again, if you are already on a plan, you will keep your existing plan limits. The only exception to that is that the maximum file upload size is now 50GB, even if you are on a Legacy plan.
>
> Basic seats: unless you are on a [Legacy plan,](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/27653385141517-Understanding-Your-Legacy-Plan) basic seats are now limited to Business plan users. All other plans can add View members who can View and Comment on projects in the Drive only.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-93
