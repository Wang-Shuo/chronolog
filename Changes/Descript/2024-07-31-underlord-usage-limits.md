# Underlord usage limits

**Descript** · 2024-07-31 · [[Themes/pricing-model|Pricing Model]] · Policy · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-95) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2024-07-31-66a9024dc946150601c712a1-ebef7dcf|Release Version 95]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Users on Free, Hobbyist, and EDU plans will encounter some new metering and enforced limits on Underlord actions in accordance with our [recent pricing updates](https://www.descript.com/pricing).
>
> When you use an [AI suite](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/16987605355277-Descript-for-Web-and-Desktop-Preview) feature (i.e. Underlord) you’ll see a counter at the top of the menu showing the number of uses you have remaining. We’ll also flash a warning when you get close to using up your allotment.
>
> Remove Filler Words and Shorten Word Gaps work on a per-composition basis. So once you’re unlocked it with a use, you’ll still be able to access and action the search results in that composition indefinitely.
>
> Note: These limits do not effect users on Legacy plans, or users on Creator plans and above.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-95
