# 7 other changes in Version 52 Release

**Descript** · 2022-11-15 · Other changes · Digest · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-52-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2022-11-15-6390ca244a4a0d64fa5651e3-75c38e8c|Version 52 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> [Introducing Scenes](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10248939749517-Scenes-overview) Use the / key to break your script into Scenes, and then arrange visuals the same way you'd work with slides

> [**A complete redesign**](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10328253083661-What-s-changed-in-Descript) We reorganized Descript to be as intuitive and accessible as possible

> [Templates](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10255950033805-Templates-overview) Select from our gallery of great-looking layouts, title sequences, and social clips or make your own

> [**Color control**](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10327606944653-Color-adjustments) Set exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, saturation, temperature, white balance, and skin tone

> [**Visual effects**](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10327783443213-Applying-and-adjusting-effects) Use blur, pixelate, film grain, and chroma key

> [More transitions and animations](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10255987947917-Animations-overview) Storyboard includes 6 new transition types and a more user-friendly interface for animating properties

> [Writing tool](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10164874717709-Script-tools) Use Overdub to write your script in Descript, laying out visuals as you go. Then choose one of our ultra realistic text-to-speech voices, or swap it out with your own recording

Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-52-release
