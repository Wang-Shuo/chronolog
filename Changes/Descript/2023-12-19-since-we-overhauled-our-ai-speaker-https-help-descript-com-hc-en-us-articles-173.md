# New AI Stock Speakers

**Descript** · 2023-12-19 · [[Themes/search-browsing|Search and Browsing]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/new-ai-stock-speakers) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-12-19-6581e51aa8c4db9cd52a3dae-6cd0b0c1|New AI Stock Speakers]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Since we overhauled our [AI Speaker](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/17372069745293-AI-Speakers-overview) model earlier this year we thought it was time that we also rebuilt our Stock Speakers library and retrained the old Stock Speakers on the new and improved model. You can find these voices under the **Speaker menu → Browse AI Speakers**.
>
> Starting today the Stock Speakers menu has been populated with 12 brand new speakers and 8 of the original Stock Speakers that have been retrained.
>
> This is just the beginning, so we’ve added a search bar to help you find the right speaker for your project, including an overall style, accent, vocal range (masculine/feminine), and age.
>
> If you’re an existing user looking for the old voices you can find them by toggling the filter setting to **Show.** These versions have not been retrained.
>
> This is a major improvement in our [AI Stock Speaker](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10165983351565-AI-stock-voices) options, and there's more to come, so we’re excited for you to give them a try.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/new-ai-stock-speakers
