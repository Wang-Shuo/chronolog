# New AI Action: Find Good Clips

**Descript** · 2023-11-30 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/new-ai-action-find-good-clips) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-11-30-6568ccbe7ece8b4ef3dbc42e-85433d79|New AI Action: Find Good Clips]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> After a long session of recording, producing, and editing the last thing you want to do is spend time trying to find the best moments to market your content.
>
> [Find Good Clips](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/20531819066509-AI-Actions-beta-#:~:text=a%20blank%20script.-,Find%20Good%20Clips,-Effortlessly%20generate%20concise) hunts down the juiciest, most concise, and shareable delicacies from your project to streamline the creation of promotional video, YouTube shorts, or anything else for your highlight reel.
>
> Sidestep the hours of manual editing and let the AI spotlight the best moments of your content.
>
> *Note: this AI action can take a few minutes, especially on longer scripts.*
>
> Just click the Action Bar, Ask AI, and select Find Good Clips:
>
> When the Action is complete, click Select in Script:
>
> And finally, you can Duplicate them to a new composition:
>
> With Find Good Clips creating captivating short clips has never been easier.
>
> Additionally, for a more directed search, you can try **Find when...** to locate specific topics or themes in the project.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/new-ai-action-find-good-clips
