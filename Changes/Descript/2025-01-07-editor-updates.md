# Editor updates

**Descript** · 2025-01-07 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-105) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-01-07-677d554eb70df614fe452c9a-10738fe7|Release Version 105]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Audio sliders are now represented by a percentage (%) value rather than a dB (decibel) value by default. This should be clearer for users without a background in audio production. r → View → Show volume as decibels.
>
> For users who prefer a decibel meter, you can toggle that on under the in-project app menu in the upper left corner.
>
> The forward and back buttons in the [playback control](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10164534109837-Playback-and-navigation) panel will now navigate between scenes, rather than skipping between Markers.
>
> The option to *Apply properties to all scenes* has been removed from the 3 dot menu on the layer and [scene](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10248991554957-Scene-properties) panel in favor of the more obvious top bar Current Scene / All Scenes button on both the canvas and layer panel.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-105
