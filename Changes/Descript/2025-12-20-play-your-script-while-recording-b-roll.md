# Play your script while recording B-roll

**Descript** · 2025-12-20 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupdecember-20th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-12-20-6945e963a88aa53ad74ae6ad-f9e37c73|Release roundup—December 20th, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now play your script while [recording screen or camera layers on top of it](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10165881574413-How-to-use-the-Editor-Recorder-in-Descript#editor-recorder-settings). This makes it easier to record b-roll that syncs with your existing Script track.
>
> Enable "Play script audio while recording layer" in your recording settings
>
> Start recording—your script will play while you capture your screen or camera
>
> *Note: Live playback won't activate if the layer you're recording already contains audio to avoid feedback and echo.*
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupdecember-20th-2025
