# Editor improvements

**Descript** · 2021-10-07 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-24-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2021-10-07-6390c9cd4a4a0d64fa56472c-f35f9385|Version 24 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We redesigned the inspector with a [Clip Inspector](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410440488077) and [Track Inspector](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410455001101). As you're clicking around your script, you'll either see an inspector for the relevant clip(s), or if you click on a boundary between clips, you'll see a transition inspector, which you can use to add fades and crossfades. This makes it easier to control your Composition without needing to switch to the timeline view.
>
> You can also change the length of a track directly from the script - you can see the endpoint, and just click and drag to move it. You can also change the length of gaps between words from the toolbar.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-24-release
