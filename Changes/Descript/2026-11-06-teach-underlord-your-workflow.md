# Teach Underlord your workflow

**Descript** · 2026-11-06 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupnovember-6-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-11-06-6aee4d89b5b0a5f95c0b0cc2-f54c26f4|Release roundup—November 6, 2026]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Typing the same instructions into Underlord every time gets old. A skill is a set of instructions Underlord can reuse. Type / in the Underlord chat to pick one of Descript's skills, or choose Create skill to write your own. When an edit goes the way you wanted, save that conversation as a skill. You can use more than one skill in a message, or add your own notes to one.
>
> Skills replace prompt templates. Templates you saved yourself won't turn into skills on their own. To keep one, go to Settings → Skills → View templates and choose Save as skill. Templates switch off on November 30.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupnovember-6-2026
