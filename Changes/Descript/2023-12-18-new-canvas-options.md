# New Canvas Options

**Descript** · 2023-12-18 · [[Themes/file-data|Files and Data]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-7910) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-12-18-658093f19d17b2db493d619c-9a7277e4|Release Version 79.1.0]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> There’s a new drop-down menu on the Sidebar that allows rounding all four corners separately with a different adjustable radius for each corner. When you have multiple corners adjusted but the dropdown is closed, it will show a Mixed status.
>
> The [canvas toolbar](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10165302607757-Canvas-tools) also has some new options to adjust the position of media in the frame, including a Top or Bottom, Left or Right, and a Fill Canvas option. This last option is great for layers that may have a slightly different aspect ratio than your main [Script.](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/20531819066509-AI-Actions-overview-beta-)
>
