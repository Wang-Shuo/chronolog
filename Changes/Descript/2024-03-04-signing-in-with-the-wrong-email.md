# Signing in with the Wrong Email

**Descript** · 2024-03-04 · [[Themes/account-access|Account and Access]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-84) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2024-03-04-65e60ab1e3ea7de031601850-e6977776|Release Version 84]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We have resolved a problem that some users have encountered: if you sign in with the wrong email address, the app will treat it like a new user signup and you have to click through the onboarding steps including a plan level. This was confusing users who had already purchased a plan. While you could always select Free, then logout and log back in with the correct email address - we’ve added a Switch Account option to the drop-down in the upper right corner as an escape hatch if you end up signing in with a new email address by mistake.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-84
