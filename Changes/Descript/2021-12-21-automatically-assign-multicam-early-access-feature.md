# Automatically assign multicam (early access feature)

**Descript** · 2021-12-21 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-29-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2021-12-21-6390c9dd4a4a0d64fa56493c-14de707a|Version 29 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> With the click of a button Descript can now automatically set your camera view to the active speaker.
>
> Studio Sound: now faster and better
>
> We launched a new version of Studio Sound which sounds better while also taking less time to process your recordings. You'll particularly notice that new Studio Sound recordings have fewer artifacts and maintain the speaker identity better. To re-apply Studio Sound to an existing recording, [replace the file](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042639371-Replace-File) in your project with the same original file, and then apply Studio Sound to it.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-29-release
