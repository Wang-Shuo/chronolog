# Improved edit reviews

**Descript** · 2026-05-14 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/the-descript-telethon-begins-but-first-releases) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-05-14-6a05e75cbbef22ce4b0633c9-b87fafc2|The Descript Telethon begins, but first... releases]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We've added an automatic review step after every [Underlord](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/36803785502221) editing turn. After Underlord makes a change, a second pass checks the diff against what you actually asked for, looking specifically for things like unintended deletions, edits that missed their target, overcorrections, or operations that went out of scope. If something looks wrong, Underlord is re-invoked automatically to fix it.
>
> You don't interact with this directly—it runs in the background. The practical effect is fewer cases where Underlord does something technically correct but contextually wrong.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/the-descript-telethon-begins-but-first-releases
