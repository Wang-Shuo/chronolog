# Ducking is now “Lower audio of other layers”

**Descript** · 2025-07-15 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupjuly-15th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-07-15-6876a4379c30e0e186778b10-1f0cb94b|Release Roundup—July 15th, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We've renamed the Ducking audio effect to [Lower audio of other layers](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10327507829773-Lower-audio-of-other-layers) to make it more intuitive and easier to understand, especially for new users.
>
> Along with this name change, we've also made an important workflow improvement: this effect is no longer applied by default to any layer. This change helps ensure that audio mixing remains more intentional and predictable in your projects.
>
> These updates are part of our ongoing effort to make Descript's features more accessible while giving you greater control over your audio mixing process.
>
> **Other editor updates**:
>
> We now support emojis in text layers when you publish your project. In the past these could cause errors.
>
> If you add an Avatar to a speaker in a project with a screen recording in the Script, or without a Script track, we’ll now automatically put the Avatar in a speaker bubble.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupjuly-15th-2025
