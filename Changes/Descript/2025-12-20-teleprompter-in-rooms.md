**Descript** · 2025-12-20 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupdecember-20th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-12-20-6945e963a88aa53ad74ae6ad-f9e37c73|Release roundup—December 20th, 2025]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now use the [teleprompter](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/30293765617549-Record-in-a-Descript-Room#teleprompter-in-rooms) during your Rooms calls. This feature helps you deliver your content smoothly while maintaining eye contact with your camera.
>
> The teleprompter allows you to read your script directly within the Rooms interface, making it easier to stay on track during recordings or live sessions.
