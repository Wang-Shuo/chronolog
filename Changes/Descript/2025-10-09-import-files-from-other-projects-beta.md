# Import files from other projects (beta)

**Descript** · 2025-10-09 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupoctober-9th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-10-09-68e7d9ed49008132ccb9626b-3840242a|Release roundup—October 9th, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’ve added the ability to [import files from other projects](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/39957710381453-Import-files-from-other-projects-beta) directly from the Project panel in the Editor. This is a great way to transfer files that you use frequently without having to re-process them, skipping the need to use Media minutes (or transcription hours if you’re on a Legacy plan.)
>
> You can access you full library of files from within any project, allowing you to find files and quickly locate that perfect clip, intro, B-roll, or sound effect you've used before.
>
> No more downloading and re-uploading files or burning extra media or transcription minutes – simply find and reuse what you already have.
>
> This is our first step toward comprehensive drive media and search functionality, with more improvements coming in future updates. We'd love to hear your feedback on how this feature enhances your editing workflow.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupoctober-9th-2025
