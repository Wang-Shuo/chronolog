# View counts

**Descript** · 2021-08-24 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-21-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2021-08-24-6390c9bd4a4a0d64fa56454c-6699b120|Version 21 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Video creators will now be able to see a view count on video pages (share.descript) that they created. If viewers are logged in to Descript while viewing, video creators will be able to see their name and avatar. Otherwise these views will appear as anonymous users. Video creators will also receive an email notification when the first view occurs. Views are counted when the viewer hits the video play button.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-21-release
