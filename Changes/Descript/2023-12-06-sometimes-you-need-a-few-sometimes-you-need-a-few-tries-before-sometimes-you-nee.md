# New AI Action: Remove Retakes

**Descript** · 2023-12-06 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/new-ai-action-remove-retakes) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-12-06-656fb6ca4232ee6ca239e301-60b85572|New AI Action: Remove Retakes]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Sometimes you need a few… Sometimes you need a few tries before… Sometimes you need a few tries before you get the right take. Taking the time to meticulously review your recordings for the best takes can be a chore, and missing one can be a major embarrassment.
>
> Remove Retakes alleviates the drudgery of manual editing by automatically scanning your recordings to identify and remove repeated takes, streamlining the editing process.
>
> Just click the Actions bar, click Ask AI, and select [**Remove retakes…**](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/21908864772493-Edit-actions)
>
> The AI will scan for lines with multiple takes, and you can edit them out just by clicking *Apply Edits to Script.*
>
> With Remove Retakes, you can turn hours of work into just a few steps.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/new-ai-action-remove-retakes
