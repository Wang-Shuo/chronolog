**Descript** · 2026-03-17 · [[Themes/image-generation|Image Generation]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupmarch-17th-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-03-17-69b3257b60b225e10f6a3a28-246aeffc|Release roundup—March 17th, 2026]]

> When you generate images while creating a [**custom avatar**](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/34723305545357-Avatars-Overview), Descript will now automatically save those generated images to your **project files**.
