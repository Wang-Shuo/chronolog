# Use Kling models for higher resolution avatars that move more naturally**

**Descript** · 2026-02-10 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupfebruary-10th-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-02-10-698b53cfff8de99c84c132f7-0801d6da|Release roundup—February 10th, 2026]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now choose between [three models](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/39869916772621-Generative-image-and-video-models-in-Descript) when generating [Avatars](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/34723305545357-Avatars-Overview):
>
> **Hedra Character 2** (default): 480p resolution, most cost-effective option at about 20 credits per minute
>
> **Kling v2**: 720p resolution, about 67 credits per minute
>
> **Kling v2 Pro**: 1080p resolution, about 138 credits per minute
>
> The Kling models are better at everything, just to be honest. They are clearer. Less twitching or eye melting. More expensive, but just do yourself a favor and try it. To select a model, open the model picker in Settings or the Avatar confirmation modal.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupfebruary-10th-2026
