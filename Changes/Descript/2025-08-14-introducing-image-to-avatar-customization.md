# Introducing Image-to-Avatar Customization

**Descript** · 2025-08-14 · [[Themes/video-generation|Video Generation]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupaugust-14th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-08-14-689cc1a7b085b5834e3bf896-ac84359e|Release roundup—August 14th, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now create and [customize AI avatars](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/34723325494157) based on images you upload. Once you've created an avatar, you can generate variations just by entering a text prompt. Adjust your avatar's background, clothing or art style. These custom avatars can then be used just like our stock avatars.
>
> Just click Create a custom avatar in the speaker menu to get started.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupaugust-14th-2025
