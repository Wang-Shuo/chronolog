# New admin controls for transcription glossary and do not translate lists**

**Descript** · 2025-12-20 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupdecember-20th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-12-20-6945e963a88aa53ad74ae6ad-f9e37c73|Release roundup—December 20th, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Drive admins can [now control](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/38106088202765-Brand-Studio#who-can-manage-brand-studio) who can edit the transcription glossary and "do not translate" list in Brand Studio. When enabled, these settings prevent other users from modifying these glossaries, giving admins more control over consistent transcription and translation across the team.
>
> *Note: This feature is only available for Admins on Drives with Brand Kit enabled.*
