# New in Brand Studio—permissions**

**Descript** · 2025-10-28 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/newbrand-studio-updates-and-sora-2) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-10-28-69011cafb870a7b6c53a3ff5-5b47667d|New—Brand studio updates and Sora 2]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Drive admins on Enterprise plans can now restrict what assets are used by their team by setting [permissions](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/40650647520653-Brand-Studio-Permissions) in Brand Studio. This helps you enforce brand compliance across all projects without constant manual review.
>
> When you set permissions, team members will only see approved brand assets in their editor. Your brand colors, fonts, logos, and media appear first in color pickers, font menus, and media browsers—making it easy for your team to stay on-brand by default.
>
> To set up permissions, open Brand Studio from your Drive, go to the Permissions tab, and choose brand assets. This feature is available on Enterprise plans.
>
> For editors on Enterprise drives, you’ll now see [a tab for brand-approved assets](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/40665479385101-Import-Brand-Studio-media-into-your-project) in your Media panel.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/newbrand-studio-updates-and-sora-2
