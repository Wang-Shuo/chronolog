# Editor Updates

**Descript** · 2024-03-18 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-85) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2024-03-18-65f881224c39e486392e4783-451fba5f|Release Version 85]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now edit or add [Speaker Labels](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10164803814285-Speakers) to a highlighted section of your script. Just highlight a range of text in the Script and use the keyboard shortcut *Shift+2 (@)* or click the *ellipses […]* menu on the Selection toolbar.
>
> Speaking of the [Selection Toolbar](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10164874052237-Selection-toolbar), we’ve updated it to include a Dropdown menu under the Ignore (strikethrough) icon that includes options to Ignore media, Delete media, Replace with Gap Clip, and Remove from Transcript.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-85
