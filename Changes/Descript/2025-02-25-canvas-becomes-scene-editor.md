# Canvas becomes Scene Editor

**Descript** · 2025-02-25 · [[Themes/file-data|Files and Data]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-109) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-02-25-67bde093ca243fdab541e3f6-d25f4f75|Release Version 109]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’ve renamed the main visual editing space in Descript from “Canvas” to [Scene editor](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10165362727821-Scene-editor-overview). This relabeling is to help illustrate the relationship between:
>
> [Layouts](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10255950033805-Layout-packs-and-layout-overview) — the visual content in a section of your video
>
> [Scenes](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10119710379917-Working-with-scenes) — sections of your video that have unique layouts
>
> While the term Canvas may be somewhat common in video editing — we’re asking you once again to rewire your brain a tiny bit: Scenes are like different sets in a play, and the Scene editor is where you stage them up.
>
> Only the wording has changed, this will not effect any existing workflows but it’s an important change to be aware of for navigating the app.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-109
