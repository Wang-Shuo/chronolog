**Descript** · 2026-11-06 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Digest · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupnovember-6-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-11-06-6aee4d89b5b0a5f95c0b0cc2-f54c26f4|Release roundup—November 6, 2026]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> USB foot pedals now connect in the desktop app.

> Teleprompter: the close and fullscreen buttons work in the Windows app, you can switch scroll speed from Variable to Constant, and settings no longer freeze or reset when you reopen it.

> Rooms on mobile keeps the microphone you picked instead of switching back to the default.
