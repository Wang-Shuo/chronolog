# New View Options in Drive View

**Descript** · 2023-10-02 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-74) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-10-02-651af1e2879b48093bb6f63c-fca4417e|Release Version 74]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’ve added a [Grid-view option to your Drive](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10163815138061-Drive-view#:~:text=in%20this%20area.-,Grid%20and%20list%20view,-You%20can%20toggle). You can now toggle Grid view to show large thumbnails for your Video projects. Thumbnails will appear automatically once a project is opened or downloaded. This is great for users who prefer a more visual navigation style.
>
> Thumbnails are generated from the first Scene card in your project, but you can also right-click any scene in your project and set that as the [Project thumbnail.](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/20024457395341-Project-thumbnails)
>
> Thumbnails also appear in your List view. Finally, we’ve added some new icons to help distinguish between project types.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-74
