# Jibber Jabber is no more

**Descript** · 2025-01-07 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Policy · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-105) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-01-07-677d554eb70df614fe452c9a-10738fe7|Release Version 105]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’ve made it easier to understand when you’re nearing your [text-to-speech limit](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10165984072845-AI-Speaker-usage-guidelines) by eliminating Jibber Jabber (replacing random words with nonsense when you’ve used your balance.) Now, we’ll simply stop generating new TTS, and you can increase your monthly limits by upgrading if you need more time. You’ll hit this prompt and click *Upgrade Now* or dismiss it.
>
> You’ll only hit this once, and then be met with a smaller prompt as a reminder that you are out of text-to-speech minutes.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-105
