# Drive View: filter to projects or recordings

**Descript** · 2021-10-19 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-25-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2021-10-19-6390c9d24a4a0d64fa5647bc-720144c3|Version 25 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> The "All" tab in [Drive View](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/4411335667341) is now called "Home", and lets you filter to view just projects or recordings. These filters also work with, and apply to the "Created by me", "Shared with me" and "Downloaded" tabs. Existing filters in the "Pages" tab have been moved to the top-right for consistency.
>
