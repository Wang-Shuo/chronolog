# Choose the best model for the job

**Descript** · 2026-01-06 · [[Themes/model-support|Model Support]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/announcingunderlord-v2) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-01-06-695d8c261791e8a1ac173e7a-d8a269af|Announcing—Underlord v2]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Reminder**: You can also customize the [AI-model that powers Underlord](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/36803785502221-Underlord-beta-Your-AI-co-editor-in-Descript#choose-your-model) depending on your use case. To do so, open the model picker and choose from the latest models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and others.
>
> Choose Claude Haiku 4.5 for an even more economical and faster experience for quick, straightforward edits and text responses, such as *“Add studio sound and fix my eye contact",* “*Create a Youtube description for my video,”* or *“Suggest some ideas for how I might improve this video.”*
>
> Choose Claude Opus 4.5 for the highest-quality (and most expensive) version of Underlord. It’s best for complex, multi-faceted queries where advanced planning and nuanced edits are important.
>
> Gemini 3.0 Pro, Claude Sonnet 4.5, and GPT 5.2 each have their strengths and personalities; try them out and [let us know](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10612803483405-Connect-with-the-Descript-community) which you prefer and why.
>
> If you’ve got an established, fine-tuned workflow with the old version of Underlord, and want to go back, you can temporarily opt out by opening [Settings → Labs](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/23515226920973-Preview-upcoming-features-with-Descript-Labs), and toggling *New Underlord v2* to OFF. This toggle will be available for the next few weeks.
>
> If you toggle it off, you’ll be presented with a quick survey about your experience. Please answer it to help us continue improving!
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/announcingunderlord-v2
