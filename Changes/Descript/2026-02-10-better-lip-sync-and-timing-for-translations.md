# Better lip sync and timing for translations**

**Descript** · 2026-02-10 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupfebruary-10th-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-02-10-698b53cfff8de99c84c132f7-0801d6da|Release roundup—February 10th, 2026]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> When you [translate and dub your videos](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/37202655823629-Translate-and-dub-speech-overview#translate-style-options), we now default to matching timing between videos. This means fewer videos where the audio feels weirdly sped up or slowed down. If a direct 1:1 translation is more important, you can choose “Direct translation” instead.
