# Eye Contact (beta)**

**Descript** · 2023-07-10 · [[Themes/multimodal|Multimodal Input]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-68-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-07-10-64ac412e0a49ed588306f998-b4887a52|Version 68 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> [This new AI video effect](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/16672551194637-Enhance-video-with-Eye-Contact) keeps your gaze locked onto the camera no matter where you’re looking — at your script, screen, or slides. While in beta, this effect processes slowly and requires more time to complete for longer and higher-quality files.
>
> **This will be rolling out to everyone over the coming days**, and once you get access you will be able to find it under **Effects > Eye Contact** in your video layer's properties panel.
>
