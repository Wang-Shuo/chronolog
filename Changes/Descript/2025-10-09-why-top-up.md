**Descript** · 2025-10-09 · [[Themes/pricing-model|Pricing Model]] · Policy · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupoctober-9th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-10-09-68e7d9ed49008132ccb9626b-3840242a|Release roundup—October 9th, 2025]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> For Creator and Business subscribers on our new plan types, [top ups](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/40053604160909-Top-Ups-Buy-Additional-Media-Minutes-and-AI-Credits) allow you to purchase additional media hours or AI credits when you're approaching your monthly limits.
