# Edit an image by drawing on it

**Descript** · 2026-08-05 · [[Themes/file-data|Files and Data]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-round-upaugust-5th-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-08-05-6a7371c25495b04db5be4841-92dbe6b5|Release round-up—August 5th, 2026]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Pick an image on the canvas, hit **Edit with AI**, and draw over the part you want to change. Describe the swap (remove the coffee cup,” “replace the sky,” “take out the logo") and Descript repaints just that region and leaves the rest of the frame alone. No round trip to another editor.
