> The main navigation areas of Descript have a new look. [Workspaces](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/45412546810253-Workspaces) are cleaner and easier to move around in, the [Drive](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10248638387085-Set-up-and-manage-your-Drive) sidebar has been reorganized for faster scanning, and Settings has been restructured so related controls are grouped more logically.
