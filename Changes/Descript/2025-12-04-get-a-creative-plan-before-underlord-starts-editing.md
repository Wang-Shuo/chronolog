**Descript** · 2025-12-04 · [[Themes/reliability-performance|Reliability and Performance]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupdecember-4th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-12-04-6930e3d143084a3405d0352d-695ede77|Release roundup—December 4th, 2025]]

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> When you start a new video with Underlord, you can now a multi-step, multi-question [Project brief](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/38948410031245-Use-templates-to-speed-up-your-Underlord-workflow#h_01K97SDMD2AMBNE3TMXBJFAR5V) that outlines the plan before any editing begins. Underlord will propose a direction for your video—including stylistic choices—and wait for your approval before taking action. This helps you feel confident about the approach before credits are spent. You can iterate on the style and direction until you're ready to move forward.
