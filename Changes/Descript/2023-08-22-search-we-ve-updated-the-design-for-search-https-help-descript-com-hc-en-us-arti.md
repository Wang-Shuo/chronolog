# **Search**: We’ve updated the design…

**Descript** · 2023-08-22 · [[Themes/search-browsing|Search and Browsing]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-71) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-08-22-64e51d957de238097a7bdd9c-4db80053|Release Version 71]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Search**: We’ve updated the design for [search](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10164807821581-Search-tool) based on your feedback to make it easier to find & use the various features. By clicking on the magnifying glass icon, you can now find a number of search actions under *Find and…*: Remove filler words, Shorten word gaps, Remove, Correct and Copy. You can also remove individual search results from the search results list in the side panel to make it easier to apply bulk actions to all but a few instances. The search results list has been cleaned up, including the removal of timestamps.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-71
