# Subscription management and checkout update

**Descript** · 2024-11-05 · [[Themes/pricing-model|Pricing Model]] · Pricing · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-102) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2024-11-05-672a4fac68acbe93f698bb4b-eff3b53e|Release Version 102]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> A few weeks ago we announced a new and improved [web-based checkout](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/12742156057229-Upgrading-your-subscription) system. We initially rolled it out to 50% of our users. We have now made it available to all users. When purchasing or editing a subscription, you will be taken to a web browser with an improved interface making it clearer what will change and the associated costs.
>
> The Subscription panel has been split into two segments:
>
> [Preferences](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/23439763984013-Changing-the-default-save-location-for-new-projects): for adjusting Drive settings such as Drive icon and Drive name
>
> [Subscription](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10255890238861-Viewing-and-managing-your-subscription): for managing subscription plan, and adding or removing card details
>
