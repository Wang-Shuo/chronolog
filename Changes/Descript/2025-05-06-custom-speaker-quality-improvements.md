# Custom speaker quality improvements

**Descript** · 2025-05-06 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-114) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-05-06-68156e35817977d45976ec98-d702eeed|Release Version 114]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> [Back in February](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog?search=%2522enterprise%2522%2520and%2520%2522speaker%2522#:~:text=Exclusive%20to%20Business%20and%20Enterprise%20users%20%E2%80%94%20improved%20custom%20AI%20speakers), we rolled out major improvements to speaker quality to our Business and Enterprise users. We have improved the quality even more and are deploying both sets of improvements to all users in the coming weeks. You should notice a higher overall audio quality with better, more natural delivery. This also means that we will soon be fully deprecating custom speakers made with our very old models. These are labeled as "Removing soon" in the AI Speakers menu in Drive view. If you’re still using one, you’ll want to create a new custom speaker with the upgraded models by 6/30/2025. You can do this by clicking the "New speaker" button in the top right of the AI speakers menu in Drive view.
>
