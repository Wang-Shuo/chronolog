# **Drawer resizing**: The Media Library and Composition Drawers are now freely…

**Descript** · 2023-04-17 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-62-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2023-04-17-643dae58600c4309156766d8-73e550bd|Version 62 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> **Drawer resizing**: The Media Library and Composition Drawers are now freely [resizable and moveable](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/13286878359565-How-to-adjust-and-organize-panels-in-the-editor). Size will be remembered within a session, even if the drawer is closed. Note that at the smallest sizes, the Media Library intentionally behaves slightly differently than at default and large sizes: you can’t double-click to add a file, slow double-click to rename a file, or multi-select files, and stock selectors turn into a drop-down.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-62-release
