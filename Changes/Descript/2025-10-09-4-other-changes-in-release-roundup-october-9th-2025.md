# 4 other changes in Release roundup—October 9th, 2025

**Descript** · 2025-10-09 · Other changes · Digest · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupoctober-9th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-10-09-68e7d9ed49008132ccb9626b-3840242a|Release roundup—October 9th, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
## Changes in this digest

> **Cancel button**: Quickly discard your current recording if you want to start fresh without saving the current take.

> **Restart button**: Instantly stop your current recording and immediately begin a new take with the same settings.

> You can change your mind and resume from the confirmation panel with both cancel and restart options.

> This highly requested feature helps streamline your recording process and ensures you can quickly recover from mistakes without disrupting your creative flow.
