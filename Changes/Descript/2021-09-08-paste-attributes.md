# Paste Attributes

**Descript** · 2021-09-08 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/version-22-release) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2021-09-08-6390c9c64a4a0d64fa56466c-ad41d82d|Version 22 Release]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Press command + shift + V (or ctrl + shift + V on Windows) to paste attributes from the media on the clipboard to the selected clip(s). An example of where this is useful is if you want to copy zoom/positioning properties from one clip to several others. [See our help center article for further details](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408714654861).
>
