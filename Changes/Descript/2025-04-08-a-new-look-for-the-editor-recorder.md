# A new look for the Editor recorder

**Descript** · 2025-04-08 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-112) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-04-08-67f566b464be60e217c949ee-140ce254|Release Version 112]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We're introducing a redesigned [Editor recorder](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10165881574413-Recorder-overview) experience in version 112. The recorder now lives in the play dock instead of the sidebar, making it more accessible within your workflow. When recording with an empty script, the recorder takes up your full screen for better focus. If there's content in your script, you can see it while recording - the recorder will only occupy the Scene Editor area. The redesign aims to make recording more intuitive and accessible, integrating seamlessly with your workflow while maintaining visibility of important content during recording sessions.
>
> The recorder has moved from the sidebar to the Play dock.
>
> When working in an empty script, the recorder will be in full screen mode.
>
> Once you have content in the script, the recording preview will occupy the Scene-editor (formerly known as the Canvas.)
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-112
