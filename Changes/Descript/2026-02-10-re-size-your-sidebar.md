# Re-size your sidebar**

**Descript** · 2026-02-10 · [[Themes/interface-experience|Interface Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupfebruary-10th-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-02-10-698b53cfff8de99c84c132f7-0801d6da|Release roundup—February 10th, 2026]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now resize the [sidebar in the editor](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/13286878359565-Customize-the-editor#adjust-editor-panels). Just drag the edge of the sidebar to adjust its width. This makes it easier to read longer chat messages in Underlord and view other sidebar content more comfortably. The sidebar width is preserved while the app is open. When you resize your window, the sidebar maintains its width rather than scaling proportionally.
>
