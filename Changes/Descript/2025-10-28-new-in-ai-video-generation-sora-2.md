# New in AI-video generation—Sora 2**

**Descript** · 2025-10-28 · [[Themes/video-generation|Video Generation]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/newbrand-studio-updates-and-sora-2) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-10-28-69011cafb870a7b6c53a3ff5-5b47667d|New—Brand studio updates and Sora 2]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’ve also added Sora 2 to our [generative video options](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/39869916772621-Generative-image-and-video-models-in-Descript) in Descript. Sora 2 creates cohesive multi-shot videos from text prompts, with smooth motion, camera continuity, and cinematic realism.
>
> Sora 2 automatically generates audio including music, sound effects, and speech when you include quoted dialogue in your prompt.
>
> *Note: that you won't be able to generate results if your input image contains a human face when using Sora 2. This is a security feature created by OpenAI.*
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/newbrand-studio-updates-and-sora-2
