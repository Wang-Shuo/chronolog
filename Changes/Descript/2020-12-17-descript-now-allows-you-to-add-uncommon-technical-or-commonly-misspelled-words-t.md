# Transcription Glossary

**Descript** · 2020-12-17 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/transcription-glossary) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2020-12-17-5fdb7b3ef466355b08f6bc83-9169b896|Transcription Glossary]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> Descript now allows you to add uncommon, technical, or commonly misspelled words to the transcription glossary to improve the chances that it will be transcribed correctly.
>
> **Note:** this feature does not yet work with Live Transcription (this should be coming early next year) so you can turn this off by clicking on the gear icon in the Recording dialog and unchecking "Automatically transcribe recording".
>
> [Transcription Glossary](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/360053978052)
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/transcription-glossary
