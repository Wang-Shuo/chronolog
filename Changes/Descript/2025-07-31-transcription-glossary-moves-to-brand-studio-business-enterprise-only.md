**Descript** · 2025-07-31 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupjuly-31st-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-07-31-688bbf021a0bafe843472e0e-c36eb6f1|Release Roundup—July 31st, 2025]]
