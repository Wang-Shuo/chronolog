# Search for what’s in the shot

**Descript** · 2026-11-06 · [[Themes/enterprise-admin|Enterprise and Administration]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupnovember-6-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-11-06-6aee4d89b5b0a5f95c0b0cc2-f54c26f4|Release roundup—November 6, 2026]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You remember the shot. The whiteboard, the product on the desk, the sunset over the parking lot. You don’t remember the file name, because the file name is IMG_4471. On Enterprise drives, search now looks at what’s in your images and videos, not just what they’re called. Describe the shot, and it comes up.
>
> It works everywhere you search your drive: the search bar, Underlord, the Descript MCP, and the API. So you can ask Underlord, or Claude or ChatGPT through the MCP, to find the whiteboard shot for you.
>
