# Edit for clarity w/ AI speech

**Descript** · 2024-09-10 · [[Themes/editor-experience|Editor Experience]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-98) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2024-09-10-66e06c5b4399807e3d863d3a-f63d1366|Release Version 98 ]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now use your AI voices directly in conjunction with the [Edit for clarity](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/21908864772493-Sound-Good-Actions) tool. This is great for times where you’re unable to re-record anything based on the suggestions you receive using Edit for Clarity. Just toggle the option labeled *Use AI speech*, and check off the speakers you’d like to have eligible for edit-for-clarity based word or phrase replacement.
>
> Any parts of the script that will be replaced will be marked in blue.
>
> *Note: This currently does not work with* [Sequences](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10256430454925-Sequence-overview), *but they should be supported in an upcoming release.*
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-98
