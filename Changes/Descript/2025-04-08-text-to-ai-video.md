# Text-to-AI Video

**Descript** · 2025-04-08 · [[Themes/video-generation|Video Generation]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-112) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-04-08-67f566b464be60e217c949ee-140ce254|Release Version 112]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> A few weeks back we created a new AI Video maker in the ChatGPT store. This tool automatically generates a video explainer on a topic of your choosing, with customizable style and tone. Now, we’ve brought [this experience into the app](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/35650061759501-Text-to-AI-Video), available from the new Home screen, so you can create narrated, illustrated video with just a few clicks. It’s a great way to experiment with the tools Descript has to offer, or make a quick piece of content for easy sharing.
>
