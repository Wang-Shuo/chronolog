# New in Labs—Upload from Mobile

**Descript** · 2025-07-15 · [[Themes/mobile-desktop|Mobile and Desktop]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupjuly-15th-2025) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2025-07-15-6876a4379c30e0e186778b10-1f0cb94b|Release Roundup—July 15th, 2025]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’ve added a new [Labs](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/23515226920973-Preview-upcoming-features-with-Descript-Labs) setting that allows you to upload files into your project from your mobile device. This is especially useful for users who record their media using a smart phone rather than a digital camera. Once you toggle it on, just click the “Upload from Mobile” button in your project to receive a QR code. Scan the code and you can then add any media files from your device directly into your Project.
>
