# Preview audio and video results in Underlord**

**Descript** · 2026-03-02 · [[Themes/voice|Voice and Audio]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupmarch-2nd-2026) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2026-03-02-69a5fea964720fb1ccd2c350-123a08f0|Release roundup—March 2nd, 2026]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> You can now preview audio and video before [Underlord](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/36803785502221-Underlord-beta-Your-AI-co-editor-in-Descript) applies them to your project. When generating media like avatars or video clips, Underlord will show you preview options so you can confirm the results match what you're looking for before committing to the generation.
>
> This helps you avoid wasting time and credits on results that don't meet your needs, especially for time-consuming or resource-intensive generations.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-roundupmarch-2nd-2026
