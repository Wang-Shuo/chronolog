# Subscription management and checkout improvements

**Descript** · 2024-10-08 · [[Themes/pricing-model|Pricing Model]] · Launch · [Official source](https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-100) · from [[Releases/Descript/descript-2024-10-08-670564dc958faa93e6166a33-dd601db4|Release Version 100]]
<!-- chronolog:end -->

<!-- chronolog:begin region="evidence" -->
> We’re launching some improvements to the [self-checkout flow](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10255890238861-Viewing-and-managing-your-subscription), which will be available initially to 50% of users as we make sure it offers a better overall experience. Users will be taken to a web browser with an improved interface making it clearer what will change and the associated costs.
>
> Users in this group will also now see that the Subscription panel has been split into two segments:
>
> Preferences: for adjusting Drive settings such as Drive icon and Drive name.
>
> Subscription: for managing subscription plan, and adding or removing card details.
>
> We’ll slowly ramp this up to all users over the coming weeks.
>
> Source: https://feedback.descript.com/changelog/release-version-100
